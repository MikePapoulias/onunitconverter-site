About Petabecquerels

The petabecquerel (PBq) equals 10¹⁵ Bq. The Chernobyl accident (1986) released ~5200 PBq of noble gases and ~1760 PBq of ¹³¹I; Fukushima Daiichi (2011) released 400-900 PBq ¹³¹I-equivalent; global ⁸⁵Kr inventory from nuclear operations is ~1300 PBq. 1 PBq = 10¹⁵ Bq = 10³ TBq.

About Femtobecquerels

The femtobecquerel (fBq) equals 10⁻¹⁵ Bq, used in accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) to quantify cosmogenic radionuclides (¹⁰Be, ³⁶Cl) in ice cores and geological samples. Ultra-low-background counting in deep underground laboratories reaches the fBq/kg level for solar neutrino detector purity assessment. 1 fBq = 10⁻¹⁵ Bq = 10⁻³ pBq.
