About Megacuries

The megacurie (MCi) equals 3.7 × 10¹⁶ Bq. Total radioactive inventory of a large nuclear power reactor core at shutdown is ~2000-10000 MCi; US atmospheric nuclear weapons tests released ~12000 MCi of ¹³¹I globally; high-level nuclear waste repositories are characterised in MCi. 1 MCi = 3.7 × 10¹⁶ Bq.

About Millibecquerels

The millibecquerel (mBq) equals 10⁻³ Bq. Radiological surface contamination surveys in nuclear facilities use mBq/cm² alert levels for alpha and beta emitters; uranium and plutonium bioassay in urine from occupationally exposed workers is reported in mBq/sample. 1 mBq = 10⁻³ Bq = 10⁻³ s⁻¹.
