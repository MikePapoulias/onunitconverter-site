About Gigacuries

The gigacurie (GCi) equals 3.7 × 10¹⁹ Bq. The total fission product inventory of a 1 GWe nuclear power reactor at equilibrium is ~0.5 GCi; global spent fuel inventories worldwide exceed 10 GCi; total reactor-produced tritium in the world's oceans is estimated at ~1 GCi. 1 GCi = 3.7 × 10¹⁹ Bq.

About Microcuries

The microcurie (µCi) equals 3.7 × 10⁴ Bq = 37 kBq. Sealed sources for educational and bench-top laboratory use are licensed up to 1-10 µCi under US NRC 10 CFR 30.18; small-animal PET imaging doses of ¹⁸F in preclinical research are expressed in µCi. 1 µCi = 3.7 × 10⁴ Bq.
