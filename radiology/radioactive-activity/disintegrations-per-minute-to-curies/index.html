About Disintegrations per minute

The disintegration per minute (dpm) equals exactly 1/60 Bq ≈ 16.7 mBq. Standard output unit of liquid scintillation counters (LSC) in biochemical and biomedical research for ³H and ¹⁴C tracer assays; radioimmunoassay protocols report results in dpm/well. 1 dpm = 1/60 Bq = 60 dph.

About Curies

The curie (Ci) equals exactly 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq = 37 GBq, originally defined as the activity of 1 g of ²²⁶Ra. Still widely used in the US: ⁶⁰Co teletherapy sources are 5000-15000 Ci; ¹³⁷Cs blood irradiators are 1000-40000 Ci; NRC source licences for industrial radiography cite Ci limits. 1 Ci = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq.
