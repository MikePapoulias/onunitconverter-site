About Petabecquerels

The petabecquerel (PBq) equals 10¹⁵ Bq. The Chernobyl accident (1986) released ~5200 PBq of noble gases and ~1760 PBq of ¹³¹I; Fukushima Daiichi (2011) released 400-900 PBq ¹³¹I-equivalent; global ⁸⁵Kr inventory from nuclear operations is ~1300 PBq. 1 PBq = 10¹⁵ Bq = 10³ TBq.

About Millicuries

The millicurie (mCi) equals 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq = 37 MBq. The primary clinical nuclear medicine unit in the US: ¹⁸F-FDG PET doses are 8-15 mCi; ⁹⁹mTc cardiac stress tests use 25-35 mCi; ¹³¹I hyperthyroidism therapy uses 5-30 mCi. US pharmacopeia and NRC regulations specify doses in mCi. 1 mCi = 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq.
