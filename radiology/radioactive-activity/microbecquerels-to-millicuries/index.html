About Microbecquerels

The microbecquerel (µBq) equals 10⁻⁶ Bq. Nuclear power plant effluent monitoring reports ¹³⁷Cs and ⁹⁰Sr in air and water at µBq/m³; dietary ²¹⁰Po intake from seafood consumption is assessed at µBq per daily serving in food safety surveys. 1 µBq = 10⁻⁶ Bq = 10⁻³ mBq.

About Millicuries

The millicurie (mCi) equals 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq = 37 MBq. The primary clinical nuclear medicine unit in the US: ¹⁸F-FDG PET doses are 8-15 mCi; ⁹⁹mTc cardiac stress tests use 25-35 mCi; ¹³¹I hyperthyroidism therapy uses 5-30 mCi. US pharmacopeia and NRC regulations specify doses in mCi. 1 mCi = 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq.
