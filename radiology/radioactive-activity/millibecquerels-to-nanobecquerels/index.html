Conversion Formula

About Millibecquerels

The millibecquerel (mBq) equals 10⁻³ Bq. Radiological surface contamination surveys in nuclear facilities use mBq/cm² alert levels for alpha and beta emitters; uranium and plutonium bioassay in urine from occupationally exposed workers is reported in mBq/sample. 1 mBq = 10⁻³ Bq = 10⁻³ s⁻¹.

About Nanobecquerels

The nanobecquerel (nBq) equals 10⁻⁹ Bq. Atmospheric surveillance for tritium and ¹⁴C near nuclear facilities reports discharge rates in nBq/m³; EU drinking water directives set indicator activity concentrations for some radionuclides at the nBq/L level. 1 nBq = 10⁻⁹ Bq = 10⁻³ µBq.
