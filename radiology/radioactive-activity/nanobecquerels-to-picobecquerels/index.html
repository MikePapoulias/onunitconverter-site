About Nanobecquerels

The nanobecquerel (nBq) equals 10⁻⁹ Bq. Atmospheric surveillance for tritium and ¹⁴C near nuclear facilities reports discharge rates in nBq/m³; EU drinking water directives set indicator activity concentrations for some radionuclides at the nBq/L level. 1 nBq = 10⁻⁹ Bq = 10⁻³ µBq.

About Picobecquerels

The picobecquerel (pBq) equals 10⁻¹² Bq. Environmental monitoring of drinking water reports naturally occurring ²²⁶Ra and ²²⁸Ra at pBq/L; ultra-low background Geiger counters in underground laboratories detect pBq-level contamination on detector surfaces. 1 pBq = 10⁻¹² Bq = 10⁻³ nBq.
