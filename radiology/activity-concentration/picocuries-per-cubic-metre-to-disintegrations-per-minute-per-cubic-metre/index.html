About Picocuries per cubic metre

The picocurie per cubic metre (pCi/m³) equals 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³ = 37 mBq/m³. Used by the US EPA and NRC for outdoor air radioactivity; airborne ⁹⁰Sr and ¹³⁷Cs near nuclear facilities are reported in pCi/m³; NRC occupational derived air concentration (DAC) tables list values in pCi/m³. 1 pCi/m³ = 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³.

About Disintegrations per minute per cubic metre

The disintegration per minute per cubic metre (dpm/m³) equals exactly 1/60 Bq/m³ ≈ 16.7 mBq/m³. Used in legacy US nuclear industry and older radiochemistry literature for low-level airborne radioactivity; some historical derived air concentration (DAC) limit publications cite values in dpm/m³. 1 dpm/m³ = 1/60 Bq/m³ ≈ 16.7 mBq/m³.
