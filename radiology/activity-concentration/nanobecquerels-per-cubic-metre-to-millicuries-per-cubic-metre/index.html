About Nanobecquerels per cubic metre

The nanobecquerel per cubic metre (nBq/m³) equals 10⁻⁹ Bq/m³. Background ¹³⁷Cs in Northern Hemisphere air averages 10-100 nBq/m³ from post-Chernobyl and Fukushima residual deposition; ⁹⁰Sr near nuclear power plants is routinely monitored at nBq/m³ levels in air samples. 1 nBq/m³ = 10⁻⁹ Bq/m³ = 10⁻³ µBq/m³.

About Millicuries per cubic metre

The millicurie per cubic metre (mCi/m³) equals 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq/m³. Encountered in nuclear facility primary coolant air monitors during maintenance and in hot cell exhaust ventilation; NRC DAC values for highly radiotoxic alpha emitters (²³⁹Pu, ²⁴¹Am) use µCi/mL = mCi/L units that translate to mCi/m³ at room scale. 1 mCi/m³ = 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq/m³.
