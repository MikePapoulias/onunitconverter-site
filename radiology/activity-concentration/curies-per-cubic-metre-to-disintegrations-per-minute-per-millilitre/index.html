About Curies per cubic metre

The curie per cubic metre (Ci/m³) equals 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/m³ = 37 GBq/L. Encountered in concentrated radioactive liquid waste from nuclear reprocessing; reactor spent fuel pool water activity after fuel cladding failure; high-level waste (HLW) concentrates in storage tanks at Hanford, Sellafield, and similar sites. 1 Ci/m³ = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/m³.

About Disintegrations per minute per millilitre

The disintegration per minute per millilitre (dpm/mL) equals exactly 10⁶/60 Bq/m³ ≈ 16 667 Bq/m³. Standard output unit of liquid scintillation counters (LSC) in biochemistry for ³H and ¹⁴C radiotracer assays; radioactivity in cell culture media, pharmacokinetic samples, and environmental water is routinely expressed in dpm/mL. 1 dpm/mL = 10⁶/60 Bq/m³.
