About Kilobecquerels per cubic metre

The kilobecquerel per cubic metre (kBq/m³) equals 10³ Bq/m³ = 1 Bq/L. High indoor radon in poorly ventilated uranium mines and homes above uranium ore bodies routinely exceeds 1-10 kBq/m³; EU mine remediation targets aim to reduce air radon below 1 kBq/m³. 1 kBq/m³ = 10³ Bq/m³ = 1 Bq/L.
