About Picocuries per cubic metre

The picocurie per cubic metre (pCi/m³) equals 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³ = 37 mBq/m³. Used by the US EPA and NRC for outdoor air radioactivity; airborne ⁹⁰Sr and ¹³⁷Cs near nuclear facilities are reported in pCi/m³; NRC occupational derived air concentration (DAC) tables list values in pCi/m³. 1 pCi/m³ = 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³.

About Kilobecquerels per cubic metre

The kilobecquerel per cubic metre (kBq/m³) equals 10³ Bq/m³ = 1 Bq/L. High indoor radon in poorly ventilated uranium mines and homes above uranium ore bodies routinely exceeds 1-10 kBq/m³; EU mine remediation targets aim to reduce air radon below 1 kBq/m³. 1 kBq/m³ = 10³ Bq/m³ = 1 Bq/L.
