Conversion Formula

GBq/m³ = nCi/m³ × 3.700000000e-8

About Nanocuries per cubic metre

The nanocurie per cubic metre (nCi/m³) equals 37 Bq/m³, identical to 1 picocurie per litre (pCi/L). The US EPA indoor radon action level is 4 pCi/L = 148 Bq/m³ ≈ 4 nCi/m³; NRC 10 CFR Part 20 DAC values for several radionuclides are expressed in the nCi/m³ range. 1 nCi/m³ = 37 Bq/m³ = 1 pCi/L.

About Gigabecquerels per cubic metre

The gigabecquerel per cubic metre (GBq/m³) equals 10⁹ Bq/m³ = 1 MBq/L. Activity concentrations in reactor primary coolant during power operation (¹⁶N, ¹⁹O short-lived activation products) and in BWR condensate demineraliser effluents reach GBq/m³; concentrated liquid radioactive waste from fuel reprocessing streams is characterised in GBq/m³. 1 GBq/m³ = 10⁹ Bq/m³.
