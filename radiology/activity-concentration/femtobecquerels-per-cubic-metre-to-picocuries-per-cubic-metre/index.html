About Femtobecquerels per cubic metre

The femtobecquerel per cubic metre (fBq/m³) equals 10⁻¹⁵ Bq/m³. Used in ultra-sensitive environmental monitoring - noble gas activities of cosmogenic nuclides (³⁶Cl, ¹⁰Be) in deep Antarctic ice cores; air purity screening for underground rare-event physics experiments (dark matter searches, neutrinoless double-beta decay) reaches fBq/m³ sensitivities. 1 fBq/m³ = 10⁻¹⁵ Bq/m³.

About Picocuries per cubic metre

The picocurie per cubic metre (pCi/m³) equals 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³ = 37 mBq/m³. Used by the US EPA and NRC for outdoor air radioactivity; airborne ⁹⁰Sr and ¹³⁷Cs near nuclear facilities are reported in pCi/m³; NRC occupational derived air concentration (DAC) tables list values in pCi/m³. 1 pCi/m³ = 3.7 × 10⁻² Bq/m³.
