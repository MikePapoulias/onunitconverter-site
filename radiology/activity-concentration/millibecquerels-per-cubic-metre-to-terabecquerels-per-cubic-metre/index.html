About Millibecquerels per cubic metre

The millibecquerel per cubic metre (mBq/m³) equals 10⁻³ Bq/m³. European atmospheric ³H, ⁸⁵Kr, and ¹⁴C concentrations near nuclear facilities are reported in mBq/m³; post-Fukushima ¹³⁴Cs and ¹³⁷Cs transport across the Pacific was measured at mBq/m³ at remote monitoring stations. 1 mBq/m³ = 10⁻³ Bq/m³ = 1 µBq/L.

About Terabecquerels per cubic metre

The terabecquerel per cubic metre (TBq/m³) equals 10¹² Bq/m³ = 1 GBq/L. Encountered only in the most concentrated radioactive liquids - freshly dissolved spent nuclear fuel in reprocessing dissolver vessels, primary coolant in design-basis loss-of-coolant accident (LOCA) scenarios, and high-level waste (HLW) evaporator concentrates. 1 TBq/m³ = 10¹² Bq/m³.
