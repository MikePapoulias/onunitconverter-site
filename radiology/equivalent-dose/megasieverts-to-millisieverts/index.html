About Megasieverts

The megasievert (MSv) equals 10⁶ Sv. Entirely theoretical for biological contexts - severe radiation damage occurs well below 1 MSv. Appears in materials science modelling of extreme radiation environments, theoretical nuclear detonation effects at point of burst, and advanced reactor pressure vessel damage accumulation calculations. 1 MSv = 10⁶ Sv = 10⁸ rem.

About Millisieverts

The millisievert (mSv) equals 10⁻³ Sv and is the primary international radiation protection unit. ICRP 103 occupational limit 20 mSv/year averaged over 5 years; worldwide average background ~2.4 mSv/year; abdominal CT ~10 mSv; EU medical exposure directive and IAEA dose limits are quoted in mSv. 1 mSv = 10⁻³ Sv = 100 mrem.
