About Nanosieverts

The nanosievert (nSv) equals 10⁻⁹ Sv. Ambient environmental gamma dose rates at sea level are ~30-50 nSv/h; portable radiation survey meters and environmental monitoring networks report ambient dose equivalent rates in nSv/h; airborne gamma spectrometry surveys record deposition rates in nSv/h. 1 nSv = 10⁻⁹ Sv = 10⁻³ µSv.

About Kilosieverts

The kilosievert (kSv) equals 10³ Sv. Doses above ~10 Sv are rapidly lethal; the kSv scale is encountered only in extreme irradiator calibration measurements, high-energy accelerator beam-loss scenarios, and nuclear detonation effects calculations at the closest survival radii. 1 kSv = 10³ Sv = 10⁵ rem.
