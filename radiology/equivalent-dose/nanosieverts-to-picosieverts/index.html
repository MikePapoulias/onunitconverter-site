About Nanosieverts

The nanosievert (nSv) equals 10⁻⁹ Sv. Ambient environmental gamma dose rates at sea level are ~30-50 nSv/h; portable radiation survey meters and environmental monitoring networks report ambient dose equivalent rates in nSv/h; airborne gamma spectrometry surveys record deposition rates in nSv/h. 1 nSv = 10⁻⁹ Sv = 10⁻³ µSv.

About Picosieverts

The picosievert (pSv) equals 10⁻¹² Sv. Microdosimetry models of single α-particle traversals through cell nuclei report mean specific energies in the pSv range; ultra-low-background radiation detectors in deep underground laboratories for dark matter searches are characterised at pSv sensitivity levels. 1 pSv = 10⁻¹² Sv = 10⁻³ nSv.
