About Megarems

The megarem (Mrem) equals 10⁶ rem = 10⁴ Sv. Found in legacy radiation hardness assurance (RHA) documentation for space and military electronics: some total ionising dose (TID) test reports specify doses in Mrem(Si); at Mrem-level equivalent doses all organic structures suffer irreparable radiation damage. 1 Mrem = 10⁴ Sv = 10 kSv.

About Kilorems

The kilorem (krem) equals 10³ rem = 10 Sv. Used in radiation hardness testing of electronics for space and military: total ionising dose (TID) specifications cite krem(Si) levels; prompt nuclear radiation effects on personnel in nuclear weapons effects literature are quoted in krem. 1 krem = 10 Sv = 10³ rem.
