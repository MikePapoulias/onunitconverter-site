About Microsieverts

The microsievert (µSv) equals 10⁻⁶ Sv. Standard unit for individual low-dose exposures: chest X-ray ~20 µSv effective dose; transatlantic flight ~80 µSv; dental panoramic X-ray ~10-25 µSv. Personal electronic dosimeters in medical and industrial workplaces accumulate and display dose in µSv. 1 µSv = 10⁻⁶ Sv.

About Megarems

The megarem (Mrem) equals 10⁶ rem = 10⁴ Sv. Found in legacy radiation hardness assurance (RHA) documentation for space and military electronics: some total ionising dose (TID) test reports specify doses in Mrem(Si); at Mrem-level equivalent doses all organic structures suffer irreparable radiation damage. 1 Mrem = 10⁴ Sv = 10 kSv.
