About Megarems

The megarem (Mrem) equals 10⁶ rem = 10⁴ Sv. Found in legacy radiation hardness assurance (RHA) documentation for space and military electronics: some total ionising dose (TID) test reports specify doses in Mrem(Si); at Mrem-level equivalent doses all organic structures suffer irreparable radiation damage. 1 Mrem = 10⁴ Sv = 10 kSv.

About Kilosieverts

The kilosievert (kSv) equals 10³ Sv. Doses above ~10 Sv are rapidly lethal; the kSv scale is encountered only in extreme irradiator calibration measurements, high-energy accelerator beam-loss scenarios, and nuclear detonation effects calculations at the closest survival radii. 1 kSv = 10³ Sv = 10⁵ rem.
