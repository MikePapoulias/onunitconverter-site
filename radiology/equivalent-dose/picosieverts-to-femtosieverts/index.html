About Picosieverts

The picosievert (pSv) equals 10⁻¹² Sv. Microdosimetry models of single α-particle traversals through cell nuclei report mean specific energies in the pSv range; ultra-low-background radiation detectors in deep underground laboratories for dark matter searches are characterised at pSv sensitivity levels. 1 pSv = 10⁻¹² Sv = 10⁻³ nSv.

About Femtosieverts

The femtosievert (fSv) equals 10⁻¹⁵ Sv. Used in computational nanodosimetry and Monte Carlo particle-transport simulations to characterise the equivalent dose from a single ion traversal through a DNA segment or cell organelle. Ultra-sensitive detector calibration in deep underground laboratories approaches the fSv level. 1 fSv = 10⁻¹⁵ Sv = 10⁻³ pSv.
