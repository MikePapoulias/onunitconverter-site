About Millirads per hour

The millirad per hour (mrad/h) equals 1e-5 Gy/h = 10 µGy/h. Legacy US radiation protection unit used by area monitors and Geiger counters calibrated before SI adoption; EPA environmental surveillance stations report in mrad/h alongside µGy/h; NRC licence boundary dose-rate surveys often cite mrad/h. 1 mrad/h = 1e-5 Gy/h = 10 µGy/h.

About Grays per hour

The gray per hour (Gy/h) equals 1/3600 Gy/s ≈ 2.78×10⁻⁴ Gy/s. Used in radiation processing and source strength reporting: ¹³⁷Cs irradiator calibrations are expressed in Gy/h at a reference distance; IAEA food irradiation standards cite Gy/h for conveyor output; radiobiological experiments for cancer cell survival curves use Gy/h. 1 Gy/h = 100 rad/h = 1000 mGy/h.
