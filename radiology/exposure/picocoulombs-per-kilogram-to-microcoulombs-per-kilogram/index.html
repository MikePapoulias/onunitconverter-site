Conversion Formula

About Picocoulombs per kilogram

The picocoulomb per kilogram (pC/kg) equals 10⁻¹² C/kg. Environmental gamma exposure rates in very low background areas approach pC/kg/h; precision ionisation chamber calibration laboratories measure leakage and secondary ionisation currents in terms of pC/kg. 1 pC/kg = 10⁻¹² C/kg = 10⁻³ nC/kg.

About Microcoulombs per kilogram

The microcoulomb per kilogram (µC/kg) equals 10⁻⁶ C/kg ≈ 3.88 mR. Used in precision ionisation chamber measurements for X-ray beam quality characterisation; national standards laboratories express free-air chamber calibration factors in µC/kg. 1 µC/kg = 10⁻⁶ C/kg = 10⁻³ mC/kg.
