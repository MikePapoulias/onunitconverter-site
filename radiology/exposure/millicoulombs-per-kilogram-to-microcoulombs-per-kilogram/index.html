Conversion Formula

µC/kg = mC/kg × 1000

About Millicoulombs per kilogram

The millicoulomb per kilogram (mC/kg) equals 10⁻³ C/kg ≈ 3.88 R. Used in high-dose-rate X-ray beam calibration protocols (IAEA TRS-398, AAPM TG-51) where free-air chambers accumulate mC/kg of charge per irradiation; high-energy medical linac beams deliver calibration exposures measured in mC/kg. 1 mC/kg = 10⁻³ C/kg ≈ 3.88 R.

About Microcoulombs per kilogram

The microcoulomb per kilogram (µC/kg) equals 10⁻⁶ C/kg ≈ 3.88 mR. Used in precision ionisation chamber measurements for X-ray beam quality characterisation; national standards laboratories express free-air chamber calibration factors in µC/kg. 1 µC/kg = 10⁻⁶ C/kg = 10⁻³ mC/kg.
