About Milliroentgens

The milliroentgen (mR) equals 2.58 × 10⁻⁷ C/kg. Standard unit in US health physics for area gamma surveys: personal dosimeters and Geiger counters in medical and industrial radiography settings display mR/h; NRC licence conditions often specify dose-rate limits in mR/h at facility boundaries. 1 mR = 2.58 × 10⁻⁷ C/kg.

About Microcoulombs per kilogram

The microcoulomb per kilogram (µC/kg) equals 10⁻⁶ C/kg ≈ 3.88 mR. Used in precision ionisation chamber measurements for X-ray beam quality characterisation; national standards laboratories express free-air chamber calibration factors in µC/kg. 1 µC/kg = 10⁻⁶ C/kg = 10⁻³ mC/kg.
