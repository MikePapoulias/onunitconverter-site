Conversion Formula

About Microroentgens

The microroentgen (µR) equals 2.58 × 10⁻¹⁰ C/kg. Standard unit for very low environmental gamma dose rates in legacy US instrumentation: airborne radiological surveys over background areas report µR/h; EPA environmental monitoring network stations historically logged µR/h for outdoor gamma levels. 1 µR = 2.58 × 10⁻¹⁰ C/kg.

About Picocoulombs per kilogram

The picocoulomb per kilogram (pC/kg) equals 10⁻¹² C/kg. Environmental gamma exposure rates in very low background areas approach pC/kg/h; precision ionisation chamber calibration laboratories measure leakage and secondary ionisation currents in terms of pC/kg. 1 pC/kg = 10⁻¹² C/kg = 10⁻³ nC/kg.
