About Kilocoulombs per kilogram

The kilocoulomb per kilogram (kC/kg) equals 10³ C/kg ≈ 3.88 × 10⁶ R. Encountered only in extreme prompt-radiation environments such as nuclear weapon burst regions and high-power flash X-ray facility calibration; at this exposure level all air volumes are multiply ionised and conventional dosimetry models break down. 1 kC/kg = 10³ C/kg.

About Kiloroentgens

The kiloroentgen (kR) equals 0.258 C/kg. Used in radiation hardness testing of electronics and legacy nuclear weapons effects literature; total ionising exposure inside nuclear facilities during design-basis accident scenarios is cited in kR; some older radiation therapy machine output specifications use kR/min. 1 kR = 0.258 C/kg = 10³ R.
