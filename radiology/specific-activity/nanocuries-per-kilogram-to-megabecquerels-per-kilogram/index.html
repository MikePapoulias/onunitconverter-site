Convert Nanocuries per kilogram to Megabecquerels per kilogram

Convert Nanocuries per kilogram (nCi/kg) to Megabecquerels per kilogram (MBq/kg) instantly and accurately.

Nanocuries per kilogram (nCi/kg)
Megabecquerels per kilogram (MBq/kg)

Conversion Formula

MBq/kg = nCi/kg × 3.700000000e-5

About Nanocuries per kilogram

The nanocurie per kilogram (nCi/kg) equals 37 Bq/kg. US EPA soil screening levels for ²²⁶Ra and ²²⁸Ra are given in pCi/g which equals nCi/kg; NRC derived concentration guides (DCGs) for radionuclides in soil for unrestricted-use release are expressed in nCi/kg in 10 CFR Part 20. 1 nCi/kg = 37 Bq/kg = 3.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ci/kg.

About Megabecquerels per kilogram

The megabecquerel per kilogram (MBq/kg) equals 10⁶ Bq/kg. Typical for enriched or irradiated materials: moderately irradiated nuclear fuel after cooling has fission-product specific activities in the MBq/kg to GBq/kg range for individual nuclides; ₉ₐSr in high-dose-rate brachytherapy sources is characterised in MBq/kg. 1 MBq/kg = 10⁶ Bq/kg.
