About Kilocuries per kilogram

The kilocurie per kilogram (kCi/kg) equals 3.7 × 10¹³ Bq/kg = 1 Ci/g. The specific activity of carrier-free ⁶⁰Co is ~1124 Ci/g (= 1.124 kCi/kg or 4.16 × 10¹³ Bq/kg); many radionuclides produced without stable carrier have specific activities in the kCi/kg range. 1 kCi/kg = 3.7 × 10¹³ Bq/kg = 1 Ci/g.

About Millicuries per kilogram

The millicurie per kilogram (mCi/kg) equals 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq/kg = 37 MBq/kg. Radiopharmaceutical preparations are sometimes characterised in mCi/kg when assessing organ uptake per body mass; ⁹⁹mTc-labelled compounds have specific activities in the mCi/µmol range that translate to high mCi/kg values. 1 mCi/kg = 3.7 × 10⁷ Bq/kg.
