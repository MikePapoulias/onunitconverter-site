About Curies per kilogram

The curie per kilogram (Ci/kg) equals 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/kg = 37 GBq/kg. High specific activity sealed sources: ¹¹¹In and ¹³³Xe produced in accelerators are characterised in Ci/kg; ⁴²K formed by neutron activation in high-potassium food samples in research irradiators reaches Ci/kg values. 1 Ci/kg = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/kg.

About Gigabecquerels per kilogram

The gigabecquerel per kilogram (GBq/kg) equals 10⁹ Bq/kg. Characterises specific activity of cyclotron-produced short-lived nuclides: ¹⁸F batches from a medical cyclotron have specific activities of 10⁴-10⁵ GBq/µmol; ⁹⁹mTc eluates from a Mo/Tc generator are in the GBq/kg concentration range for clinical use. 1 GBq/kg = 10⁹ Bq/kg.
