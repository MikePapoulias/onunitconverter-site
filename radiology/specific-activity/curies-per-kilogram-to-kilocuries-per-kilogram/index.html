About Curies per kilogram

The curie per kilogram (Ci/kg) equals 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/kg = 37 GBq/kg. High specific activity sealed sources: ¹¹¹In and ¹³³Xe produced in accelerators are characterised in Ci/kg; ⁴²K formed by neutron activation in high-potassium food samples in research irradiators reaches Ci/kg values. 1 Ci/kg = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq/kg.

About Kilocuries per kilogram

The kilocurie per kilogram (kCi/kg) equals 3.7 × 10¹³ Bq/kg = 1 Ci/g. The specific activity of carrier-free ⁶⁰Co is ~1124 Ci/g (= 1.124 kCi/kg or 4.16 × 10¹³ Bq/kg); many radionuclides produced without stable carrier have specific activities in the kCi/kg range. 1 kCi/kg = 3.7 × 10¹³ Bq/kg = 1 Ci/g.
