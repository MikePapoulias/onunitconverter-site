About Terabecquerels per kilogram

The terabecquerel per kilogram (TBq/kg) equals 10¹² Bq/kg. Applies to short-lived nuclides of high specific activity: freshly separated ¹³¹I from reactor fission exceeds 1 TBq/kg; ⁶⁰Co sealed brachytherapy sources and ¹⁹²Ir radiography sources exhibit specific activities of several TBq/g, i.e. thousands of TBq/kg. 1 TBq/kg = 10¹² Bq/kg.

About Millibecquerels per kilogram

The millibecquerel per kilogram (mBq/kg) equals 10⁻³ Bq/kg. Used for very low-level radioactivity in food and environmental samples; trace ¹³⁷Cs in ocean water and ²²⁶Ra in mineral waters are reported in mBq/kg; occupational bioassay detection limits for actinides in urine and feces are expressed in mBq/kg. 1 mBq/kg = 10⁻³ Bq/kg.
