Conversion Formula

About Femtobecquerels per kilogram

The femtobecquerel per kilogram (fBq/kg) equals 10⁻¹⁵ Bq/kg. Used in accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) to quantify cosmogenic radionuclide concentrations (¹⁰Be, ³⁶Cl) in ice cores, sediments, and geological minerals where activities per unit mass are at the femto-level. Ultra-clean radiochemistry purity assessments for solar neutrino detectors also reach fBq/kg sensitivities. 1 fBq/kg = 10⁻¹⁵ Bq/kg.

About Picobecquerels per kilogram

The picobecquerel per kilogram (pBq/kg) equals 10⁻¹² Bq/kg. Environmental monitoring of very pure water samples and ultra-low-background detector materials reports natural radioactivity (²²⁶Ra, ²²⁸Ra) at pBq/kg; material purity screening for low-background physics experiments (neutrinoless double-beta decay searches) uses pBq/kg sensitivity thresholds. 1 pBq/kg = 10⁻¹² Bq/kg.
