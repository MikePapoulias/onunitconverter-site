About Gigaamperes per weber

The gigaampere per weber (GA/Wb) equals 10⁹ H⁻¹. The scale for small air gaps and miniaturized cores: a 0.1 mm air gap with 1 mm² cross-section has ℛ = 0.1×10⁻³ / (1.257×10⁻⁶ × 10⁻⁶) ≈ 80 GA/Wb; MEMS micro-inductors and integrated on-chip magnetics operate in the GA/Wb range. 1 GA/Wb = 10⁹ H⁻¹.

About Kiloamperes per weber

The kiloampere per weber (kA/Wb) equals 10³ H⁻¹. Typical for wound high-permeability cores: a 200-turn CRGO silicon-steel toroid with L = 40 H has ℛ = 10³ kA/Wb; nanocrystalline and amorphous common-mode chokes for EMI filtering typically have core reluctances in the kA/Wb - MA/Wb range. 1 kA/Wb = 10³ H⁻¹.
