About Microamperes per weber

The microampere per weber (µA/Wb) equals 10⁻⁶ H⁻¹. Represents extremely-low-reluctance circuits, only achievable in superconducting systems where flux is sustained without current. A 1 000-turn superconducting coil with L = 10⁶ H has ℛ = N²/L = 10⁻³ µA/Wb. A purely theoretical reference scale for normal materials. 1 µA/Wb = 10⁻⁶ H⁻¹.

About Gigaamperes per weber

The gigaampere per weber (GA/Wb) equals 10⁹ H⁻¹. The scale for small air gaps and miniaturized cores: a 0.1 mm air gap with 1 mm² cross-section has ℛ = 0.1×10⁻³ / (1.257×10⁻⁶ × 10⁻⁶) ≈ 80 GA/Wb; MEMS micro-inductors and integrated on-chip magnetics operate in the GA/Wb range. 1 GA/Wb = 10⁹ H⁻¹.
