About Gilberts per maxwell

The gilbert per maxwell (Gb/Mx) is the CGS-EMU unit of magnetic reluctance. 1 Gb/Mx = 10⁹/(4π) A/Wb ≈ 79.577 MA/Wb, placing it between MA/Wb and GA/Wb. Found in pre-SI magnetics literature and design tables. Derived from: 1 gilbert = 10/(4π) A·t and 1 maxwell = 10⁻⁸ Wb. 1 Gb/Mx ≈ 7.9577 × 10⁷ A/Wb.

About Gigaamperes per weber

The gigaampere per weber (GA/Wb) equals 10⁹ H⁻¹. The scale for small air gaps and miniaturized cores: a 0.1 mm air gap with 1 mm² cross-section has ℛ = 0.1×10⁻³ / (1.257×10⁻⁶ × 10⁻⁶) ≈ 80 GA/Wb; MEMS micro-inductors and integrated on-chip magnetics operate in the GA/Wb range. 1 GA/Wb = 10⁹ H⁻¹.
