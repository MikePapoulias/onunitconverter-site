About Kilooersteds

The kilooersted (kOe) equals 10⁶/(4π) A/m ≈ 79 577 A/m. Standard unit for permanent magnet coercivity in North American and Japanese industry datasheets: AlNiCo magnets have Hᴄ = 1-2 kOe, hard ferrites 1.5-4 kOe, NdFeB 10-30 kOe, SmCo 15-30 kOe. X-band EPR spectrometers sweep H from 0 to ≈3.5 kOe. 1 kOe = 10⁶/(4π) A/m.

About Milliamperes per metre

The milliampere per metre (mA/m) equals 10⁻³ A/m. Antenna near-field H-field measurements and EMC pre-compliance tests use mA/m; CISPR 14/16 and FCC Part 15 limit radiated field strength for consumer electronics in mA/m or equivalent dBµA/m at specified distances. 1 mA/m = 10⁻³ A/m = 10⁻³ Oe × (1000/4π).
