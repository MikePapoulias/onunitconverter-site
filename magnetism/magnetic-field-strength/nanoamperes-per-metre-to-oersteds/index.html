About Nanoamperes per metre

The nanoampere per metre (nA/m) equals 10⁻⁹ A/m. Geomagnetic surveys for mineral exploration report spatial variations in Earth's field at nA/m resolution; optically pumped caesium and rubidium magnetometers used in archaeological prospection detect nA/m-scale buried structure anomalies. 1 nA/m = 10⁻⁹ A/m = 10⁻³ µA/m.

About Oersteds

The oersted (Oe) equals exactly 1000/(4π) A/m ≈ 79.577 A/m, the CGS unit of magnetic field strength named after Hans Christian Ørsted. Still widely used in permanent magnet characterisation (NdFeB, SmCo, AlNiCo), magnetic recording, and older MRI literature. Earth's surface field is ≈ 0.5 Oe; a typical speaker magnet ≈ 5000 Oe. 1 Oe = 1000/(4π) A/m.
