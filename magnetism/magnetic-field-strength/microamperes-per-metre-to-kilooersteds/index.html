About Microamperes per metre

The microampere per metre (µA/m) equals 10⁻⁶ A/m. The Earth's geomagnetic field at mid-latitudes is approximately 40-50 A/m (50 000 nT / µ₀); low-frequency EMC immunity testing of sensitive electronics involves µA/m-level ambient AC magnetic fields. 1 µA/m = 10⁻⁶ A/m = 10⁻³ mA/m.

About Kilooersteds

The kilooersted (kOe) equals 10⁶/(4π) A/m ≈ 79 577 A/m. Standard unit for permanent magnet coercivity in North American and Japanese industry datasheets: AlNiCo magnets have Hᴄ = 1-2 kOe, hard ferrites 1.5-4 kOe, NdFeB 10-30 kOe, SmCo 15-30 kOe. X-band EPR spectrometers sweep H from 0 to ≈3.5 kOe. 1 kOe = 10⁶/(4π) A/m.
