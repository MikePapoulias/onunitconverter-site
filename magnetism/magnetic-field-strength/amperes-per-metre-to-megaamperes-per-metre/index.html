About Amperes per metre

The ampere per metre (A/m) is the SI coherent unit of magnetic field strength H, expressing the magnetomotive force per unit length. It defines how strongly a field drives magnetic flux through a material. Earth's geomagnetic H field is approximately 40-60 A/m; a typical transformer core operates at 100-1000 A/m. 1 A/m = 1 A m⁻¹.

About Megaamperes per metre

The megaampere per metre (MA/m) equals 10⁶ A/m. High-field electromagnets for physics research (Bitter magnets, hybrid magnets) generate 40-100 T requiring MA/m fields; particle accelerator dipole magnets (LHC: 8.3 T) operate at several MA/m; saturation magnetisation of iron is ≈1.36 MA/m. 1 MA/m = 10⁶ A/m ≈ 12 566 Oe.
