About Megaamperes per metre

The megaampere per metre (MA/m) equals 10⁶ A/m. High-field electromagnets for physics research (Bitter magnets, hybrid magnets) generate 40-100 T requiring MA/m fields; particle accelerator dipole magnets (LHC: 8.3 T) operate at several MA/m; saturation magnetisation of iron is ≈1.36 MA/m. 1 MA/m = 10⁶ A/m ≈ 12 566 Oe.

About Megaoersteds

The megaoersted (MOe) equals 10⁹/(4π) A/m ≈ 7.958 × 10⁷ A/m. Encountered in ultra-high-field pulsed magnet research - National High Magnetic Field Laboratory (NHMFL) 100 T pulsed fields correspond to ≈1 MOe; internal crystalline fields at magnetic rare-earth sites in hyperfine studies are of this order. 1 MOe = 10⁹/(4π) A/m.
