About Millioersteds

The millioersted (mOe) equals 1/(4π) A/m ≈ 79.577 mA/m. Used in magnetic recording media characterisation - remanent coercivity of flexible magnetic media (floppy disks, cassette tape) is often cited in mOe in legacy test standards; sensitive fluxgate magnetometers report data in mOe in older geomagnetic survey literature. 1 mOe = 1/(4π) A/m ≈ 0.07958 A/m.

About Microamperes per metre

The microampere per metre (µA/m) equals 10⁻⁶ A/m. The Earth's geomagnetic field at mid-latitudes is approximately 40-50 A/m (50 000 nT / µ₀); low-frequency EMC immunity testing of sensitive electronics involves µA/m-level ambient AC magnetic fields. 1 µA/m = 10⁻⁶ A/m = 10⁻³ mA/m.
