About Kilogilberts

The kilogilbert (kGb) equals 10³ Gb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t ≈ 795.8 A·t. Used in permanent magnet characterisation and demagnetisation testing; hard ferrite magnets develop a coercive MMF of roughly 1-5 kGb across a centimetre-scale air gap. 1 kGb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t.

About Nanoampere-turns

The nanoampere-turn (nA·t) equals 10⁻⁹ A·t. Applicable in fluxgate and search-coil magnetometer calibration for geomagnetic survey instruments, and in micro-inductor testing for CMOS RF circuits. 1 nA·t = 10⁻⁹ A·t.
