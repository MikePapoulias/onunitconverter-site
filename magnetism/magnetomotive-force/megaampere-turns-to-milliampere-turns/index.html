Conversion Formula

About Megaampere-turns

The megaampere-turn (MA·t) equals 10⁶ A·t. Characteristic MMF of large superconducting solenoids - a 3 T MRI bore magnet requires roughly 2-5 MA·t; particle accelerator dipole bending magnets at CERN reach several MA·t. 1 MA·t = 10⁶ A·t.

About Milliampere-turns

The milliampere-turn (mA·t) equals 10⁻³ A·t. Practical unit for reed relays and small inductors - a typical reed relay operates at 20-100 mA·t; audio transformer cores for hearing aids are magnetised at a few mA·t. 1 mA·t = 10⁻³ A·t.
