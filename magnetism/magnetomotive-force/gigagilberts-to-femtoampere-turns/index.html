About Gigagilberts

The gigagilbert (GGb) equals 10⁹ Gb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁸ A·t. An extreme unit used in astrophysical and fusion research; pulsed-power Z-pinch drivers and tokamak plasma experiments transiently approach GGb-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 GGb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t.

About Femtoampere-turns

The femtoampere-turn (fA·t) equals 10⁻¹⁵ A·t. Used in SQUID-based nanoscale magnetic sensor calibration and spintronics research where single-turn excitation loops carry femtoampere bias currents. 1 fA·t = 10⁻¹⁵ A·t.
