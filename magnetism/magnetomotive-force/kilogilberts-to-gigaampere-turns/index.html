About Kilogilberts

The kilogilbert (kGb) equals 10³ Gb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t ≈ 795.8 A·t. Used in permanent magnet characterisation and demagnetisation testing; hard ferrite magnets develop a coercive MMF of roughly 1-5 kGb across a centimetre-scale air gap. 1 kGb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t.

About Gigaampere-turns

The gigaampere-turn (GA·t) equals 10⁹ A·t. Relevant only in astrophysics - the equivalent MMF of a magnetar neutron star's surface field spans GA·t to TA·t; proposed inertial confinement fusion targets require GA·t-scale pulsed solenoid drives. 1 GA·t = 10⁹ A·t.
