About Gilberts

The gilbert (Gb) equals exactly 10/(4π) A·t ≈ 0.7958 A·t, the CGS unit of magnetomotive force named after William Gilbert (1544-1603). Widely used in older relay and electromagnet datasheets; 1 Gb = 1 oersted·centimetre. 1 Gb = 10/(4π) A·t.

About Megagilberts

The megagilbert (MGb) equals 10⁶ Gb = 10⁷/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁵ A·t. Found in CGS-based calculations for large resistive electromagnets; some superconducting magnetic energy storage (SMES) coils reach the MGb range. 1 MGb = 10⁷/(4π) A·t.
