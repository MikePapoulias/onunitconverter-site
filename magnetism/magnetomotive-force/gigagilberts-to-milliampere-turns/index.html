About Gigagilberts

The gigagilbert (GGb) equals 10⁹ Gb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁸ A·t. An extreme unit used in astrophysical and fusion research; pulsed-power Z-pinch drivers and tokamak plasma experiments transiently approach GGb-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 GGb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t.

About Milliampere-turns

The milliampere-turn (mA·t) equals 10⁻³ A·t. Practical unit for reed relays and small inductors - a typical reed relay operates at 20-100 mA·t; audio transformer cores for hearing aids are magnetised at a few mA·t. 1 mA·t = 10⁻³ A·t.
