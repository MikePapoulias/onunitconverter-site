Conversion Formula

About Megaampere-turns

The megaampere-turn (MA·t) equals 10⁶ A·t. Characteristic MMF of large superconducting solenoids - a 3 T MRI bore magnet requires roughly 2-5 MA·t; particle accelerator dipole bending magnets at CERN reach several MA·t. 1 MA·t = 10⁶ A·t.

About Kilogilberts

The kilogilbert (kGb) equals 10³ Gb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t ≈ 795.8 A·t. Used in permanent magnet characterisation and demagnetisation testing; hard ferrite magnets develop a coercive MMF of roughly 1-5 kGb across a centimetre-scale air gap. 1 kGb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t.
