About Milligilberts

The milligilbert (mGb) equals 10⁻³ Gb = 10/(4000π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁻⁴ A·t. A CGS submultiple rarely seen in modern practice; encountered in early 20th-century electrical engineering literature and vintage relay test records. 1 mGb = 10/(4000π) A·t.

About Nanoampere-turns

The nanoampere-turn (nA·t) equals 10⁻⁹ A·t. Applicable in fluxgate and search-coil magnetometer calibration for geomagnetic survey instruments, and in micro-inductor testing for CMOS RF circuits. 1 nA·t = 10⁻⁹ A·t.
