About Kilogilberts

The kilogilbert (kGb) equals 10³ Gb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t ≈ 795.8 A·t. Used in permanent magnet characterisation and demagnetisation testing; hard ferrite magnets develop a coercive MMF of roughly 1-5 kGb across a centimetre-scale air gap. 1 kGb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t.

About Ampere-turns

The ampere-turn (A·t) is the SI practical unit of magnetomotive force, defined as F = N × I (turns × current). A 100-turn relay coil at 10 mA produces 1 A·t; power transformer cores typically operate at 100-1000 A·t. 1 A·t = 1 A (turn is dimensionless in SI).
