About Microampere-turns

The microampere-turn (µA·t) equals 10⁻⁶ A·t. Used in precision current transformer calibration and thin-film magnetic sensor testing where small coil excitations generate µA·t-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 µA·t = 10⁻⁶ A·t.

About Ampere-turns

The ampere-turn (A·t) is the SI practical unit of magnetomotive force, defined as F = N × I (turns × current). A 100-turn relay coil at 10 mA produces 1 A·t; power transformer cores typically operate at 100-1000 A·t. 1 A·t = 1 A (turn is dimensionless in SI).
