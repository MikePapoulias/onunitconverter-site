About Ampere-turns

The ampere-turn (A·t) is the SI practical unit of magnetomotive force, defined as F = N × I (turns × current). A 100-turn relay coil at 10 mA produces 1 A·t; power transformer cores typically operate at 100-1000 A·t. 1 A·t = 1 A (turn is dimensionless in SI).

About Gilberts

The gilbert (Gb) equals exactly 10/(4π) A·t ≈ 0.7958 A·t, the CGS unit of magnetomotive force named after William Gilbert (1544-1603). Widely used in older relay and electromagnet datasheets; 1 Gb = 1 oersted·centimetre. 1 Gb = 10/(4π) A·t.
