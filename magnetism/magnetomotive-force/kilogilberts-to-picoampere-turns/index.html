About Kilogilberts

The kilogilbert (kGb) equals 10³ Gb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t ≈ 795.8 A·t. Used in permanent magnet characterisation and demagnetisation testing; hard ferrite magnets develop a coercive MMF of roughly 1-5 kGb across a centimetre-scale air gap. 1 kGb = 10⁴/(4π) A·t.

About Picoampere-turns

The picoampere-turn (pA·t) equals 10⁻¹² A·t. Relevant in ultra-sensitive flux detection with SQUID magnetometers for magnetoencephalography (MEG), where millikelvin-cooled coils respond to pA·t-level signals from neural currents. 1 pA·t = 10⁻¹² A·t.
