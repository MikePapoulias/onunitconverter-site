About Megagilberts

The megagilbert (MGb) equals 10⁶ Gb = 10⁷/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁵ A·t. Found in CGS-based calculations for large resistive electromagnets; some superconducting magnetic energy storage (SMES) coils reach the MGb range. 1 MGb = 10⁷/(4π) A·t.

About Nanoampere-turns

The nanoampere-turn (nA·t) equals 10⁻⁹ A·t. Applicable in fluxgate and search-coil magnetometer calibration for geomagnetic survey instruments, and in micro-inductor testing for CMOS RF circuits. 1 nA·t = 10⁻⁹ A·t.
