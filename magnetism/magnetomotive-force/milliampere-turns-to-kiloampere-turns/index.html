About Milliampere-turns

The milliampere-turn (mA·t) equals 10⁻³ A·t. Practical unit for reed relays and small inductors - a typical reed relay operates at 20-100 mA·t; audio transformer cores for hearing aids are magnetised at a few mA·t. 1 mA·t = 10⁻³ A·t.

About Kiloampere-turns

The kiloampere-turn (kA·t) equals 10³ A·t. Standard engineering unit for industrial electromagnets and transformer windings - a 500-turn winding at 2 A produces 1 kA·t; MRI gradient coils are driven at tens of kA·t. 1 kA·t = 10³ A·t.
