About Milligilberts

The milligilbert (mGb) equals 10⁻³ Gb = 10/(4000π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁻⁴ A·t. A CGS submultiple rarely seen in modern practice; encountered in early 20th-century electrical engineering literature and vintage relay test records. 1 mGb = 10/(4000π) A·t.

About Gigagilberts

The gigagilbert (GGb) equals 10⁹ Gb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁸ A·t. An extreme unit used in astrophysical and fusion research; pulsed-power Z-pinch drivers and tokamak plasma experiments transiently approach GGb-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 GGb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t.
