About Gigagilberts

The gigagilbert (GGb) equals 10⁹ Gb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t ≈ 7.958 × 10⁸ A·t. An extreme unit used in astrophysical and fusion research; pulsed-power Z-pinch drivers and tokamak plasma experiments transiently approach GGb-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 GGb = 10¹⁰/(4π) A·t.

About Microampere-turns

The microampere-turn (µA·t) equals 10⁻⁶ A·t. Used in precision current transformer calibration and thin-film magnetic sensor testing where small coil excitations generate µA·t-scale magnetomotive forces. 1 µA·t = 10⁻⁶ A·t.
