About Gausses

The gauss (G) equals exactly 10⁻⁴ T, the CGS unit of magnetic flux density. Still widely used in magnetics engineering: permanent magnet remanence is typically 5 000-14 000 G for NdFeB; MRI field strengths are sometimes quoted as 10 000 G (1 T); loudspeaker gap fields are 8 000-20 000 G. 1 G = 10⁻⁴ T.

About Milliteslas

The millitesla (mT) equals 10⁻³ T = 10 G. Used in industrial induction heating, magnetic particle inspection (MPI), and low-field MRI systems; the fringe field at the 5 G (0.5 mT) safety line around clinical MRI scanners is the standard exclusion boundary. 1 mT = 10⁻³ T = 10 G.
