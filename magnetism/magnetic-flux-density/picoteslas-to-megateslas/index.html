About Picoteslas

The picotesla (pT) equals 10⁻¹² T. Used in magnetocardiography (MCG) and magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) - the heart's magnetic field peaks at around 50-100 pT; a submerged ferromagnetic submarine distorts Earth's field by a few pT at detection range. 1 pT = 10⁻¹² T.

About Megateslas

The megatesla (MT) equals 10⁶ T. Relevant only in astrophysics - neutron star (magnetar) surface fields reach 10⁸-10¹¹ T (0.1-100 000 MT); laser-driven implosion experiments have produced fields approaching 0.001 MT transiently. 1 MT = 10⁶ T = 10¹⁰ G.
