About Microteslas

The microtesla (µT) equals 10⁻⁶ T. Earth's surface magnetic field is approximately 25-65 µT; the WHO recommends 100 µT as an occupational limit for 50/60 Hz fields; metal detectors and electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) tests routinely work in the µT range. 1 µT = 10⁻⁶ T = 10 mG.

About Gausses

The gauss (G) equals exactly 10⁻⁴ T, the CGS unit of magnetic flux density. Still widely used in magnetics engineering: permanent magnet remanence is typically 5 000-14 000 G for NdFeB; MRI field strengths are sometimes quoted as 10 000 G (1 T); loudspeaker gap fields are 8 000-20 000 G. 1 G = 10⁻⁴ T.
