About Megateslas

The megatesla (MT) equals 10⁶ T. Relevant only in astrophysics - neutron star (magnetar) surface fields reach 10⁸-10¹¹ T (0.1-100 000 MT); laser-driven implosion experiments have produced fields approaching 0.001 MT transiently. 1 MT = 10⁶ T = 10¹⁰ G.

About Milliteslas

The millitesla (mT) equals 10⁻³ T = 10 G. Used in industrial induction heating, magnetic particle inspection (MPI), and low-field MRI systems; the fringe field at the 5 G (0.5 mT) safety line around clinical MRI scanners is the standard exclusion boundary. 1 mT = 10⁻³ T = 10 G.
