About Picoteslas

The picotesla (pT) equals 10⁻¹² T. Used in magnetocardiography (MCG) and magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) - the heart's magnetic field peaks at around 50-100 pT; a submerged ferromagnetic submarine distorts Earth's field by a few pT at detection range. 1 pT = 10⁻¹² T.

About Milliteslas

The millitesla (mT) equals 10⁻³ T = 10 G. Used in industrial induction heating, magnetic particle inspection (MPI), and low-field MRI systems; the fringe field at the 5 G (0.5 mT) safety line around clinical MRI scanners is the standard exclusion boundary. 1 mT = 10⁻³ T = 10 G.
