About Microteslas

The microtesla (µT) equals 10⁻⁶ T. Earth's surface magnetic field is approximately 25-65 µT; the WHO recommends 100 µT as an occupational limit for 50/60 Hz fields; metal detectors and electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) tests routinely work in the µT range. 1 µT = 10⁻⁶ T = 10 mG.

About Nanoteslas

The nanotesla (nT) equals 10⁻⁹ T. The standard unit in geomagnetism and space physics: Earth's surface field is 25 000-65 000 nT; geomagnetic storms cause fluctuations of hundreds to thousands of nT; solar wind field at 1 AU is 2-10 nT. 1 nT = 10⁻⁹ T = 1 γ.
