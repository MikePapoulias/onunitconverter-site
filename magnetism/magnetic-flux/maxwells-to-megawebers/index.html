About Maxwells

The maxwell (Mx) equals exactly 10⁻⁸ Wb, the CGS unit of magnetic flux named after James Clerk Maxwell. Still widely used in permanent magnet and motor design datasheets; a typical 10 mm NdFeB disc magnet has a remanent flux of around 10 000-100 000 Mx. 1 Mx = 10⁻⁸ Wb.

About Megawebers

The megaweber (MWb) equals 10⁶ Wb. Encountered in calculations for very large power plant generators and astrophysical applications; the total flux through a cross-section of a large sunspot group can reach MWb scale. 1 MWb = 10⁶ Wb = 10¹⁴ Mx.
