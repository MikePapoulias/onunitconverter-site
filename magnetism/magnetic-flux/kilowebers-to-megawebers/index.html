About Kilowebers

The kiloweber (kWb) equals 10³ Wb. Relevant for large power generator and turbine alternator flux calculations; a large hydro generator rotor pole may link 1-5 kWb; high-field research electromagnets with large-area pole faces reach kWb total flux. 1 kWb = 10³ Wb = 10¹¹ Mx.

About Megawebers

The megaweber (MWb) equals 10⁶ Wb. Encountered in calculations for very large power plant generators and astrophysical applications; the total flux through a cross-section of a large sunspot group can reach MWb scale. 1 MWb = 10⁶ Wb = 10¹⁴ Mx.
