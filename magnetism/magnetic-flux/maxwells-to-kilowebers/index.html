About Maxwells

The maxwell (Mx) equals exactly 10⁻⁸ Wb, the CGS unit of magnetic flux named after James Clerk Maxwell. Still widely used in permanent magnet and motor design datasheets; a typical 10 mm NdFeB disc magnet has a remanent flux of around 10 000-100 000 Mx. 1 Mx = 10⁻⁸ Wb.

About Kilowebers

The kiloweber (kWb) equals 10³ Wb. Relevant for large power generator and turbine alternator flux calculations; a large hydro generator rotor pole may link 1-5 kWb; high-field research electromagnets with large-area pole faces reach kWb total flux. 1 kWb = 10³ Wb = 10¹¹ Mx.
