About Maxwells

The maxwell (Mx) equals exactly 10⁻⁸ Wb, the CGS unit of magnetic flux named after James Clerk Maxwell. Still widely used in permanent magnet and motor design datasheets; a typical 10 mm NdFeB disc magnet has a remanent flux of around 10 000-100 000 Mx. 1 Mx = 10⁻⁸ Wb.

About Milliwebers

The milliweber (mWb) equals 10⁻³ Wb. Practical unit for small power inductors and relay cores - a typical E-core ferrite transformer with a 1 cm² cross-section at 0.3 T saturates at 0.3 mWb; audio transformer cores operate in the mWb range. 1 mWb = 10⁻³ Wb = 10⁵ Mx.
