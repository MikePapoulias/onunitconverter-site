About Microwebers

The microweber (µWb) equals 10⁻⁶ Wb = 100 Mx. Used in small inductor and reed switch characterisation; a ferrite bead on a PCB trace links a few µWb at normal operating flux densities. 1 µWb = 10⁻⁶ Wb = 100 Mx.

About Kilowebers

The kiloweber (kWb) equals 10³ Wb. Relevant for large power generator and turbine alternator flux calculations; a large hydro generator rotor pole may link 1-5 kWb; high-field research electromagnets with large-area pole faces reach kWb total flux. 1 kWb = 10³ Wb = 10¹¹ Mx.
