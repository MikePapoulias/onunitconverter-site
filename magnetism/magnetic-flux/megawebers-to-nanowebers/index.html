About Megawebers

The megaweber (MWb) equals 10⁶ Wb. Encountered in calculations for very large power plant generators and astrophysical applications; the total flux through a cross-section of a large sunspot group can reach MWb scale. 1 MWb = 10⁶ Wb = 10¹⁴ Mx.

About Nanowebers

The nanoweber (nWb) equals 10⁻⁹ Wb. Relevant in search-coil magnetometer calibration, fluxgate sensor testing, and geomagnetic observatory instruments; a 100-turn coil of 10 cm² area in Earth's field (∼50 µT) links ∼50 nWb. 1 nWb = 10⁻⁹ Wb = 10 Mx.
