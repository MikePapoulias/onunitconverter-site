About Flux quanta

The magnetic flux quantum (Φ₀ = h/2e) equals exactly 2.067833848×10⁻¹⁵ Wb (2019 SI definition). The natural unit of flux in superconductors - flux enters a type-II superconductor in integer multiples of Φ₀ (Abrikosov vortices). Used in SQUID, Josephson junction and quantum computing metrology. 1 Φ₀ ≈ 2.068 fWb.

About Megawebers

The megaweber (MWb) equals 10⁶ Wb. Encountered in calculations for very large power plant generators and astrophysical applications; the total flux through a cross-section of a large sunspot group can reach MWb scale. 1 MWb = 10⁶ Wb = 10¹⁴ Mx.
