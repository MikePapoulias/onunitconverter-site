About Femtohenries per metre

The femtohenry per metre (fH/m) equals 10⁻¹⁵ H/m. A theoretical subunit used in precision electromagnetic metrology. Even the extreme diamagnet bismuth (µᵣ = 0.999834) departs from µ₀ by about −207 nH/m - far above the fH/m scale. Primarily a reference unit for quantum EM standard calculations. 1 fH/m = 10⁻¹⁵ H/m.

About Vacuum permeabilities

The vacuum permeability (µ₀), also called the magnetic constant, equals 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m ≈ 1.25664 µH/m. In the 2019 SI it is experimentally determined: µ₀ = 1.25663706212(19) × 10⁻⁶ H/m. The absolute permeability of any material is µ = µᵣ × µ₀. 1 µ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m.
