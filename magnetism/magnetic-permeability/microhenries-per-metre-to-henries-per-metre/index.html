About Microhenries per metre

The microhenry per metre (µH/m) equals 10⁻⁶ H/m. The most practical scale for absolute permeability: vacuum permeability µ₀ ≈ 1.257 µH/m, so all non-magnetic materials (air, plastics, biological tissue) have µ between 1.256 and 1.258 µH/m. Relative permeability is simply µᵣ = µ / 1.257 µH/m. 1 µH/m = 10⁻⁶ H/m.

About Henries per metre

The henry per metre (H/m) is the SI coherent unit of absolute magnetic permeability, equal to 1 kg·m·s⁻²·A⁻². Only the highest-permeability alloys reach this scale: annealed Mu-metal achieves µ ≈ 0.1-0.4 H/m (µᵣ up to 300 000); Supermalloy under optimal conditions approaches µ ≈ 1 H/m (µᵣ ≈ 10⁶). 1 H/m ≈ 7.96 × 10⁵ µ₀.
