About Nanohenries per metre

The nanohenry per metre (nH/m) equals 10⁻⁹ H/m. The natural scale for expressing permeability deviations of nearly non-magnetic materials from µ₀ ≈ 1 256.6 nH/m: paramagnetic aluminium (µᵣ = 1.0000220) exceeds µ₀ by about 28 nH/m; diamagnetic copper (µᵣ = 0.999994) falls about 8 nH/m below µ₀. 1 nH/m = 10⁻⁹ H/m.

About Kilohenries per metre

The kilohenry per metre (kH/m) equals 10³ H/m ≈ 7.96 × 10⁸ µ₀. A theoretical reference scale beyond any known bulk magnetic material: achieving kH/m would require µᵣ ≳ 10⁹, far exceeding the highest measured value (~10⁶ for Supermalloy). Used as an upper bound in theoretical magnetics. 1 kH/m = 10³ H/m.
