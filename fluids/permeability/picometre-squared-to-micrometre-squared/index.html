About Picometre squared

The picometre squared (pm²) equals 10⁻²⁴ m², used in molecular simulation and crystallography for the intrinsic permeability of dense crystalline solids. Halite and anhydrite crystal lattices have calculated matrix permeabilities below 1 pm²; theoretical studies of gas diffusion through graphene oxide membranes work at this scale. 1 pm² = 10⁻⁶ nm² = 10⁻²⁴ m².

About Micrometre squared

The micrometre squared (µm²) equals 10⁻¹² m², the SI-consistent permeability unit used in European reservoir standards and ISO documents. 1 µm² ≈ 1.0133 D - numerically close to but distinct from the darcy. Clean gravel: 1-10 µm²; clean sandstone: 10⁻³-0.1 µm². 1 µm² = 10⁶ nm² = 10⁻⁶ mm².
