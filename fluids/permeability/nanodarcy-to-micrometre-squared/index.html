About Nanodarcy

The nanodarcy (nD) equals 10⁻⁹ D ≈ 9.869 × 10⁻²² m², the standard permeability unit for ultra-tight unconventional reservoirs. Barnett, Marcellus, and Eagle Ford shale matrix: 1-1 000 nD; crystalline basement rocks and caprocks: below 1 nD. Measured by pulse-decay or GRI crushed-rock methods. 1 nD = 10⁻³ µD = 10⁻⁶ mD.

About Micrometre squared

The micrometre squared (µm²) equals 10⁻¹² m², the SI-consistent permeability unit used in European reservoir standards and ISO documents. 1 µm² ≈ 1.0133 D - numerically close to but distinct from the darcy. Clean gravel: 1-10 µm²; clean sandstone: 10⁻³-0.1 µm². 1 µm² = 10⁶ nm² = 10⁻⁶ mm².
