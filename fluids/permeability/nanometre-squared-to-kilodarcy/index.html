About Nanometre squared

The nanometre squared (nm²) equals 10⁻¹⁸ m², used in membrane science and nanotechnology for nanoporous media. ZSM-5 zeolite pore cross-section ≈ 0.25 nm²; ceramic ultrafiltration membranes: 0.01-1 nm². Numerically, 1 nm² ≈ 1.013 µD. 1 nm² = 10⁻⁶ µm² = 10⁻¹⁸ m².

About Kilodarcy

The kilodarcy (kD) equals 10³ D ≈ 9.869 × 10⁻¹⁰ m², used for very high-permeability porous structures. Gravel packs in oil wells: 1-100 kD; vuggy dissolved carbonates: 1-10 kD; loose sand-gravel mixtures: 10-1 000 kD. 1 kD = 10³ D = 10⁶ mD.
