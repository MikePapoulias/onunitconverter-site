About Kilomoles per cubic metre

The kilomole per cubic metre (kmol/m³) equals 1 000 mol/m³, numerically identical to mol/L. It is used in chemical and process engineering where molar flow rates are in kmol/h and reactor volumes in m³, making kmol/m³ the natural concentration unit for material balance calculations. 1 kmol/m³ = 1 mol/L = 1 mol/dm³.

About Moles per decilitre

The mole per decilitre (mol/dL) equals 10 000 mol/m³ = 10 mol/L, a rarely encountered unit occasionally found in older US clinical-chemistry references for highly concentrated analytes or conversion tables alongside mg/dL values. 1 mol/dL = 10 mol/L = 10 000 mmol/L.
