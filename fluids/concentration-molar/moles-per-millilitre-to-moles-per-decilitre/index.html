Conversion Formula

mol/dL = mol/mL × 100

About Moles per millilitre

The mole per millilitre (mol/mL) equals 10⁶ mol/m³ = 1 000 mol/L, used for extremely concentrated solutions and in nuclear medicine for radioligand and radiopharmaceutical stock solutions. Numerically identical to mol/cm³. 1 mol/mL = 1 000 mol/L = 10⁶ mol/m³.

About Moles per decilitre

The mole per decilitre (mol/dL) equals 10 000 mol/m³ = 10 mol/L, a rarely encountered unit occasionally found in older US clinical-chemistry references for highly concentrated analytes or conversion tables alongside mg/dL values. 1 mol/dL = 10 mol/L = 10 000 mmol/L.
