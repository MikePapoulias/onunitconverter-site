About Millimoles per litre

The millimole per litre (mmol/L, mM) equals 1 mol/m³, the dominant unit in clinical chemistry worldwide (SI system). Blood glucose: 3.9-6.1 mmol/L; serum sodium: 135-145 mmol/L; total cholesterol: 3.9-5.2 mmol/L. Numerically identical to mol/m³. 1 mmol/L = 10⁻³ mol/L = 1 mol/m³.

About Moles per cubic centimetre

The mole per cubic centimetre (mol/cm³) equals 10⁶ mol/m³ = 1 000 mol/L, the CGS coherent unit of molar concentration. Numerically identical to mol/mL. It appears in older physical chemistry literature and materials science when CGS units are used for molar volume or lattice-site density. 1 mol/cm³ = 1 mol/mL = 10⁶ mol/m³.
