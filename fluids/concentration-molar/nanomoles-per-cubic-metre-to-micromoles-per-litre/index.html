About Nanomoles per cubic metre

The nanomole per cubic metre (nmol/m³) equals 10⁻⁹ mol/m³, used in atmospheric chemistry to express trace gas concentrations in air. At NTP, 1 ppb (v/v) of a gas ≈ 40 nmol/m³; indoor benzene at 1 ppb ≈ 40 nmol/m³. Numerically identical to pmol/L. 1 nmol/m³ = 10⁻³ µmol/m³.

About Micromoles per litre

The micromole per litre (µmol/L, µM) equals 10⁻³ mol/m³, widely used in pharmacology, clinical toxicology, and biochemistry for metabolite and drug concentrations. Serum creatinine: 62-115 µmol/L; uric acid: 200-430 µmol/L; therapeutic phenytoin: 40-80 µmol/L. 1 µmol/L = 10⁻³ mmol/L.
