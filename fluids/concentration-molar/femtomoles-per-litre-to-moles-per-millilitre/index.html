Convert Femtomoles per litre (fmol/L) to Moles per millilitre (mol/mL) instantly and accurately.

Conversion Formula

mol/mL = fmol/L × 1.000000000e-18

About Femtomoles per litre

The femtomole per litre (fmol/L, fM) equals 10⁻¹² mol/m³, the standard unit in ultra-sensitive immunoassay, digital ELISA, and LC-MS/MS quantification. Cytokines such as IL-6 occur at 1-100 fmol/L in healthy serum; high-sensitivity cardiac troponin assays report results at this level. 1 fmol/L = 10⁻³ pmol/L = 10⁻¹² mol/m³.

About Moles per millilitre

The mole per millilitre (mol/mL) equals 10⁶ mol/m³ = 1 000 mol/L, used for extremely concentrated solutions and in nuclear medicine for radioligand and radiopharmaceutical stock solutions. Numerically identical to mol/cm³. 1 mol/mL = 1 000 mol/L = 10⁶ mol/m³.
