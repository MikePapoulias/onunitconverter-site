About Kilostokes

The kilostoke (kSt) equals 1000 St = 0.1 m²/s. It is used for extremely viscous materials such as heavy polymer melts and bitumen at low temperatures, where centistoke values would reach into the hundreds of thousands. 1 kSt = 10³ St = 10⁵ cSt = 0.1 m²/s.

About Square millimetres per minute

The square millimetre per minute (mm²/min) equals 1/(6 × 10⁷) m²/s ≈ 1.667 × 10⁻⁸ m²/s. It is used in polymer-film formation and printing-ink diffusion studies where minute-scale time resolution is more convenient. 1 mm²/min = 1/60 cSt.
