About Square millimetres per second

The square millimetre per second (mm²/s) equals 10⁻⁶ m²/s, numerically identical to 1 centistoke (cSt). It is the preferred SI-consistent expression: ISO 3104, DIN 51562, and ASTM viscosity standards all use mm²/s interchangeably with cSt. Water at 20 °C ≈ 1.004 mm²/s; SAE 10W-30 at 100 °C: 9.3-12.5 mm²/s. 1 mm²/s = 1 cSt.

About Square metres per second

The square metre per second (m²/s) is the SI coherent unit of kinematic viscosity (ν = μ/ρ). Air at 20 °C ≈ 1.516 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s; water at 20 °C ≈ 1.004 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s; glycerol ≈ 1.19 × 10⁻³ m²/s. It is the standard in CFD codes and SI research literature. 1 m²/s = 10⁴ St = 10⁶ cSt.
