About Kilostokes

The kilostoke (kSt) equals 1000 St = 0.1 m²/s. It is used for extremely viscous materials such as heavy polymer melts and bitumen at low temperatures, where centistoke values would reach into the hundreds of thousands. 1 kSt = 10³ St = 10⁵ cSt = 0.1 m²/s.

About Square inches per hour

The square inch per hour (in²/h) equals 0.0254²/3600 ≈ 1.792 × 10⁻⁷ m²/s. It is found in US heat-transfer and diffusion textbooks that use the BTU-inch-hour unit system alongside thermal diffusivity tables. 1 in²/h ≈ 0.1792 cSt.
