About Millistokes

The millistoke (mSt) equals 10⁻⁷ m²/s. It appears in research on low-viscosity solvents, dilute polymer solutions, and light gases whose kinematic viscosity is in the sub-centistoke range. 1 mSt = 10⁻³ St = 0.1 cSt = 10⁻⁷ m²/s.

About Square feet per minute

The square foot per minute (ft²/min) equals 0.3048²/60 ≈ 1.548 × 10⁻³ m²/s. It appears in US HVAC, ventilation, and turbine-cooling design where velocities are quoted in ft/min. 1 ft²/min ≈ 1548 cSt.
