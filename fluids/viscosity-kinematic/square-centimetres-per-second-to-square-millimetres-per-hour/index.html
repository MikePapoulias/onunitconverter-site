Conversion Formula

mm²/h = cm²/s × 360000

About Square centimetres per second

The square centimetre per second (cm²/s) equals 10⁻⁴ m²/s, numerically identical to 1 stoke (St). It is the explicit CGS dimensional form, preferred in dimensional analysis or when avoiding named units. 1 cm²/s = 1 St = 100 cSt.

About Square millimetres per hour

The square millimetre per hour (mm²/h) equals 1/(3.6 × 10⁹) m²/s ≈ 2.78 × 10⁻¹⁰ m²/s. It appears in materials science for solid-state diffusion and thin-film growth studies where hourly timescales are more natural than seconds. 1 mm²/h = 1/3600 mm²/s = 1/3600 cSt.
