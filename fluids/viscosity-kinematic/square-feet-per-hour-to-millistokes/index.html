Conversion Formula

mSt = ft²/h × 258.064

About Square feet per hour

The square foot per hour (ft²/h) equals 0.3048²/3600 ≈ 2.581 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s. It is the standard US unit for thermal diffusivity in the BTU-ft-h system, used alongside heat-transfer and mass-transfer calculations. Air at 20 °C ≈ 0.573 ft²/h; water at 20 °C ≈ 0.0096 ft²/h. 1 ft²/h ≈ 25.81 cSt.

About Millistokes

The millistoke (mSt) equals 10⁻⁷ m²/s. It appears in research on low-viscosity solvents, dilute polymer solutions, and light gases whose kinematic viscosity is in the sub-centistoke range. 1 mSt = 10⁻³ St = 0.1 cSt = 10⁻⁷ m²/s.
