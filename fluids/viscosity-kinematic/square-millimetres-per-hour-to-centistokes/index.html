About Square millimetres per hour

The square millimetre per hour (mm²/h) equals 1/(3.6 × 10⁹) m²/s ≈ 2.78 × 10⁻¹⁰ m²/s. It appears in materials science for solid-state diffusion and thin-film growth studies where hourly timescales are more natural than seconds. 1 mm²/h = 1/3600 mm²/s = 1/3600 cSt.

About Centistokes

The centistoke (cSt) equals 10⁻⁶ m²/s = 1 mm²/s. It is the dominant unit for kinematic viscosity in industry worldwide. ASTM D445, ISO 3104, and ACEA lubricant specifications all use cSt. Water at 20 °C ≈ 1.004 cSt; diesel at 40 °C: 2.0-4.5 cSt; SAE 40 oil at 100 °C: 12.5-16.3 cSt. 1 cSt = 10⁻² St.
