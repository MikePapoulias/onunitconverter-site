About Square inches per minute

The square inch per minute (in²/min) equals 0.0254²/60 ≈ 1.075 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s. It appears in US coating and printing industry references where area coverage rates in in²/min are combined with kinematic flow data. 1 in²/min ≈ 10.75 cSt.

About Square inches per second

The square inch per second (in²/s) equals 0.0254² = 6.4516 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s. It is the coherent FPS kinematic unit based on the inch, found in US fluid power, hydraulics, and bearing-design references. 1 in²/s = 645.16 cSt = 6.4516 St.
