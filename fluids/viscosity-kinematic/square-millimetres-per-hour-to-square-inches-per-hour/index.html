About Square millimetres per hour

The square millimetre per hour (mm²/h) equals 1/(3.6 × 10⁹) m²/s ≈ 2.78 × 10⁻¹⁰ m²/s. It appears in materials science for solid-state diffusion and thin-film growth studies where hourly timescales are more natural than seconds. 1 mm²/h = 1/3600 mm²/s = 1/3600 cSt.

About Square inches per hour

The square inch per hour (in²/h) equals 0.0254²/3600 ≈ 1.792 × 10⁻⁷ m²/s. It is found in US heat-transfer and diffusion textbooks that use the BTU-inch-hour unit system alongside thermal diffusivity tables. 1 in²/h ≈ 0.1792 cSt.
