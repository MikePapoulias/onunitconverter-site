Conversion Formula

About Square feet per minute

The square foot per minute (ft²/min) equals 0.3048²/60 ≈ 1.548 × 10⁻³ m²/s. It appears in US HVAC, ventilation, and turbine-cooling design where velocities are quoted in ft/min. 1 ft²/min ≈ 1548 cSt.

About Kilostokes

The kilostoke (kSt) equals 1000 St = 0.1 m²/s. It is used for extremely viscous materials such as heavy polymer melts and bitumen at low temperatures, where centistoke values would reach into the hundreds of thousands. 1 kSt = 10³ St = 10⁵ cSt = 0.1 m²/s.
