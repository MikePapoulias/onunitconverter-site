About Milligrams per square metre per second

The milligram per square metre per second (mg/(m²·s)) equals 10⁻⁶ kg/(m²·s). It is used in aerosol science and atmospheric deposition studies for measuring dry deposition fluxes of particulate matter. Typical urban PM2.5 dry deposition velocities produce fluxes of 0.01-1 mg/(m²·s). 1 mg/(m²·s) = 3 600 mg/(m²·h).

About Grams per square metre per hour

The gram per square metre per hour (g/(m²·h)) equals 10⁻³/3600 ≈ 2.778 × 10⁻⁷ kg/(m²·s). It is widely used in textile and paper engineering for moisture management, evaporation studies, and water-vapour transmission rate (WVTR) testing of barrier materials per ISO 2528 and ASTM E96. Breathable fabrics range 500-10 000 g/(m²·h) (=500-10 000 gsm/h). 1 g/(m²·h) = 10⁻³ kg/(m²·h).
