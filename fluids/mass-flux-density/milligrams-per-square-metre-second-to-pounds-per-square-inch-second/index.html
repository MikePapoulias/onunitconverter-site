About Milligrams per square metre per second

The milligram per square metre per second (mg/(m²·s)) equals 10⁻⁶ kg/(m²·s). It is used in aerosol science and atmospheric deposition studies for measuring dry deposition fluxes of particulate matter. Typical urban PM2.5 dry deposition velocities produce fluxes of 0.01-1 mg/(m²·s). 1 mg/(m²·s) = 3 600 mg/(m²·h).

About Pounds per square inch per second

The pound per square inch per second (lb/(in²·s)) equals 0.45359237/(0.0254 × 0.0254) ≈ 703.07 kg/(m²·s). It is used in US firearms ballistics, shaped-charge and explosives engineering, and hydraulic press analysis where forces per square inch dominate the unit system. 1 lb/(in²·s) ≈ 703.07 kg/(m²·s) = 144 lb/(ft²·s).
