About Grams per square metre per second

The gram per square metre per second (g/(m²·s)) equals 10⁻³ kg/(m²·s). It is used in combustion engineering for solid-propellant burn rate and ablation rate data, and in food-process engineering for spray drying and freeze-drying mass-transfer calculations. 1 g/(m²·s) = 3 600 g/(m²·h).

About Pounds per square foot per minute

The pound per square foot per minute (lb/(ft²·min)) equals 0.45359237/(0.09290304 × 60) ≈ 0.08137 kg/(m²·s). It appears in US filtration and membrane technology standards, where filter-press and belt-press dewatering rates are stated per square foot per minute. 1 lb/(ft²·min) ≈ 81.37 g/(m²·s).
