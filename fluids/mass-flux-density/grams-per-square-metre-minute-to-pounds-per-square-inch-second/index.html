About Grams per square metre per minute

The gram per square metre per minute (g/(m²·min)) equals 10⁻³/60 ≈ 1.667 × 10⁻⁵ kg/(m²·s). It is used in spray-coating and inkjet-printing research where deposition rates are measured per pass per unit area. 1 g/(m²·min) = 60 g/(m²·h).

About Pounds per square inch per second

The pound per square inch per second (lb/(in²·s)) equals 0.45359237/(0.0254 × 0.0254) ≈ 703.07 kg/(m²·s). It is used in US firearms ballistics, shaped-charge and explosives engineering, and hydraulic press analysis where forces per square inch dominate the unit system. 1 lb/(in²·s) ≈ 703.07 kg/(m²·s) = 144 lb/(ft²·s).
