About Kilograms per square centimetre per second

The kilogram per square centimetre per second (kg/(cm²·s)) equals 10⁴ kg/(m²·s) (exact: 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m²). It is used for extreme high-pressure mass-flux situations such as detonation physics, shaped-charge jet penetration, and hypervelocity impact analysis. 1 kg/(cm²·s) = 10⁴ kg/(m²·s) = 1 000 g/(cm²·s).

About Milligrams per square metre per minute

The milligram per square metre per minute (mg/(m²·min)) equals 10⁻⁶/60 ≈ 1.667 × 10⁻⁸ kg/(m²·s). It appears in pharmaceutical dissolution testing and transdermal drug-delivery patch characterisation, where drug release rates measured minute-by-minute are compared to in-vivo absorption data. 1 mg/(m²·min) = 60 mg/(m²·h).
