About Micromoles per hour

The micromole per hour (µmol/h) equals 10⁻⁶/3600 ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁰ mol/s, used for photosynthesis gas-exchange measurements, soil respiration CO₂ efflux, and slow metabolic pathway fluxes. Leaf gas analysers (LI-COR, WALZ) report CO₂ assimilation in µmol/h; plant root nutrient uptake studies use µmol/h/g fresh weight. 1 µmol/h ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁰ mol/s.

About Moles per day

The mole per day (mol/d) equals 1/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻⁵ mol/s, used in environmental biogeochemistry, pharmaceutical pharmacokinetics, and slow-rate industrial processes. A deciduous tree assimilates 100-400 mol CO₂/d on a sunny summer day; a resting adult produces ≈1 200 mol CO₂/d through cellular respiration. 1 mol/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻⁵ mol/s ≈ 0.04167 mol/h.
