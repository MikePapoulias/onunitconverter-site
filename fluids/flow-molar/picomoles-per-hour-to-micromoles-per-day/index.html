About Picomoles per hour

The picomole per hour (pmol/h) equals 10⁻¹²/3600 ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁶ mol/s, used in dermal permeability studies and slow-release pharmaceutical patch bioavailability tests. Passive diffusion of steroid hormones through biological membranes is quantified at pmol/h per cm² of membrane area. 1 pmol/h ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁶ mol/s.

About Micromoles per day

The micromole per day (µmol/d) equals 10⁻⁶/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ mol/s, applied in clinical nutrition for micronutrient metabolism and in environmental geochemistry for slow pore-water diffusive fluxes. Iron absorption in the human gut is typically 50-200 µmol/d; zinc urinary excretion is 60-80 µmol/d under normal dietary conditions. 1 µmol/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ mol/s.
