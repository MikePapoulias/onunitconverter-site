About Femtomoles per minute

The femtomole per minute (fmol/min) equals 10⁻¹⁵/60 ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹⁷ mol/s, used in microplate enzyme-kinetics assays where the reaction progress over minutes is tracked at sub-picomolar concentrations. High-sensitivity ELISA assays for cytokines and high-affinity receptor-binding studies report rates at fmol/min. 1 fmol/min = 1/60 fmol/s ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹⁷ mol/s.

About Kilomoles per day

The kilomole per day (kmol/d) equals 1000/86400 ≈ 0.011574 mol/s, used for daily material balance and inventory tracking in chemical manufacturing and environmental reporting. A medium-scale industrial electrolysis plant can produce 500-2 000 kmol Cl₂/d; industrial CO₂ capture units track absorption throughput in kmol/d. 1 kmol/d ≈ 0.011574 mol/s ≈ 0.4167 kmol/h.
