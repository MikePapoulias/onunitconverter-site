Conversion Formula

About Micromoles per hour

The micromole per hour (µmol/h) equals 10⁻⁶/3600 ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁰ mol/s, used for photosynthesis gas-exchange measurements, soil respiration CO₂ efflux, and slow metabolic pathway fluxes. Leaf gas analysers (LI-COR, WALZ) report CO₂ assimilation in µmol/h; plant root nutrient uptake studies use µmol/h/g fresh weight. 1 µmol/h ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁰ mol/s.

About Picomoles per second

The picomole per second (pmol/s) equals 10⁻¹² mol/s, the scale of neurotransmitter release at individual synapses and elution peaks in capillary electrophoresis. Cardiac troponin release from ischaemic myocytes is estimated at 1-10 pmol/s per cell; sub-microlitre continuous-flow microreactors in drug-discovery automation operate at this throughput. 1 pmol/s = 10⁻¹² mol/s = 1000 fmol/s.
