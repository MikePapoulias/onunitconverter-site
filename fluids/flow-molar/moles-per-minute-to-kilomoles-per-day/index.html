Conversion Formula

kmol/d = mol/min × 1.44

About Moles per minute

The mole per minute (mol/min) equals 1/60 mol/s ≈ 0.01667 mol/s, used in process development when studying batch-to-continuous reaction scale-up. A semi-batch neutralisation reactor discharging at 60 mmol/s corresponds to 3.6 mol/min of acid added; microwave-assisted flow reactors for fine-chemical synthesis operate at similar rates. 1 mol/min = 1/60 mol/s = 60 mol/h.

About Kilomoles per day

The kilomole per day (kmol/d) equals 1000/86400 ≈ 0.011574 mol/s, used for daily material balance and inventory tracking in chemical manufacturing and environmental reporting. A medium-scale industrial electrolysis plant can produce 500-2 000 kmol Cl₂/d; industrial CO₂ capture units track absorption throughput in kmol/d. 1 kmol/d ≈ 0.011574 mol/s ≈ 0.4167 kmol/h.
