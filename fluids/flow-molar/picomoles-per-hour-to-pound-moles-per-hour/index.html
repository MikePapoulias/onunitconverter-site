About Picomoles per hour

The picomole per hour (pmol/h) equals 10⁻¹²/3600 ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁶ mol/s, used in dermal permeability studies and slow-release pharmaceutical patch bioavailability tests. Passive diffusion of steroid hormones through biological membranes is quantified at pmol/h per cm² of membrane area. 1 pmol/h ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹⁶ mol/s.

About Pound-moles per hour

The pound-mole per hour (lb-mol/h) equals 453.59237/3600 ≈ 0.12600 mol/s, the standard molar flow unit in US chemical process engineering simulation (Aspen Plus, HYSYS, Pro/II). Distillation column feed rates, reactor effluent streams, and compressor suction flows are universally expressed in lb-mol/h in US petrochemical and refinery design. 1 lb-mol/h = 453.59237/3600 mol/s ≈ 0.12600 mol/s ≈ 0.4536 kmol/h.
