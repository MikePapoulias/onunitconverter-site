About Pound-moles per day

The pound-mole per day (lb-mol/d) equals 453.59237/86400 ≈ 5.249×10⁻³ mol/s, used for daily material-balance reconciliation in US petroleum refineries and chemical plants. Process computer logs accumulate flow integrals as lb-mol/d; pipeline natural-gas custody-transfer accounting also uses lb-mol/d figures in US contracts. 1 lb-mol/d ≈ 5.249×10⁻³ mol/s = 453.59 mol/d.

About Nanomoles per day

The nanomole per day (nmol/d) equals 10⁻⁹/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹⁴ mol/s, applied in clinical pharmacokinetics for very-low-dose drug clearance and in environmental geochemistry for slow pore-water diffusive fluxes. Renal excretion of certain steroid metabolites and trace-element urinary clearance in clinical endocrinology are reported in nmol/d. 1 nmol/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹⁴ mol/s.
