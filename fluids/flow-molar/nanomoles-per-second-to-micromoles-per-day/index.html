Conversion Formula

µmol/d = nmol/s × 86.4

About Nanomoles per second

The nanomole per second (nmol/s) equals 10⁻⁹ mol/s, the scale of moderate laboratory flow reactions and microreactors in pharmaceutical synthesis. A micro-HPLC column handling a 1 μM analyte at 1 mL/min delivers ≈16.7 nmol/s; continuous-flow photochemistry reactors in API synthesis routinely reach 10-100 nmol/s throughput. 1 nmol/s = 10⁻⁹ mol/s = 60 nmol/min.

About Micromoles per day

The micromole per day (µmol/d) equals 10⁻⁶/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ mol/s, applied in clinical nutrition for micronutrient metabolism and in environmental geochemistry for slow pore-water diffusive fluxes. Iron absorption in the human gut is typically 50-200 µmol/d; zinc urinary excretion is 60-80 µmol/d under normal dietary conditions. 1 µmol/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ mol/s.
