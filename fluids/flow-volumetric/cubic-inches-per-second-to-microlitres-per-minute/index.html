About Cubic inches per second

The cubic inch per second (in³/s) equals 1.6387064×10⁻⁵ m³/s, used in US automotive engineering for engine displacement sweep rate, fuel-injector calibration, and hydraulic actuator speed specifications. An engine injector delivering 15 in³/s of fuel corresponds to roughly 15.5 mL/s; hydraulic jacks in US equipment are rated in in³/s at operating pressure. 1 in³/s ≈ 1.639×10⁻⁵ m³/s = 0.016387 L/s.

About Microlitres per minute

The microlitre per minute (µL/min) equals 10⁻⁹/60 ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ m³/s, standard in nano-HPLC chromatography and microfluidic chip pumping. A typical nano-LC column runs at 200-400 µL/min; piezoelectric micro-dosing systems for pharmaceutical assays dispense at 1-50 µL/min. 1 µL/min = 1/60 µL/s ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ m³/s.
