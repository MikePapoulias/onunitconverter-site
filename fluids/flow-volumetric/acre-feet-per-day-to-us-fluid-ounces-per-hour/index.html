About Acre-feet per day

The acre-foot per day (ac-ft/d) equals 1233.48183754752/86400 ≈ 0.014278 m³/s, the practical unit for daily water-delivery scheduling in western US irrigation districts and river-compact administration. Water rights in the Colorado River system are specified in acre-feet per year; daily delivery schedules convert to ac-ft/d. A moderate-size irrigation lateral delivers 5-50 ac-ft/d. 1 ac-ft/d ≈ 0.014278 m³/s ≈ 504.2 US gal/min.

About US fluid ounces per hour

The US fluid ounce per hour (fl oz/h) equals 3.785411784×10⁻³/128/3600 ≈ 8.215×10⁻⁹ m³/s, used in low-flow medical devices and slow-infusion clinical equipment. A nasal cannula oxygen humidifier delivers humidity at 10-50 fl oz/h of water vapour equivalent; micro-dosing chemical feed pumps for water treatment are calibrated in fl oz/h in US systems. 1 fl oz/h ≈ 8.215×10⁻⁹ m³/s.
