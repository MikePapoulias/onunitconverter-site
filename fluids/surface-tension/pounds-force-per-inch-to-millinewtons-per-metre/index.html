About Pounds-force per inch

The pound-force per inch (lbf/in) equals (0.45359237 × 9.80665) / 0.0254 ≈ 175.127 N/m, used in US industrial specifications for adhesive peel strength, hot-melt bond lines, and surface tension of molten metals. 1 lbf/in = 12 lbf/ft ≈ 175.127 N/m ≈ 175 127 mN/m.

About Millinewtons per metre

The millinewton per metre (mN/m) equals 10⁻³ N/m, the most widely used practical unit for liquid surface tension worldwide. Water at 20 °C: 72.8 mN/m; ethanol: 22.1 mN/m; mercury: 485 mN/m; olive oil: ~32 mN/m. Numerically identical to dyn/cm, erg/cm² and mJ/m². 1 mN/m = 10⁻³ N/m.
