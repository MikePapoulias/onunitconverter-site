About Millijoules per square metre

The millijoule per square metre (mJ/m²) equals 10⁻³ N/m - the SI surface-energy-per-area form, numerically identical to mN/m and dyn/cm. The preferred notation in materials science for solid surface energy and work of adhesion: polypropylene ~30 mJ/m², epoxy ~45 mJ/m², steel ~2 000 mJ/m². 1 mJ/m² = 1 mN/m = 1 dyn/cm.

About Pounds-force per foot

The pound-force per foot (lbf/ft) equals (0.45359237 × 9.80665) / 0.3048 ≈ 14.5939 N/m, used in US reservoir engineering and geotechnical drainage for capillary pressure and interfacial tension data. Drilling muds and reservoir simulation codes (Eclipse, CMG) sometimes report in lbf/ft. 1 lbf/ft ≈ 14.594 N/m ≈ 14 594 mN/m.
