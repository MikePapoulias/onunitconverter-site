Conversion Formula

mg/d = µg/min × 1.44

About Micrograms per minute

The microgram per minute (µg/min) equals 10⁻⁹/60 ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ kg/s, used in capillary electrophoresis and gas chromatography mass flow calibration. A typical cold-trap atmospheric sampler collecting particulate matter draws air at a rate characterized in µg/min of PM2.5. 1 µg/min = 1/60 µg/s ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ kg/s.

About Milligrams per day

The milligram per day (mg/d) equals 10⁻⁶/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s, used in toxicology (daily intake limits) and in slow-release pharmaceutical implants. WHO acceptable daily intake for food additives is expressed in mg/kg body mass per day; a 70 kg adult at 1 mg/kg/d represents a 70 mg/d dose. 1 mg/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s.
