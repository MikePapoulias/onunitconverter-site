About Short hundredweights per hour

The short hundredweight per hour (cwt/h, US) equals 45.359237/3600 ≈ 0.012600 kg/s. The US short hundredweight (100 lb exactly) is used in grain elevator and feed-mill billing sheets, cotton-gin output metering, and livestock-feed blending operations. A small feed mill might process 100-500 short cwt/h of mixed grain. 1 short cwt/h ≈ 0.012600 kg/s = 100 lb/h.

About Micrograms per hour

The microgram per hour (µg/h) equals 10⁻⁹/3600 ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹³ kg/s, used in corrosion science to quantify mass loss rates of thin films or passive oxide layers. A stainless steel specimen losing 1 µg/h represents an extremely low corrosion rate typical of passive surfaces in neutral aqueous environments. 1 µg/h = 1/3600 µg/s ≈ 2.778×10⁻¹³ kg/s.
