About Micrograms per second

The microgram per second (µg/s) equals 10⁻⁹ kg/s, the working unit for ultra-trace flow measurements. HPLC pumps delivering isotopic tracers, or nanoliter dispense systems in drug-discovery robotics, operate at µg/s throughput. A 1 µl/min water stream at 1 g/cm³ corresponds to ≈16.7 µg/s. 1 µg/s = 10⁻⁹ kg/s = 60 µg/min.

About Micrograms per minute

The microgram per minute (µg/min) equals 10⁻⁹/60 ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ kg/s, used in capillary electrophoresis and gas chromatography mass flow calibration. A typical cold-trap atmospheric sampler collecting particulate matter draws air at a rate characterized in µg/min of PM2.5. 1 µg/min = 1/60 µg/s ≈ 1.667×10⁻¹¹ kg/s.
