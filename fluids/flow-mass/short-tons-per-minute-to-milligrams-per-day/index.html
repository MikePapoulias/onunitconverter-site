About Short tons per minute

The short ton per minute (ton/min, US) equals 907.18474/60 ≈ 15.120 kg/s, typical for high-capacity conveyor belts and bucket-wheel excavators in open-cast mining. A large surface coal mine loads unit trains with overland conveyors rated at 5 000-15 000 short ton/h = 83-250 ton/min. 1 short ton/min ≈ 15.120 kg/s = 2 000 lb/min.

About Milligrams per day

The milligram per day (mg/d) equals 10⁻⁶/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s, used in toxicology (daily intake limits) and in slow-release pharmaceutical implants. WHO acceptable daily intake for food additives is expressed in mg/kg body mass per day; a 70 kg adult at 1 mg/kg/d represents a 70 mg/d dose. 1 mg/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s.
