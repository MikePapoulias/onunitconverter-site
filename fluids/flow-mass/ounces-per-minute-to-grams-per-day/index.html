About Ounces per minute

The ounce per minute (oz/min) equals 0.028349523125/60 ≈ 4.725×10⁻⁴ kg/s, used in US beverage dispensing (draft beer taps, fountain-drink machines) and food-service portion control. A properly set draft beer faucet pours at ≈16-20 oz/min; a commercial coffee brewer delivers ≈12-16 oz/min of brew. 1 oz/min ≈ 4.725×10⁻⁴ kg/s = 1/16 lb/min.

About Grams per day

The gram per day (g/d) equals 10⁻³/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻⁸ kg/s, applied in nutritional biochemistry, pharmacology, and low-rate environmental monitoring. Human daily protein synthesis rates are in the range of 250-300 g/d; WHO recommended dietary iron intake is 8-18 mg/d; refrigerant leakage regulations cap systems at a few g/d. 1 g/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻⁸ kg/s.
