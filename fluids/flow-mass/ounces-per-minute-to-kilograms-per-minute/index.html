About Ounces per minute

The ounce per minute (oz/min) equals 0.028349523125/60 ≈ 4.725×10⁻⁴ kg/s, used in US beverage dispensing (draft beer taps, fountain-drink machines) and food-service portion control. A properly set draft beer faucet pours at ≈16-20 oz/min; a commercial coffee brewer delivers ≈12-16 oz/min of brew. 1 oz/min ≈ 4.725×10⁻⁴ kg/s = 1/16 lb/min.

About Kilograms per minute

The kilogram per minute (kg/min) equals 1/60 kg/s ≈ 0.01667 kg/s, convenient for medium-scale industrial processes and chemical reactors. A cement transit-mixer discharges ready-mix at ≈200-400 kg/min; a grain auger on a combine harvester typically moves 300-600 kg/min; an industrial coffee roaster cycles 120-180 kg/min of beans through the drum. 1 kg/min = 1/60 kg/s = 60 kg/h.
