About Ounces per day

The ounce per day (oz/d) equals 0.028349523125/86400 ≈ 3.281×10⁻⁷ kg/s, used in US clinical nutrition (tube-feeding rates, oral-supplement intake) and very-low flow industrial metering. A patient receiving 6 oz of enteral formula per hour (via a drip-tube) receives ≈144 oz/d. 1 oz/d ≈ 3.281×10⁻⁷ kg/s.

About Milligrams per minute

The milligram per minute (mg/min) equals 10⁻⁶/60 ≈ 1.667×10⁻⁸ kg/s, the preferred unit for laboratory peristaltic pump output and aerosol generator calibration. Metered-dose inhalers deliver 100-500 µg of active compound per actuation; at 1 actuation per minute that is 0.1-0.5 mg/min. 1 mg/min = 1/60 mg/s ≈ 1.667×10⁻⁸ kg/s.
