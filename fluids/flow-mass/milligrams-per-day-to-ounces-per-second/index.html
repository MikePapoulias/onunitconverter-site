About Milligrams per day

The milligram per day (mg/d) equals 10⁻⁶/86400 ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s, used in toxicology (daily intake limits) and in slow-release pharmaceutical implants. WHO acceptable daily intake for food additives is expressed in mg/kg body mass per day; a 70 kg adult at 1 mg/kg/d represents a 70 mg/d dose. 1 mg/d ≈ 1.157×10⁻¹¹ kg/s.

About Ounces per second

The ounce per second (oz/s) equals 0.028349523125 kg/s, a practical US unit for moderate liquid or solid flow measurements. A garden hose delivering 1.5 US gal/min discharges water at roughly 2.1 oz/s; a small popcorn machine pops roughly 0.5-1 oz/s of popped corn at full rate. 1 oz/s = 0.028349523125 kg/s ≈ 0.0625 lb/s.
