About Ounces per cubic inch

The ounce per cubic inch (oz/in³) equals ≈1729.994 kg/m³, a US customary unit for dense compact materials. Precision ballistic-projectile and machined-component datasheets give density in oz/in³: structural steel ≈4.51 oz/in³, lead ≈6.55 oz/in³, gold ≈11.16 oz/in³. Also found in explosive loading-density specifications. 1 oz/in³ = (1/16) lb/in³ ≈108.12 lb/ft³ ≈1729.994 kg/m³.

About Ounces per US gallon

The ounce per US gallon (oz/gal US) equals ≈7.489 kg/m³, combining the avoirdupois ounce (28.349 g) and the US liquid gallon (3.785 L). Used in US brewing and beverage production for measuring dissolved solids in wort and syrup; homebrewing grain-to-water ratios are often cited in oz/gal. 1 oz/gal (US) ≈7.489 kg/m³ = (1/16) lb/gal (US).
