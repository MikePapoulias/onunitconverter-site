About Micrograms per litre

The microgram per litre (µg/L) equals 10⁻⁶ kg/m³, numerically equal to mg/m³, and the standard unit for trace-level water quality. EPA maximum contaminant level for arsenic is 10 µg/L; WHO guideline for lead in drinking water is 10 µg/L; pharmaceutical water-for-injection requires total organic carbon below 500 µg/L. 1 µg/L = 10⁻⁶ kg/m³ = 1 mg/m³ = 1 ppb (mass) in dilute aqueous solutions.

About Ounces per US gallon

The ounce per US gallon (oz/gal US) equals ≈7.489 kg/m³, combining the avoirdupois ounce (28.349 g) and the US liquid gallon (3.785 L). Used in US brewing and beverage production for measuring dissolved solids in wort and syrup; homebrewing grain-to-water ratios are often cited in oz/gal. 1 oz/gal (US) ≈7.489 kg/m³ = (1/16) lb/gal (US).
