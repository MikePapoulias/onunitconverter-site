About Kilograms per cubic metre

The kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³) is the SI coherent derived unit of density. Air at standard sea level is ≈1.225 kg/m³; fresh water at 4°C is ≈999.97 kg/m³; liquid mercury is 13 534 kg/m³; osmium (densest stable element) is 22 590 kg/m³. Used in the continuity equation, Bernoulli's principle, and all SI-based fluid dynamics calculations. 1 kg/m³ = 1 g/L ≈0.06243 lb/ft³.

About Micrograms per cubic metre

The microgram per cubic metre (µg/m³) equals 10⁻⁹ kg/m³, the standard unit for ambient air quality reporting. WHO 2021 guidelines set annual mean limits for PM2.5 at 5 µg/m³ and PM10 at 15 µg/m³; NIOSH REL for nuisance dust is 10 000 µg/m³. Urban background air typically contains 10-50 µg/m³ of total suspended particles. 1 µg/m³ = 10⁻⁹ kg/m³ = 10⁻⁶ g/m³.
