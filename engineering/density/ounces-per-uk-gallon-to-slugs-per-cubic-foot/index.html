About Ounces per UK gallon

The ounce per UK gallon (oz/gal UK) equals ≈6.236 kg/m³, based on the avoirdupois ounce (28.349 g) and the UK gallon (4.54609 L). Used historically in British brewing, food manufacturing, and naval fuel specifications before metric adoption. Legacy recipes and Admiralty tables still express density in oz/gal UK. 1 oz/gal (UK) ≈6.236 kg/m³ = (1/16) lb/gal (UK).

About Slugs per cubic foot

The slug per cubic foot (slug/ft³) equals ≈515.379 kg/m³, used in US customary dynamics where forces are in pound-force. The US Standard Atmosphere gives sea-level air density as 2.377 × 10⁻³ slug/ft³; it appears in pre-SI aerospace textbooks (NACA technical notes) and compressible flow equations. 1 slug ≈14.594 kg. 1 slug/ft³ ≈515.379 kg/m³ ≈32.174 lb/ft³.
