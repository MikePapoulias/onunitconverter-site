About Milligrams per cubic metre

The milligram per cubic metre (mg/m³) equals 10⁻⁶ kg/m³, the reference unit for industrial occupational exposure limits (OELs). NIOSH REL for respirable crystalline silica is 0.05 mg/m³; OSHA PEL for Portland cement 15 mg/m³; ACGIH TLV for calcium carbonate dust 10 mg/m³. Numerically equal to µg/L, bridging airborne and aqueous analysis. 1 mg/m³ = 10⁻⁶ kg/m³ = 1 µg/L.

About Tonnes per cubic metre

The tonne per cubic metre (t/m³) equals 1000 kg/m³, coincident with g/cm³ and kg/L. Geotechnical engineering specifies bulk unit weights in t/m³: saturated clay 1.8-2.2 t/m³, dry sand 1.4-1.7 t/m³, reinforced concrete 2.4-2.5 t/m³. Bulk-material handling uses t/m³ for conveyor and silo capacity calculations, yielding mass in tonnes directly from volume in m³. 1 t/m³ = 1000 kg/m³ = 1 g/cm³.
