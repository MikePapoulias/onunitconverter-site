About Kilometres per hour per second

The kilometre per hour per second (km/h/s) equals 1/3.6 m/s² ≈ 0.2778 m/s², the most intuitive acceleration unit for vehicle performance in countries using km/h. A 0-100 km/h time of 6.3 s (VW Golf GTI) gives a mean acceleration of 15.9 km/h/s ≈ 4.4 m/s²; a 2.6 s EV sprint corresponds to 38.5 km/h/s ≈ 10.7 m/s². Railway standards (EU TSI, JIS) use km/h/s: high-speed trains launch at 2.5-4 km/h/s; emergency braking must reach at least 9.0 km/h/s. 1 km/h/s = 1/3.6 m/s² ≈ 2.83 × 10⁻² g₀.

About Knots per second

The knot per second (kn/s) equals 1852/3600 m/s² ≈ 0.5144 m/s², used in aviation and maritime operations where speed is measured in knots. Aircraft carrier catapult launches accelerate an F/A-18 from 0 to ~150 kn in 2-2.5 s, giving 60-75 kn/s ≈ 31-39 m/s². A commercial jet rejected takeoff from V1 (~145 kn) to rest in ≈25 s decelerates at 5-6 kn/s ≈ 2.6-3.1 m/s². 1 kn/s = 463/900 m/s² ≈ 5.25 × 10⁻² g₀.
