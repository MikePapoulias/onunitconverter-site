About Gram-force millimetres

The gram-force millimetre (gf·mm) equals 9.80665 × 10⁻⁶ N·m, the natural unit for precision micro-mechanisms. Swiss watchmaking datasheets list hairspring and balance-wheel detent torques in gf·mm; micro-servo holding torques, precision force gauge spring calibration, and miniature dental handpiece torque limits also fall in this range. A typical watch hairspring torque is 0.1-5 gf·mm. 1 gf·mm = 9.80665 × 10⁻⁶ N·m = 100 dyn·cm = 0.1 gf·cm.

About Meganewton metres

The meganewton metre (MN·m) equals 10⁶ N·m, the unit for the largest rotating machines and extreme structural loads. Offshore wind turbine tower-base bending moments reach 300-800 MN·m in storm conditions; large hydro-generator shaft torques range 100-500 MN·m; ocean-going vessel propulsion crankshaft peak torques 10-100 MN·m. 1 MN·m = 1000 kN·m ≈ 737 562 lbf·ft ≈ 737.6 kip·ft.
