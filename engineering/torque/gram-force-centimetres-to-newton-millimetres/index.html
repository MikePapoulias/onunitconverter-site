Conversion Formula

N·mm = gf·cm × 0.0980665

About Gram-force centimetres

The gram-force centimetre (gf·cm) equals 9.80665 × 10⁻⁵ N·m, the standard unit for small servo and hobby motor specifications. RC servo motor stall torques are quoted in gf·cm - a typical 9 g mini-servo delivers 1500-2000 gf·cm. Small stepper motors for 3D printers range 400-600 gf·cm; model train gearboxes around 200-800 gf·cm. 1 gf·cm = 9.80665 × 10⁻⁵ N·m = 10 gf·mm = 0.01 kgf·cm.

About Newton millimetres

The newton millimetre (N·mm) equals 10⁻³ N·m, widely used in mechanical engineering and FEA software. ANSYS, Abaqus, and Siemens NX models built in millimetres report torques in N·mm for dimensional consistency. Fastener torque for M3-M6 screws (typically 0.3-5 N·m range), micro-pump impeller loads, and precision gearbox calibration values are expressed in N·mm. 1 N·mm = 10⁻³ N·m = 0.1 N·cm ≈ 8.851 × 10⁻³ lbf·in.
