About Cubic metres per kilogram

The cubic metre per kilogram (m³/kg) is the SI coherent derived unit of specific volume - the reciprocal of density. In steam and refrigerant thermodynamics, m³/kg appears in state tables: water at 100°C, 0.1 MPa has specific volume 1.674 m³/kg (vapour) and 1.044 × 10⁻³ m³/kg (liquid). The specific gas constant and molar volume calculations use m³/kg. 1 m³/kg = 1000 L/kg = 1000 cm³/g.

About Millilitres per gram

The millilitre per gram (mL/g) equals 10⁻³ m³/kg, coincidentally equal to cm³/g and L/kg (1 mL = 1 cm³ exactly). Used in pharmaceutical and cosmetic formulations: specific volume of a cream or gel is measured in mL/g with a pycnometer. Intrinsic viscosity data and sedimentation analysis also report volume in mL/g. 1 mL/g = 10⁻³ m³/kg = 1 cm³/g = 1 L/kg.
