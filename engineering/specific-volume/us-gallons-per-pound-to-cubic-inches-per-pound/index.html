About US gallons per pound

The US gallon per pound (gal (US)/lb) equals 0.003785411784 / 0.45359237 ≈0.0083454 m³/kg, used in American chemical process engineering. Specific volume of cooling water and process fluids is cited in gal (US)/lb in pre-metric Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook tables. 1 gal (US)/lb ≈0.0083454 m³/kg ≈8.345 L/kg.

About Cubic inches per pound

The cubic inch per pound (in³/lb) equals 1.6387064 × 10⁻⁵ / 0.45359237 ≈ 3.6127 × 10⁻⁵ m³/kg. US aerospace propellant tables and legacy NACA compressible-flow charts list gas specific volumes in in³/lb; pre-metric Mollier charts for steam plotted enthalpy vs. in³/lb. 1 in³/lb ≈3.6127 × 10⁻⁵ m³/kg ≈0.03613 cm³/g.
