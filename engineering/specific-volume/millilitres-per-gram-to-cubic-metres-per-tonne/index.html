About Millilitres per gram

The millilitre per gram (mL/g) equals 10⁻³ m³/kg, coincidentally equal to cm³/g and L/kg (1 mL = 1 cm³ exactly). Used in pharmaceutical and cosmetic formulations: specific volume of a cream or gel is measured in mL/g with a pycnometer. Intrinsic viscosity data and sedimentation analysis also report volume in mL/g. 1 mL/g = 10⁻³ m³/kg = 1 cm³/g = 1 L/kg.

About Cubic metres per tonne

The cubic metre per tonne (m³/t) equals 10⁻³ m³/kg, coincidentally equal to L/kg and cm³/g, preferred in bulk-material handling, geotechnical engineering, and quarrying. Swell factor tables for excavated rock list specific volume in m³/t: solid granite ≈0.37 m³/t, blasted rock ≈0.45 m³/t. International shipping designates cargo stowage factors in m³/t. 1 m³/t = 10⁻³ m³/kg = 1 L/kg.
