About Cubic centimetres per gram

The cubic centimetre per gram (cm³/g) equals 10⁻³ m³/kg, numerically identical to mL/g and L/kg. In thermodynamics, steam tables traditionally list specific volume in cm³/g: saturated steam at 100°C = 1671 cm³/g; water at 20°C = 1.0018 cm³/g. Polymer melt flow and food-science density data sheets also use cm³/g. 1 cm³/g = 10⁻³ m³/kg = 1 mL/g = 1 L/kg.

About Cubic feet per slug

The cubic foot per slug (ft³/slug) equals 0.028316846592 / (0.45359237 × 9.80665 / 0.3048) ≈1.940 × 10⁻³ m³/kg, the natural specific-volume unit in US customary aerodynamics where mass is in slugs. The USAF Standard Atmosphere and pre-SI textbooks (Anderson, Shevell) give air specific volume in ft³/slug. 1 ft³/slug ≈1.940 × 10⁻³ m³/kg.
