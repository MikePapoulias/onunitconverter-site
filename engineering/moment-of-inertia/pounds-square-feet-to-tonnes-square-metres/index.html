About Pound square feet

The pound square foot (lb·ft²) equals ≈0.04214 kg·m², the dominant imperial unit for machine and vehicle rotational inertias. Aircraft control-surface counterbalancing, propeller blade inertia, and turbocharger rotor dynamics are specified in lb·ft²; steam turbine discs and helicopter tail-rotor assemblies: 1-10 000 lb·ft². 1 lb·ft² ≈0.04214 kg·m² = 144 lb·in² ≈0.03108 slug·ft².

About Tonne square metres

The tonne square metre (t·m²) equals 1000 kg·m², used for large industrial rotors and heavy rotating machinery. A 60-tonne paper-machine calendar roll of 0.5 m radius has I ≈7.5 t·m²; synchronous hydro-generator rotors: 50-2000 t·m²; large marine propulsion shafts: 5-50 t·m². 1 t·m² = 1000 kg·m² ≈737.6 slug·ft² ≈23 730 lb·ft².
