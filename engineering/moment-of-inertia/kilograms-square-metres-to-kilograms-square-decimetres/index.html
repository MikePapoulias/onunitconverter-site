About Kilogram square metres

The kilogram square metre (kg·m²) is the SI coherent derived unit of moment of inertia. It defines the rotational analogue of mass in Newton's second law: τ = Iα (torque in N·m, angular acceleration in rad/s²). A solid disc of 10 kg and 0.5 m radius has I = 1.25 kg·m². Electric motor rotors: 0.001-10 kg·m². Wind turbine rotors: 10⁶-10⁸ kg·m². 1 kg·m² = 10 000 kg·cm² ≈0.7376 slug·ft².

About Kilogram square decimetres

The kilogram square decimetre (kg·dm²) equals 10⁻² kg·m², occasionally used in SI-metric flywheel and rotor analysis when the decimetre is a convenient length scale. Lathe chuck jaws, grinding wheel flanges, and small centrifuges fall in the 0.1-10 kg·dm² range. 1 kg·dm² = 10⁻² kg·m² = 100 kg·cm² = 10 g·m².
