About Revolutions per hour

The revolution per hour (rev/h) equals π/1800 rad/s ≈ 1.745 × 10⁻³ rad/s, used for very slow rotating machinery. Cement rotary kilns - the world's longest continuously rotating machines (up to 230 m, 4000 t) - rotate at 1-5 rev/h; ball mills for ore grinding at 10-20 rev/h; concrete mixer trucks at 1-4 rev/h during transport. Large astronomical antenna drives and slow-scan telescope azimuth movements are also conveniently expressed in rev/h. 1 rev/h = π/1800 rad/s ≈ 0.02778 rpm.

About Degrees per minute

The degree per minute (°/min) equals π/10800 rad/s ≈ 2.909 × 10⁻⁴ rad/s. The sidereal tracking rate for astronomical telescopes is 0.25 °/min (15 °/h); modern robotic observatories slew at 100-400 °/min between targets. A clock's hour hand turns at 0.5 °/min; the minute hand at 6 °/min. Industrial drum conveyors for cement kilns rotate at 0.5-5 °/min; automotive power-steering columns during parking manoeuvres rotate at 30-180 °/min. 1 °/min ≈ 2.909 × 10⁻⁴ rad/s = 1/60 rpm.
