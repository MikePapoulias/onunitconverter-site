About Apostilbs

The apostilb (asb) equals 1/π cd/m² ≈ 0.3183 cd/m², defined as the luminance of a perfectly diffuse Lambertian surface with an exitance of 1 lm/m². Introduced by the CIE in 1940 and used in European photometry standards and retinal illuminance calculations. 1 asb = 1/π cd/m² ≈ 0.3183 cd/m².

About Millicandelas per square metre

The millicandela per square metre (mcd/m²) equals 10⁻³ cd/m². The luminance of a moonless night sky is ~10⁻³ cd/m²; a quarter-moon sky ~10⁻² cd/m². OLED screens in deep-black mode and unlit LCD panels measure a few mcd/m². Also the range for very dim indicator lamps. 1 mcd/m² = 10⁻³ cd/m².
