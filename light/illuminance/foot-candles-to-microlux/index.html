About Foot-candles

The foot-candle (fc) equals 1 lm/ft² ≈ 10.764 lx. Standard in North American lighting design: IESNA recommends 30-50 fc for general offices, 50-100 fc for retail displays, 12-16 fc for cinema screens. Still the dominant unit in the US, Canada and some other countries. 1 fc = 1/0.09290304 lx.

About Microlux

The microlux (µlx) equals 10⁻⁶ lx. Natural night illuminance: a moonless overcast sky ~100 µlx; scattered starlight ~200-300 µlx; crescent moon ~10-50 µlx. Used in night-vision equipment sensitivity specifications and observatory site-quality assessments. 1 µlx = 10⁻⁶ lx.
