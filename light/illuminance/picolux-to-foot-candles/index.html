About Picolux

The picolux (plx) equals 10⁻¹² lx. A theoretical subunit used only in precision photometric standard traceability. Even the faintest measurable natural illuminance - starlight on a moonless night at ~200 nlx - is 200 billion times brighter. Appears only in SI reference chains for calibration laboratories. 1 plx = 10⁻¹² lx.

About Foot-candles

The foot-candle (fc) equals 1 lm/ft² ≈ 10.764 lx. Standard in North American lighting design: IESNA recommends 30-50 fc for general offices, 50-100 fc for retail displays, 12-16 fc for cinema screens. Still the dominant unit in the US, Canada and some other countries. 1 fc = 1/0.09290304 lx.
