About Microlux

The microlux (µlx) equals 10⁻⁶ lx. Natural night illuminance: a moonless overcast sky ~100 µlx; scattered starlight ~200-300 µlx; crescent moon ~10-50 µlx. Used in night-vision equipment sensitivity specifications and observatory site-quality assessments. 1 µlx = 10⁻⁶ lx.

About Phots

The phot (ph) equals 1 lm/cm² = 10 000 lx. The CGS-photometric unit of illuminance. Pre-SI optics texts express daylight in phots: full tropical sunshine is ~10 ph (100 klx); overcast sky ~0.1-0.2 ph. Wholly superseded by lux and kilolux in modern standards. 1 ph = 10⁴ lx.
