About Foot-candles

The foot-candle (fc) equals 1 lm/ft² ≈ 10.764 lx. Standard in North American lighting design: IESNA recommends 30-50 fc for general offices, 50-100 fc for retail displays, 12-16 fc for cinema screens. Still the dominant unit in the US, Canada and some other countries. 1 fc = 1/0.09290304 lx.

About Megalux

The megalux (Mlx) equals 10⁶ lx. Reached only by extreme short-range sources: a short-arc xenon lamp at 5 cm produces ~1-10 Mlx; pulsed photographic strobes at close range several Mlx. The full solar disc on Earth's surface gives ~100 klx - far below 1 Mlx. 1 Mlx = 10⁶ lx.
