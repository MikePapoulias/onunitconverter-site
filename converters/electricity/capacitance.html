Common Capacitance Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 F = 103 mF = 106 µF (SI prefix chain)
    • 
        
  • 1 µF = 10-6 F (standard scale for power supply capacitors)
    • 
        
  • 1 nF = 10-3 µF = 10-9 F (ceramic bypass and ESD protection scale)
    • 
        
  • 1 pF = 10-3 nF = 10-12 F (RF circuits and crystal oscillator loading)
    • 
        
  • 1 fF = 10-15 F (CMOS gate capacitance in nm-scale process nodes)
    • 
        
  • 1 kF = 103 F (large supercapacitor assembly range)
    • 
        
  • 1 statF ≈ 1.113 × 10-12 F (CGS-Gaussian, exact: 105/c² F)
    • 
        
  • 1 abF = 109 F = 1 GF (CGS-EMU abfarad, exact)
