Fluids Converters

Convert between units for fluid flow, mass flow, and related fluid properties.

  • Flow Rate (Mass) Converter - Mass transferred per unit time
  • Flow Rate (Molar) Converter - Molar quantity transferred per unit time
  • Flow Rate (Volumetric) Converter - Volume of fluid transferred per unit time
  • Viscosity (Dynamic) Converter - Resistance of fluid to shear flow
  • Viscosity (Kinematic) Converter - Kinematic viscosity: dynamic viscosity divided by density
  • Mass Flux Density Converter - Mass flow rate per unit area
  • Concentration (Molar) Converter - Amount of substance per unit volume
  • Surface Tension Converter - Force per unit length at fluid interfaces
  • Permeability Converter - Ease of fluid flow through a porous medium
