Common Molar Concentration Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 mol/L (M) = 1 000 mmol/L = 1 000 mol/m³
    • 
        
  • 1 mmol/L = 1 mol/m³ = 10-3 mol/L (SI clinical unit equals SI coherent unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 µmol/L = 10-3 mmol/L = 10-6 mol/L
    • 
        
  • 1 nmol/L = 10-3 µmol/L = 10-9 mol/L
    • 
        
  • 1 pmol/L = 10-3 nmol/L = 10-12 mol/L
    • 
        
  • 1 mol/L = 1 mol/dm³ = 1 kmol/m³ (three notations, identical value)
    • 
        
  • Blood glucose: 5.0 mmol/L = 5.0 mol/m³ = 0.005 mol/L = 90 mg/dL
    • 
        
  • 1 mol/mL = 1 mol/cm³ = 1 000 mol/L = 106 mol/m³
