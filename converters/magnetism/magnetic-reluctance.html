Common Magnetic Reluctance Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 A/Wb = 1 H-1 (magnetic reluctance = inverse inductance for a 1-turn circuit)
    • 
        
  • 1 kA/Wb = 103 A/Wb = 103 H-1
    • 
        
  • 1 MA/Wb = 106 A/Wb (typical SMPS ferrite core reluctance)
    • 
        
  • 1 Gb/Mx ≈ 7.9577 × 107 A/Wb = 109/(4π) A/Wb (CGS unit)
    • 
        
  • N-turn coil with inductance L: ℛ = N2 / L (in A/Wb)
    • 
        
  • Air gap (length l, area A): ℛ = l / (µ0 A) ≈ l / (1.257 × 10-6 A) A/Wb
    • 
        
  • Ferrite E-core (100 kHz SMPS): ℛ ≈ 1-50 MA/Wb
    • 
        
  • 0.1 mm air gap, 1 mm2 cross-section: ℛ ≈ 80 GA/Wb
