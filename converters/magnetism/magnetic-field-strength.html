Common Magnetic Field Strength Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Oe = 1000/(4π) A/m ≈ 79.577 A/m (exact; CGS-Gaussian definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 kOe = 1000/(4π) kA/m ≈ 79.577 kA/m (permanent magnet coercivity scale)
    • 
        
  • 1 A/cm = 100 A/m (metric practical unit used in ferrite datasheets)
    • 
        
  • 1 A/in ≈ 39.370 A/m (US coil winding specification)
    • 
        
  • 1 A/ft ≈ 3.2808 A/m (US engineering unit; 1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly)
    • 
        
  • Earth's geomagnetic field H ≈ 40–60 A/m (≈ 0.5–0.75 Oe at mid-latitudes)
    • 
        
  • NdFeB magnet coercivity Hc ≈ 900–2000 kA/m (≈ 11–25 kOe)
    • 
        
  • MRI 3 T bore: free-space H = B/μ0 = 3/(4π×10-7) ≈ 2387 kA/m
