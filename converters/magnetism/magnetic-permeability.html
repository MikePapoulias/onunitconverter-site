Common Magnetic Permeability Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 H/m = 106 µH/m = 103 mH/m (SI prefix conversions)
    • 
        
  • µ0 = 4π × 10-7 H/m ≈ 1.25664 µH/m (vacuum permeability)
    • 
        
  • 1 H/m ≈ 7.958 × 105 µ0 (expressed in units of µ0)
    • 
        
  • Vacuum / air / most plastics: µr ≈ 1, µ ≈ 1.257 µH/m
    • 
        
  • Paramagnetic Al: µr = 1.0000220, µ ≈ µ0 + 28 nH/m
    • 
        
  • Silicon steel (CRGO): µr ≈ 1 000-10 000, µ ≈ 1.3-13 mH/m
    • 
        
  • Permalloy (Ni78Fe22): µr up to ~100 000, µ up to ~126 mH/m
    • 
        
  • Mu-metal (annealed): µr up to ~300 000, µ up to ~0.38 H/m
    • 
        
  • Supermalloy (optimal): µr up to ~106, µ up to ~1.26 H/m
