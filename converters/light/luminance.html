Common Luminance Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 cd/m² = 1 nit (informal name used in display engineering)
    • 
        
  • 1 fL (foot-lambert) ≈ 3.4263 cd/m² (SMPTE cinema standard: 14 fL ≈ 48 cd/m²)
    • 
        
  • 1 L (lambert) = 104/π cd/m² ≈ 3 183 cd/m² (CGS unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 sb (stilb) = 104 cd/m² = 1 cd/cm² (CGS unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 asb (apostilb) = 1/π cd/m² ≈ 0.3183 cd/m² (CIE 1940)
    • 
        
  • 1 cd/ft² ≈ 10.764 cd/m² (Imperial luminance unit)
    • 
        
  • Typical laptop screen: 250-500 cd/m²
    • 
        
  • HDR display peak: 1 000-4 000 cd/m² (1-4 kcd/m²)
    • 
        
  • Clear blue sky: ~3 000-8 000 cd/m²
    • 
        
  • Solar disc at zenith: ~1.6 × 109 cd/m² (~1 600 Mcd/m²)
