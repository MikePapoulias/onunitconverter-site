Common Illuminance Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 lx = 1 lm/m² (SI definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 fc (foot-candle) = 1 lm/ft² ≈ 10.764 lx
    • 
        
  • 1 ph (phot) = 1 lm/cm² = 10 000 lx (CGS unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 mlx = 1 nox (CIE historical unit; exact equivalence)
    • 
        
  • Moonless clear night sky: ~1 mlx; full moon at zenith: ~100-270 mlx
    • 
        
  • Typical office (EN 12464-1): 300-500 lx
    • 
        
  • Direct mid-latitude sunlight at noon: ~80-100 klx
    • 
        
  • IESNA general office recommendation: 30-50 fc (≈ 320-540 lx)
    • 
        
  • SMPTE cinema screen standard: 12-16 fc (≈ 130-172 lx)
    • 
        
  • Point source inverse-square law: E = I / r² (I in cd, r in m, E in lx)
