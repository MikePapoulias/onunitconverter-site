Common Luminous Intensity Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 cd = 1 lm/sr (SI base unit definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 HK (Hefnerkerze) = 0.920 cd (DIN 5031; German standard 1884-1942)
    • 
        
  • 1 Vl (violle) ≈ 20.17 cd (1 cm² platinum at solidification point)
    • 
        
  • Standard wax candle: ~1 cd; 60 W incandescent bulb: ~60 cd
    • 
        
  • 3 mm through-hole LED: 5-500 mcd at 20 mA
    • 
        
  • High-power LED in 5° beam: ~100 cd per watt
    • 
        
  • Automotive low-beam headlight (beam axis): ~2-3 kcd
    • 
        
  • Large maritime lighthouse: 1-10 Mcd
    • 
        
  • Luminous intensity from flux: I = Φ / Ω (cd = lm / sr)
