Common Luminous Flux Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 lm = 1 cd·sr (SI derived unit definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 W (at 555 nm) = 683 lm (exact - 2019 SI definition; Kcd = 683 lm/W at 540 THz)
    • 
        
  • Standard wax candle: ~12 lm total output
    • 
        
  • 40 W incandescent bulb: ~450 lm; 60 W: ~800 lm; 100 W: ~1 600 lm
    • 
        
  • 800 lm LED lamp (standard 60 W replacement): ~9-10 W input
    • 
        
  • 150 W PAR38 halogen flood: ~2 000 lm (~2 klm)
    • 
        
  • 1 000 W high-pressure sodium street lamp: ~130 000 lm (~130 klm)
    • 
        
  • LED stadium floodlight array (full pitch): 1-10 Mlm
    • 
        
  • Total luminous flux of the Sun: approximately 3.6 × 1028 lm
    • 
        
  • Luminous flux from intensity: Φ = I × Ω (lm = cd × sr)
