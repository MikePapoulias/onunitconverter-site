Common Image Resolution Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 PPI = 1/2.54 px/cm ≈ 0.3937 px/cm = 1/25.4 px/mm ≈ 0.03937 px/mm
    • 
        
  • 1 px/mm = 25.4 PPI  |  1 px/cm = 2.54 PPI  |  1 px/m = 0.0254 PPI
    • 
        
  • 72 PPI = 1 dot/pt (original Macintosh screen density, 1984)
    • 
        
  • 96 PPI = 1 dppx (CSS reference screen density)
    • 
        
  • 300 PPI ≈ 118.11 px/cm ≈ 11.81 px/mm (standard offset print quality, ISO 12647-2)
    • 
        
  • 1 dpcm = 1 px/cm = 2.54 DPI = 2.54 PPI (numerically equal as units)
    • 
        
  • 1 lp/mm = 1 px/mm = 25.4 lpi = 25.4 PPI (same dimension; lp/mm used in optics)
    • 
        
  • 600 PPI ≈ 23.62 px/mm (fine-art Giclée printing minimum)
    • 
        
  • 1 dot/pica = 6 PPI  |  1 dot/pt = 72 PPI  |  1 dppx = 96 PPI
