Common Sound Level Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Np = 20/ln(10) dB ≈ 8.6859 dB ≈ 0.8686 B
    • 
        
  • 1 dB = ln(10)/20 Np ≈ 0.11513 Np = 0.1 B = 10 cB = 100 mB
    • 
        
  • 1 B = 10 dB = ln(10)/2 Np ≈ 1.15129 Np
    • 
        
  • 0 dB SPL = threshold of human hearing in air (reference: 20 µPa)
    • 
        
  • 60 dB SPL = normal conversation at ~1 m distance
    • 
        
  • 85 dB SPL = NIOSH/OSHA 8-hour occupational exposure limit
    • 
        
  • 120 dB SPL = threshold of pain
    • 
        
  • 194 dB SPL = maximum theoretical undistorted sound pressure in air at 1 atm
