SI unit: -

Decimal (SI) vs Binary (IEC): Networking and telecommunications use SI decimal prefixes: 1 kbit/s = 1,000 bit/s, 1 Mbit/s = 1,000,000 bit/s (powers of 10). Some hardware engineering and memory contexts use IEC binary prefixes: 1 Kibit/s = 1,024 bit/s, 1 Mibit/s = 1,048,576 bit/s (powers of 2). ISPs always advertise speeds in SI decimal Mbit/s. Divide by 8 to convert Mbit/s to MB/s. The dropdowns below group units by standard.
