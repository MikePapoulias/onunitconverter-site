Common Heat Flux Density Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 W/m² = 1 J/(s·m²) (exact); solar constant ≈ 1361 W/m²; Earth surface irradiance ≈ 1000 W/m²
    • 
        
  • 1 kW/m² = 1000 W/m² = 0.1 W/cm² (exact); 1 mW/cm² = 10 W/m² (exact)
    • 
        
  • 1 BTU(IT)/(h·ft²) ≈ 3.15459 W/m²; 1 W/m² ≈ 0.31700 BTU(IT)/(h·ft²)
    • 
        
  • 1 kcal(th)/(h·m²) = 4184/3600 ≈ 1.1622 W/m² (exact: 1 kcal(th) = 4184 J)
    • 
        
  • 1 W/cm² = 104 W/m² = 10 kW/m² (exact; 1 cm² = 10-4 m²)
    • 
        
  • 1 cal(th)/(s·cm²) = 41840 W/m² (exact: 1 cal(th) = 4.184 J; 1 cm² = 10-4 m²)
    • 
        
  • 1 erg/(s·cm²) = 10-3 W/m² = 1 mW/m² (exact: 1 erg = 10-7 J; CGS irradiance unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 W/mm² = MW/m² = 106 W/m² (exact); used in high-power laser and fusion engineering
