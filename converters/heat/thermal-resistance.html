Common Thermal Resistance Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 K/W = 1 °C/W (exact: 1 K = 1 °C for temperature intervals)
    • 
        
  • 1 °F/W = 5/9 K/W ≈ 0.5556 K/W; 1 K/W = 9/5 °F/W = 1.8 °F/W
    • 
        
  • 1 h·°F/BTU = (5/9 × 3600 / 1055.05585262) K/W ≈ 1.8956 K/W
    • 
        
  • 1 h·°C/kcal = 3600 / 4186.8 K/W ≈ 0.8598 K/W
    • 
        
  • 1 min·K/W = 60 K/W; 1 h·K/W = 3600 K/W; 1 day·K/W = 86 400 K/W
    • 
        
  • 1 mK/W = 10-3 K/W; 1 kK/W = 1000 K/W
    • 
        
  • CPU heatsink: ≈ 0.1-1 K/W; Thermal paste layer: ≈ 0.01-0.1 K/W
    • 
        
  • IC junction-to-case (RθJC): typically 0.1-10 °C/W; junction-to-ambient (RθJA): 1-100 °C/W
