Heat Converters

Converters for heat transfer, thermodynamic properties, material thermal characteristics, and related quantities.

  • Fuel Efficiency Converter (Mass) - Distance per unit fuel mass
  • Thermal Diffusivity Converter - Rate of heat diffusion through materials
  • Thermal Expansion Converter - Linear thermal expansion coefficient
  • Thermal Resistance Converter - Temperature rise per unit power
  • Thermal Conductivity Converter - Heat flow rate per unit length per unit temperature
  • Specific Heat Capacity Converter - Heat required per unit mass per unit temperature
  • Thermal Energy Density Converter - Energy stored or released per unit volume
  • Heat Flux Density Converter - Power transferred per unit area
  • Heat Transfer Coefficient Converter - Heat flow per unit area per unit temperature difference
