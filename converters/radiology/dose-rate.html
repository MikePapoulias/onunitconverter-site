Common Dose Rate Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Gy/s = 60 Gy/min = 3600 Gy/h (exact; SI prefix time conversions)
    • 
        
  • 1 Gy/s = 100 rad/s = 6000 rad/min = 360 000 rad/h (exact; 1 rad = 0.01 Gy)
    • 
        
  • 1 mSv/h = 1 mGy/h for photon radiation (wR = 1; ICRP 103)  |  1 µSv/h = 1 µGy/h for photons
    • 
        
  • 1 Gy/min = 100 rad/min = 1.6̅ × 10-2 Gy/s (exact)
    • 
        
  • Typical background: 0.1-0.3 µSv/h  |  Transatlantic flight: ~3 µSv/h
    • 
        
  • Clinical radiotherapy: 1-6 Gy/min  |  HDR brachytherapy: 12+ Gy/min  |  FLASH-RT: >40 Gy/s
