Common Exposure Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 R = 2.58 × 10-4 C/kg (exact, 1962 ICRU definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 C/kg ≈ 3876 R  |  1 mC/kg ≈ 3.876 R  |  1 µC/kg ≈ 3.876 mR
    • 
        
  • 1 kR = 0.258 C/kg  |  1 MR = 258 C/kg
    • 
        
  • Background gamma at sea level: ~8-10 µR/h ≈ 2-3 pC/kg/s
    • 
        
  • Chest X-ray entrance exposure: ~20-40 mR ≈ 5-10 µC/kg
    • 
        
  • Radiation survey instrument display: typically in mR/h or µSv/h
