Common Radioactive Activity Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Ci = 3.7 × 1010 Bq (exact, BIPM 1975 definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 Rd = 106 Bq = 1 MBq (exact, rutherford, Montreal Conference 1946)
    • 
        
  • 1 dpm = 1/60 Bq ≈ 16.667 mBq (exact - used in liquid scintillation counting)
    • 
        
  • 1 mCi = 37 MBq = 3.7 × 107 Bq (US clinical nuclear medicine standard)
    • 
        
  • 1 kBq = 103 Bq  |  1 MBq = 106 Bq  |  1 GBq = 109 Bq  |  1 TBq = 1012 Bq
    • 
        
  • 18F-FDG PET dose: 370 MBq = 10 mCi  |  99mTc bone scan: 740 MBq = 20 mCi
