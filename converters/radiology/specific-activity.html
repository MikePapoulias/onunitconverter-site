Common Specific Activity Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Ci/g = 3.7 × 1013 Bq/kg (exact) = 1 kCi/kg
    • 
        
  • 1 Bq/kg = 1 mBq/g = 10-3 Bq/g
    • 
        
  • 1 kBq/kg = 1 Bq/g  |  1 MBq/kg = 1 kBq/g
    • 
        
  • 1 nCi/kg = 37 Bq/kg ≈ 1 pCi/g (= pCi per gram)
    • 
        
  • 1 dpm/g = 1000/60 Bq/kg ≈ 16.67 Bq/kg
    • 
        
  • Specific activity of 226Ra (pure): ≈ 37 GBq/g = 37 TBq/kg = 1 Ci/g
    • 
        
  • Natural potassium (40K, 0.0117 %): ≈ 31.3 Bq/g = 31.3 kBq/kg
    • 
        
  • EU food safety limit for 137Cs: 600 Bq/kg (general foods)
