Common Absorbed Dose Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Gy = 100 rad = 1 J/kg (exact, SI definition)
    • 
        
  • 1 rad = 0.01 Gy = 1 cGy (exact; note: 1 rad = 1 cGy numerically - used in radiotherapy)
    • 
        
  • 1 erg/g = 10-4 Gy = 0.1 mGy (exact; CGS absorbed dose unit)
    • 
        
  • 1 krad = 10 Gy  |  1 kGy = 100 krad  |  1 Mrad = 10 kGy
    • 
        
  • Chest X-ray: ~0.1 mGy skin dose  |  Abdominal CT: ~10-30 mGy
    • 
        
  • Radiotherapy tumour dose: 40-80 Gy (fractionated)  |  Food sterilisation: 25 kGy
