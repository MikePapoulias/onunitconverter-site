Common Equivalent Dose Conversions

      
    
        
  • 1 Sv = 100 rem = 1 J/kg (exact, SI definition for equivalent dose)
    • 
        
  • 1 rem = 0.01 Sv = 1 cSv (exact; note: 1 rem = 1 cSv numerically - used in US radiation protection)
    • 
        
  • 1 mSv = 100 mrem  |  1 µSv = 0.1 mrem
    • 
        
  • Worldwide average background: ~2.4 mSv/year  |  US average: ~3.1 mSv/year
    • 
        
  • Occupational limit (ICRP 103): 20 mSv/year  |  NRC (US): 50 mSv/year (5 rem/year)
    • 
        
  • CT abdomen: ~10 mSv  |  Chest X-ray: ~0.1 mSv  |  Transatlantic flight: ~0.08 mSv
