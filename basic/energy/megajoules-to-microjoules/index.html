About Megajoules

A megajoule (MJ) is 10⁶ J - the scale of fuel combustion in vehicles and kinetic energy of road traffic. Energy content: 1 L diesel ~34.9 MJ; 1 L petrol ~32.2 MJ. A 1,500 kg car at 100 km/h has KE = ½ × 1,500 × 27.78² ≈ 0.579 MJ; at 200 km/h it carries 2.31 MJ - explaining crash severity's quadratic speed dependence. A 60 kWh EV battery stores 60 × 3.6 = 216 MJ. Detonation of 1 kg TNT releases 4.184 MJ. Adults at rest metabolise ~6-9 MJ/day. 1 MJ = 10⁶ J = 239.0 kcal = 0.27778 kWh = 947.8 BTU.

About Microjoules

A microjoule (µJ) is 10⁻⁶ J - the scale of precision laser ablation, ESD damage events, and individual LIDAR pulses. LASIK eye surgery delivers 0.5-2 µJ per pulse at 193 nm, ablating ~0.25 µm of corneal tissue per pulse. ESD events that destroy IC gates correspond to 1-1,000 µJ; the Human Body Model test discharges ~500 µJ at 2 kV. Q-switched microchip laser pulses for rangefinding are 1-100 µJ. The kinetic energy of a 1 mg grain of sand falling 10 cm is mgh ≈ 0.98 µJ. 1 µJ = 10⁻⁶ J.
