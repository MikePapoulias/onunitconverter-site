About Microjoules

A microjoule (µJ) is 10⁻⁶ J - the scale of precision laser ablation, ESD damage events, and individual LIDAR pulses. LASIK eye surgery delivers 0.5-2 µJ per pulse at 193 nm, ablating ~0.25 µm of corneal tissue per pulse. ESD events that destroy IC gates correspond to 1-1,000 µJ; the Human Body Model test discharges ~500 µJ at 2 kV. Q-switched microchip laser pulses for rangefinding are 1-100 µJ. The kinetic energy of a 1 mg grain of sand falling 10 cm is mgh ≈ 0.98 µJ. 1 µJ = 10⁻⁶ J.

About Exajoules

An exajoule (EJ) is 10¹⁸ J - the scale of global energy consumption. World primary energy supply in 2022 was ~580-610 EJ: coal ~160 EJ; oil ~190 EJ; gas ~145 EJ; nuclear ~26 EJ; wind ~8 EJ; solar PV ~8 EJ. Global electricity generation of ~29,000 TWh = 104 EJ. Solar energy incident on Earth's atmosphere is ~5.5 × 10⁶ EJ/year - approximately 9,000× total human demand. Annual fossil fuel combustion releases ~490 EJ/year, producing ~36.8 Gt CO₂. 1 EJ = 10¹⁸ J = 277.78 TWh = 238.85 Mtoe.
