About Minutes

A minute (min) = exactly 60 s, inherited from Babylonian sexagesimal astronomy. Medieval astronomers named the sixtieth part of a degree 'pars minuta prima' (hence minute); its sixtieth 'pars minuta secunda' (hence second). Running performance is expressed in min/km or min/mile; music tempo in BPM (beats per minute, ~40-220); angles in degrees-minutes-seconds (DMS). 1 min = 60 s = 1/60 h.

About Microseconds

A microsecond (µs) = 10⁻⁶ s; light travels ~300 m. Professional DSLR shutters reach 125 µs (1/8,000 s); electronic flash freezes motion in 1-50 µs. Radar pulse repetition intervals: 100-2,000 µs (15-300 km range). Ultrasound echo round-trips from 1-15 cm tissue depth: 13-200 µs. Muscle twitch contraction starts within 1-5 µs of the action potential. 1 µs = 10⁻⁶ s.
