About Megayears

A megayear (1 Ma) = 10⁶ yr ≈ 3.156 × 10¹³ s - the timescale of geology and tectonic evolution. The K-Pg mass extinction at 66.0 Ma; Cambrian explosion ~538.8 Ma; Great Oxygenation Event ~2,400 Ma. Plate motion at 2-10 cm/yr = 200-1,000 km per Ma (Atlantic ~3,000 km wider since Pangaea rifting ~200 Ma ago). Background extinction rate measured in extinctions per million species-years. 1 Ma = 10⁶ yr.

About Microseconds

A microsecond (µs) = 10⁻⁶ s; light travels ~300 m. Professional DSLR shutters reach 125 µs (1/8,000 s); electronic flash freezes motion in 1-50 µs. Radar pulse repetition intervals: 100-2,000 µs (15-300 km range). Ultrasound echo round-trips from 1-15 cm tissue depth: 13-200 µs. Muscle twitch contraction starts within 1-5 µs of the action potential. 1 µs = 10⁻⁶ s.
