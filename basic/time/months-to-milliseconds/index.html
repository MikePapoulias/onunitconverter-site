About Months

A mean Gregorian calendar month = 365.2425 ÷ 12 = 30.436875 days = 2,629,746 s exactly (averaged over the 400-year cycle with exactly 97 leap years). Individual months have 28-31 days. Loan schedules, subscriptions, and mobile billing reference 'per month', requiring explicit agreement on meaning. ISO 8601 uses 'P1M'. 1 mo = 30.436875 d = 2,629,746 s.

About Milliseconds

A millisecond (ms) = 10⁻³ s. Human visual reaction time 150-300 ms; auditory reaction 140-160 ms. Echo delays below ~30 ms cause Haas reinforcement; 100-500 ms are perceived as distinct echoes. One cinema frame at 24 fps = 41.7 ms; 60 fps gaming requires 16.7 ms/frame. Millisecond pulsars rotate in 1.4-30 ms. MIDI keyboard on-latency 1-3 ms. 1 ms = 10⁻³ s.
