About Pounds per Square Inch

One pound per square inch (psi) ≈ 6,894.76 Pa - the dominant pressure unit in US industry and consumer products. Car tyres: 32-36 psi; truck tyres 80-120 psi; bicycle road tyres 80-130 psi. Home water supply: 40-80 psi; residential maximum 80 psi per plumbing code. Compressed air tools: 90-150 psi. Steam autoclave sterilisation: 15-30 psi. The distinction between gauge (psig) and absolute (psia) pressure is critical: gauge pressure is relative to ambient; absolute = gauge + ~14.696 psi. 1 psi = 6,894.76 Pa.

About Nanopascals

A nanopascal (nPa) is 10⁻⁹ Pa - the pressure scale of deep interplanetary space. LIGO and Virgo gravitational wave detectors must isolate mirror displacement signals against noise floors in the nanopascal regime. Low Earth orbit residual atmosphere is ~100 µPa; deep interplanetary space is ~1-10 nPa depending on solar wind activity. Thermospheric gas layers above 200 km altitude fall into the nPa-µPa range. Casimir force calculations and quantum vacuum fluctuation modelling also operate at nanopascal scales. 1 nPa = 10⁻⁹ Pa.
