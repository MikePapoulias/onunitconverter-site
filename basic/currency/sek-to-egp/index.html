About Swedish Krona

The Swedish Krona (SEK) is the official currency of Sweden. Despite being an EU member since 1995, Sweden held a referendum in 2003 in which voters rejected adopting the Euro, and Sweden has since maintained the krona with no current plans for Eurozone entry. The Riksbank, established in 1668, is the world's oldest central bank and manages monetary policy. Sweden has a highly developed, export-oriented economy known for brands like IKEA, Volvo, H&M, Ericsson, and Spotify. The krona is divided into 100 ore, though ore coins are no longer issued.

About Egyptian Pound

The Egyptian Pound (EGP) is the official currency of Egypt and one of the oldest currency systems in the world, with versions of the pound used since Egypt adopted a monetary system in the 19th century. The Central Bank of Egypt manages monetary policy. Egypt is the most populous Arab country and the third most populous in Africa, with an economy driven by tourism, Suez Canal revenues, remittances, oil exports, and agriculture. The pound underwent significant devaluations in 2016 and 2023 as Egypt moved to a more flexible exchange rate policy under IMF-backed economic reform programmes.
