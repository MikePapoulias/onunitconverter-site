About Feet

A foot (ft) is equal to 12 inches or 30.48 centimeters, originally based on the length of a human foot. It remains widely used in the United States and some other countries for measuring height, room dimensions, and distances. For reference, a standard door is about 6.5-7 feet tall, ceiling height in homes is typically 8-9 feet, and a basketball hoop stands 10 feet high. The foot is also used in aviation (altitude) and real estate (square footage).

About Millimeters

A millimeter (mm) is one thousandth of a meter, making it the smallest unit commonly used in everyday measurements. To visualize this, a credit card is about 1 mm thick, and a paperclip wire is roughly 1 mm in diameter. Millimeters are essential in engineering, manufacturing, and medicine for precise measurements. Rainfall is measured in millimeters, with 1 mm representing a very light drizzle. Most rulers and measuring tapes include millimeter markings for accuracy.
