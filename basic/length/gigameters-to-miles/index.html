About Gigameters

A gigameter (Gm) is one billion meters (10⁹ m), making it useful for planetary measurements and inner solar system distances. The Sun's diameter is about 1.39 Gm, while Jupiter's diameter is roughly 0.14 Gm. The distance from Earth to the Moon is approximately 0.38 Gm. This unit bridges the gap between terrestrial measurements (megameters) and astronomical distances, providing a convenient scale for describing planetary sizes and nearby celestial distances in our solar system.

About Miles

A mile (mi) is 5,280 feet or approximately 1.609 kilometers, originating from the Roman 'mille passus' (thousand paces). It remains the primary distance unit in the United States and United Kingdom for road travel, with speed limits and distances posted in miles per hour (mph) and miles respectively. For perspective, a 15-20 minute drive in the city typically covers 2-4 miles, while a marathon is about 26.2 miles. The mile is also used in aviation (nautical miles) and running events.
