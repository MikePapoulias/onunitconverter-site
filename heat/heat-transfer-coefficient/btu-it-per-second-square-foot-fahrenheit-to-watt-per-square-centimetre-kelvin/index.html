Conversion Formula

About International table BTU per second per square foot per degree Fahrenheit

The international table BTU per second per square foot per degree Fahrenheit (BTU(IT)/(s·ft²·°F)) equals ≈ 20442 W/(m²·K) (= 3600 × BTU(IT)/(h·ft²·°F)). Encountered in atmospheric re-entry aerodynamic heating analysis, solid rocket motor nozzle throat design, and nuclear reactor thermal-hydraulics with peak surface heat transfer rates. 1 BTU(IT)/(s·ft²·°F) ≈ 20442 W/(m²·K).

About Watt per square centimetre kelvin

The watt per square centimetre kelvin (W/(cm²·K)) equals exactly 10⁴ W/(m²·K). Relevant for extreme cooling: nuclear reactor fuel rod cladding surfaces reach up to 5 W/(cm²·K), spray cooling and microjet impingement in power electronics reach 1-10 W/(cm²·K), and condensation of refrigerants can approach 1 W/(cm²·K). 1 W/(cm²·K) = 10⁴ W/(m²·K) = 10 kW/(m²·K).
