Conversion Formula

About Per millikelvin

The per millikelvin (1/mK) equals exactly 10³ /K and is encountered in cryogenic physics where working temperatures lie in the millikelvin range. Near quantum phase transitions and magnetic ordering temperatures, anomalous CTE values can reach orders far above room-temperature CTE. 1 /mK = 10³ /K = 10⁹ ppm/K.

About Per microkelvin

The per microkelvin (1/μK) equals exactly 10⁶ /K. It is used in ultra-precision experiments probing anomalous thermal expansion extremely close to second-order phase transitions, such as the superfluid He-4 lambda point (2.1768 K) and magnetic phase boundaries in rare-earth compounds. 1 /μK = 10⁶ /K = 10¹² ppm/K.
