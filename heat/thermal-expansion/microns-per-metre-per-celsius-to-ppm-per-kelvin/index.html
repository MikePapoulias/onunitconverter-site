Convert Microns per metre per degree Celsius to ppm per kelvin

Convert Microns per metre per degree Celsius (μm/(m·°C)) to ppm per kelvin (ppm/K) instantly and accurately.

Microns per metre per degree Celsius (μm/(m·°C))

Conversion Formula

ppm/K = μm/(m·°C) × 1

About Microns per metre per degree Celsius

The micrometre per metre per degree Celsius (μm/(m·°C)) equals exactly 10⁻⁶ /K, since temperature intervals 1 °C = 1 K (exact). It is the standard CTE notation in European engineering datasheets, ISO material standards, and many textbooks. Numerically identical to ppm/K. Aluminium: 23.1 μm/(m·°C); steel: ≈12 μm/(m·°C). 1 μm/(m·°C) = 1 ppm/K = 10⁻⁶ /K.

About ppm per kelvin

The ppm/K (= μm/(m·K) exactly) equals 10⁻⁶ /K and is the universal CTE unit in international materials standards (ISO, EN, DIN). Aluminium: 23.1 ppm/K; steel: ≈12 ppm/K; ordinary glass: ≈9 ppm/K; fused silica: 0.55 ppm/K; Invar: ≈1.2 ppm/K. 1 ppm/K = 10⁻⁶ /K.
