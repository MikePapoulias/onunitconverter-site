Conversion Formula

About ppm per degree Fahrenheit

The ppm/°F (= μin/(in·°F) exactly) equals 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻⁶ /K and is the dominant CTE unit in US ASTM standards, ASME piping codes, and mechanical engineering handbooks. Steel ≈6.7 ppm/°F; aluminium ≈12.8 ppm/°F; copper ≈9.4 ppm/°F. 1 ppm/°F = 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K.

About Microinches per inch per degree Fahrenheit

The microinch per inch per degree Fahrenheit (μin/(in·°F)) equals exactly 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K = 1 ppm/°F. It is the native US CTE unit in ASTM E228 and ASTM E831 standards; most US material datasheets list CTE values in this unit. Steel: ≈6.7 μin/(in·°F); aluminium: ≈12.8 μin/(in·°F); titanium: ≈4.9 μin/(in·°F). 1 μin/(in·°F) = 1 ppm/°F.
