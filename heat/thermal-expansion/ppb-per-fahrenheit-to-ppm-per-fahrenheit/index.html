Conversion Formula

ppm/°F = ppb/°F × 0.001

About ppb per degree Fahrenheit

The ppb/°F equals 9/5 × 10⁻⁹ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻⁹ /K, the Fahrenheit counterpart of ppb/K. Used when ultra-low-expansion glass ceramics are characterised in US laboratories using Fahrenheit protocols. Zerodur at ±0.02 ppb/K ≈ ±0.011 ppb/°F. 1 ppb/°F = 9/5 × 10⁻⁹ /K ≈1.8 ppb/K.

About ppm per degree Fahrenheit

The ppm/°F (= μin/(in·°F) exactly) equals 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻⁶ /K and is the dominant CTE unit in US ASTM standards, ASME piping codes, and mechanical engineering handbooks. Steel ≈6.7 ppm/°F; aluminium ≈12.8 ppm/°F; copper ≈9.4 ppm/°F. 1 ppm/°F = 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K.
