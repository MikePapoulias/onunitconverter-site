Convert Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit to ppm per degree Fahrenheit

Convert Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit (mm/(m·°F)) to ppm per degree Fahrenheit (ppm/°F) instantly and accurately.

Conversion Formula

ppm/°F = mm/(m·°F) × 1000

About Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit

The mm/(m·°F) equals 9/5 × 10⁻³ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻³ /K, combining metric displacement with Fahrenheit temperature. It appears in US-origin pipeline and structural reports where dimensions are given in metres but temperatures in °F. Steel: ≈0.00667 mm/(m·°F); aluminium: ≈0.01283 mm/(m·°F). 1 mm/(m·°F) = 9/5 × 10⁻³ /K.

About ppm per degree Fahrenheit

The ppm/°F (= μin/(in·°F) exactly) equals 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻⁶ /K and is the dominant CTE unit in US ASTM standards, ASME piping codes, and mechanical engineering handbooks. Steel ≈6.7 ppm/°F; aluminium ≈12.8 ppm/°F; copper ≈9.4 ppm/°F. 1 ppm/°F = 9/5 × 10⁻⁶ /K.
