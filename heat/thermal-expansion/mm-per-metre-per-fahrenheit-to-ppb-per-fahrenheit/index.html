Convert Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit (mm/(m·°F)) to ppb per degree Fahrenheit (ppb/°F) instantly and accurately.

Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit (mm/(m·°F))
ppb per degree Fahrenheit (ppb/°F)

Conversion Formula

ppb/°F = mm/(m·°F) × 1000000

About Millimetres per metre per degree Fahrenheit

The mm/(m·°F) equals 9/5 × 10⁻³ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻³ /K, combining metric displacement with Fahrenheit temperature. It appears in US-origin pipeline and structural reports where dimensions are given in metres but temperatures in °F. Steel: ≈0.00667 mm/(m·°F); aluminium: ≈0.01283 mm/(m·°F). 1 mm/(m·°F) = 9/5 × 10⁻³ /K.

About ppb per degree Fahrenheit

The ppb/°F equals 9/5 × 10⁻⁹ /K ≈1.8 × 10⁻⁹ /K, the Fahrenheit counterpart of ppb/K. Used when ultra-low-expansion glass ceramics are characterised in US laboratories using Fahrenheit protocols. Zerodur at ±0.02 ppb/K ≈ ±0.011 ppb/°F. 1 ppb/°F = 9/5 × 10⁻⁹ /K ≈1.8 ppb/K.
