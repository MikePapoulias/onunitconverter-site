Conversion Formula

About Millijoule per cubic metre

The millijoule per cubic metre (mJ/m³) equals 10⁻³ J/m³. Used in radiation physics for describing energy densities of laser pulses in large volumes and in some acoustic energy density contexts. 1 mJ/m³ = 10⁻³ J/m³ = 10³ µJ/m³.

About International table kilocalorie per cubic metre

The international table kilocalorie per cubic metre (kcal(IT)/m³) equals exactly 4186.8 J/m³ (1 kcal(IT) = 4186.8 J). The slightly larger IT variant of the kilocalorie used in steam tables and international thermodynamics. 1 kcal(IT)/m³ = 4186.8 J/m³ ≈ 1.163 Wh/m³.
