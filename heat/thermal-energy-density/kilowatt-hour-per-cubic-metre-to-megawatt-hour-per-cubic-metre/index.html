About Kilowatt-hour per cubic metre

The kilowatt-hour per cubic metre (kWh/m³) equals exactly 3.6 × 10⁶ J/m³. Widely used in battery technology and electrical energy storage: Li-ion cells ≈ 200-700 kWh/m³, pumped hydro ≈ 0.27-1.5 Wh/m³. Also used for volumetric electricity price comparisons. 1 kWh/m³ = 3.6 MJ/m³ = 3600 kJ/m³.

About Megawatt-hour per cubic metre

The megawatt-hour per cubic metre (MWh/m³) equals exactly 3.6 × 10⁹ J/m³. Used in large-scale energy storage infrastructure - pumped hydro, grid-scale battery installations, and thermal storage plants. Petrol ≈ 9.5 MWh/m³, LNG ≈ 6.2 MWh/m³, Li-ion pack ≈ 0.56 MWh/m³. 1 MWh/m³ = 3.6 GJ/m³ = 3600 MJ/m³.
