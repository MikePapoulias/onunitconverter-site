About Microjoule per cubic metre

The microjoule per cubic metre (µJ/m³) equals 10⁻⁶ J/m³. Encountered in precision measurements of very low energy-density fields such as weak electromagnetic radiation in shielded environments and in certain astrophysical energy density calculations. 1 µJ/m³ = 10⁻⁶ J/m³ = 10³ nJ/m³.

About International table calorie per cubic centimetre

The international table calorie per cubic centimetre (cal(IT)/cm³) equals exactly 4.1868 × 10⁶ J/m³ (1 cal(IT) = 4.1868 J; 1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³). The IT variant of the cal/cm³ used in steam-table-based thermodynamics and older international standards. Numerically equal to kcal(IT)/L. 1 cal(IT)/cm³ = 4.1868 MJ/m³.
