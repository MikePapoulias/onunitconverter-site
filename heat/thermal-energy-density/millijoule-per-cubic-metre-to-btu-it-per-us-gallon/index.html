About Millijoule per cubic metre

The millijoule per cubic metre (mJ/m³) equals 10⁻³ J/m³. Used in radiation physics for describing energy densities of laser pulses in large volumes and in some acoustic energy density contexts. 1 mJ/m³ = 10⁻³ J/m³ = 10³ µJ/m³.

About International table BTU per US gallon

The international table BTU per US gallon (BTU(IT)/gal(US)) equals 1055.05585262 J / 3.785411784 × 10⁻³ m³ ≈ 278716.27 J/m³. The dominant unit in the US petroleum and fuel markets for expressing heating values - gasoline ≈ 115,000 BTU/gal, diesel ≈ 129,500 BTU/gal. 1 BTU(IT)/gal(US) ≈ 278.72 kJ/m³.
