Conversion Formula

About Nautical miles per kilogram

The nautical mile per kilogram (nmi/kg) equals exactly 1.852 km/kg, used in aviation and maritime fuel performance analyses expressed in metric mass units. A large turbofan airliner achieves ≈0.15-0.25 nmi/kg of jet fuel per seat; a high-efficiency glider with a small auxiliary engine around 5-20 nmi/kg. 1 nmi/kg = 1.852 km/kg.

About Metres per kilogram

The metre per kilogram (m/kg) is the SI coherent derived unit of mass-based fuel efficiency. It is used in combustion research, rocket propellant comparative testing, and laboratory fuel-cell efficiency benchmarking. A car at 15 km/kg petrol = 15 000 m/kg; a hydrogen fuel cell bus at 120 km/kg H₂ = 120 000 m/kg. 1 m/kg = 1e⁻³ km/kg = 1/1000 km/kg.
