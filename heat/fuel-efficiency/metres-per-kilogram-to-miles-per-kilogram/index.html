About Metres per kilogram

The metre per kilogram (m/kg) is the SI coherent derived unit of mass-based fuel efficiency. It is used in combustion research, rocket propellant comparative testing, and laboratory fuel-cell efficiency benchmarking. A car at 15 km/kg petrol = 15 000 m/kg; a hydrogen fuel cell bus at 120 km/kg H₂ = 120 000 m/kg. 1 m/kg = 1e⁻³ km/kg = 1/1000 km/kg.

About Miles per kilogram

The mile per kilogram (mi/kg) equals 1.609344 km/kg, used in academic hydrogen vehicle studies and some US-metric hybrid engineering databases. A Toyota Mirai at 140 km/kg H₂ ≈87 mi/kg; a propane bus at 5 km/kg LPG ≈3.1 mi/kg. 1 mi/kg = 1.609344 km/kg ≈1.609 km/kg.
