About Kilometres per pound

The kilometre per pound (km/lb) equals ≈2.205 km/kg, common in US-to-metric cross-border transport analysis and fuel research papers where distances are in km but fuel invoices are weighed in pounds. A car burning 6 kg/100 km petrol gets ≈16.7 km/kg = 7.57 km/lb. 1 km/lb = 1/0.45359237 km/kg ≈2.205 km/kg.

About Miles per pound

The mile per pound (mi/lb) equals ≈3.548 km/kg, the traditional US aviation mass-efficiency unit. Cessna 172 on avgas: ≈0.28-0.32 mi/lb; Piper Cherokee: ≈0.30-0.35 mi/lb; Boeing 737 MAX per seat: ≈0.10-0.15 mi/lb. US military aircraft fuel efficiency analyses and early NASA propellant studies used mi/lb. 1 mi/lb ≈3.548 km/kg = 1.609344/0.45359237 km/kg.
