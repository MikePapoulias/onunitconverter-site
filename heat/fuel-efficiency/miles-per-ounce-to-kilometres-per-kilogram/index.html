About Miles per ounce

The mile per ounce (mi/oz) equals ≈56.78 km/kg, used for small general aviation aircraft and large model aircraft where fuel loads are a few ounces. A 100 cc glow-engine aircraft consuming 1 oz per mile of flight = 1 mi/oz; small RC turbine jets achieve 0.5-3 mi/oz. Also used in US drag-racing fuel consumption records. 1 mi/oz ≈56.78 km/kg = 16 mi/lb.

About Kilometres per kilogram

The kilometre per kilogram (km/kg) is the standard metric mass-based fuel efficiency unit. Toyota Mirai FCEV: ≈140 km/kg H₂; current-generation hydrogen buses: 8-12 km/kg; LPG cars: 12-17 km/kg; CNG cars: 14-18 km/kg. Aviation: Airbus A320neo ≈0.08 km/kg fuel per seat. 1 km/kg = 1000 m/kg ≈0.621 mi/kg.
