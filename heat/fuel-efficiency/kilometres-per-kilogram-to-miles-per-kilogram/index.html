About Kilometres per kilogram

The kilometre per kilogram (km/kg) is the standard metric mass-based fuel efficiency unit. Toyota Mirai FCEV: ≈140 km/kg H₂; current-generation hydrogen buses: 8-12 km/kg; LPG cars: 12-17 km/kg; CNG cars: 14-18 km/kg. Aviation: Airbus A320neo ≈0.08 km/kg fuel per seat. 1 km/kg = 1000 m/kg ≈0.621 mi/kg.

About Miles per kilogram

The mile per kilogram (mi/kg) equals 1.609344 km/kg, used in academic hydrogen vehicle studies and some US-metric hybrid engineering databases. A Toyota Mirai at 140 km/kg H₂ ≈87 mi/kg; a propane bus at 5 km/kg LPG ≈3.1 mi/kg. 1 mi/kg = 1.609344 km/kg ≈1.609 km/kg.
