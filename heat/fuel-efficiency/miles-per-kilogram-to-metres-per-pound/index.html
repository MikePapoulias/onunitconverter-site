About Miles per kilogram

The mile per kilogram (mi/kg) equals 1.609344 km/kg, used in academic hydrogen vehicle studies and some US-metric hybrid engineering databases. A Toyota Mirai at 140 km/kg H₂ ≈87 mi/kg; a propane bus at 5 km/kg LPG ≈3.1 mi/kg. 1 mi/kg = 1.609344 km/kg ≈1.609 km/kg.

About Metres per pound

The metre per pound (m/lb) equals ≈2.205 × 10⁻³ km/kg, a transitional unit bridging SI metres with imperial pound mass. It appears in some international academic fuel cell papers and comparative combustion analysis where authors mix SI distance with US fuel weight measurements. 1 m/lb ≈2.205 × 10⁻³ km/kg ≈1.094 yd/lb.
