About Kilometres per gram

The kilometre per gram (km/g) equals 1000 km/kg, the unit for ultra-efficient propulsion systems and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles. A Toyota Mirai at 140 km/kg H₂ = 0.14 km/g; hypothetical rocket-propellant comparison studies use km/g to express chemical energy density. A solid-propellant gas generator releasing 1 km of equivalent travel per gram of propellant represents extreme efficiency. 1 km/g = 1000 km/kg.

About Miles per kilogram

The mile per kilogram (mi/kg) equals 1.609344 km/kg, used in academic hydrogen vehicle studies and some US-metric hybrid engineering databases. A Toyota Mirai at 140 km/kg H₂ ≈87 mi/kg; a propane bus at 5 km/kg LPG ≈3.1 mi/kg. 1 mi/kg = 1.609344 km/kg ≈1.609 km/kg.
