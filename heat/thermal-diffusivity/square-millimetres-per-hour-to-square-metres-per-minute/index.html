Conversion Formula

About Square millimetres per hour

The square millimetre per hour (mm²/h) equals 1/3 600 000 000 m²/s, the smallest practical thermal diffusivity unit. It is used in very slow heat-transfer processes such as diffusion through frozen soils or phase-change materials studied over many hours. Heavy clay soils can show diffusivity values of 1-2 mm²/h. 1 mm²/h = 1e-6/3600 m²/s.

About Square metres per minute

The square metre per minute (m²/min) equals 1/60 m²/s ≈0.01667 m²/s, a non-standard unit seen in some process-engineering contexts where heat soak times are measured in minutes rather than seconds. Nuclear fuel rod thermal analysis and large refractory furnace lining design occasionally cite diffusivity in m²/min. 1 m²/min = 1/60 m²/s ≈ 6000 cm²/s.
