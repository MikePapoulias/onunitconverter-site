About Square millimetres per minute

The square millimetre per minute (mm²/min) equals 1/60 000 000 m²/s. It appears in material testing standards where thermal diffusivity of polymers and composites is measured over minute-scale heating pulses. ASTM and ISO laser-flash methods sometimes report in mm²/min as an intermediate step. 1 mm²/min = 1e-6/60 m²/s ≈ 1.667 × 10⁻¸ m²/s.

About Square centimetres per second

The square centimetre per second (cm²/s) equals 1 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s and is the CGS unit for thermal diffusivity, sometimes called the stokes by analogy with kinematic viscosity. Pre-SI thermophysics literature and many older reference databases list values in cm²/s: iron ≈0.18 cm²/s; aluminium ≈0.84 cm²/s; water ≈1.4 × 10⁻³ cm²/s. 1 cm²/s = 1 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s.
