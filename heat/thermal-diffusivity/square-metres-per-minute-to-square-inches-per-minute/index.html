About Square metres per minute

The square metre per minute (m²/min) equals 1/60 m²/s ≈0.01667 m²/s, a non-standard unit seen in some process-engineering contexts where heat soak times are measured in minutes rather than seconds. Nuclear fuel rod thermal analysis and large refractory furnace lining design occasionally cite diffusivity in m²/min. 1 m²/min = 1/60 m²/s ≈ 6000 cm²/s.

About Square inches per minute

The square inch per minute (in²/min) equals ≈1.075 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s, found in US military and aerospace specifications for thermal protection materials and ablative heat shields. Some NASA technical reports on reentry vehicle TPS (Thermal Protection System) materials quote diffusivity in in²/min. 1 in²/min = 0.00064516/60 m²/s.
