Conversion Formula

Wh/(kg·K) = CHU/(lb·°C) × 1.163

About Celsius Heat Unit per pound per degree Celsius

The Celsius heat unit per pound per degree Celsius (CHU/(lb·°C)) equals exactly (1055.05585262 × 9)/(0.45359237 × 5) J/(kg·K) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K). The CHU is 9/5 BTU(IT); combined with a 1 °C instead of 1 °F interval the factor is the same as BTU(IT)/(lb·°F). Used in older UK and Canadian engineering practice. Water: 1 CHU/(lb·°C). 1 CHU/(lb·°C) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K).

About Watt-hour per kilogram kelvin

The watt-hour per kilogram kelvin (Wh/(kg·K)) equals exactly 3600 J/(kg·K). Used in battery and thermal energy storage engineering to express the heat capacity of phase-change materials (PCM), molten salts, and battery cells where energy is quantified in watt-hours. Paraffin wax ≈0.055 Wh/(kg·K). 1 Wh/(kg·K) = 3600 J/(kg·K) = 3.6 kJ/(kg·K).
