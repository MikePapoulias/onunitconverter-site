Conversion Formula

kJ/(kg·K) = BTU(IT)/(lb·°F) × 4.1868

About International table BTU per pound per degree Fahrenheit

The international table BTU per pound per degree Fahrenheit (BTU(IT)/(lb·°F)) equals (1055.05585262 × 9)/(0.45359237 × 5) J/(kg·K) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K). The standard US unit for specific heat capacity in ASHRAE, ASTM, and mechanical engineering references. Water: 1.000 BTU(IT)/(lb·°F); steel: ≈0.108; air: ≈0.240. 1 BTU(IT)/(lb·°F) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K).

About Kilojoule per kilogram kelvin

The kilojoule per kilogram kelvin (kJ/(kg·K)) equals exactly 1000 J/(kg·K). The most common unit in engineering thermodynamics textbooks and technical data sheets: water ≈4.182, steam ≈2.08, dry air ≈1.005 kJ/(kg·K). Found throughout ASHRAE, ISO, and EN standards for heat exchanger and HVAC design. 1 kJ/(kg·K) = 1000 J/(kg·K) = 1 kJ/(kg·°C).
