Conversion Formula

BTU(IT)/(lb·°F) = kcal(IT)/(kg·°C) × 1

About International table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius

The international table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius (kcal(IT)/(kg·°C)) equals exactly 4186.8 J/(kg·K) (1 kcal(IT) = 4186.8 J). The common practical unit in European industry and the 'large calorie' of nutrition science. Water: 1 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C); ethanol: 0.588 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C). 1 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K) = 1 cal(IT)/(g·°C).

About International table BTU per pound per degree Fahrenheit

The international table BTU per pound per degree Fahrenheit (BTU(IT)/(lb·°F)) equals (1055.05585262 × 9)/(0.45359237 × 5) J/(kg·K) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K). The standard US unit for specific heat capacity in ASHRAE, ASTM, and mechanical engineering references. Water: 1.000 BTU(IT)/(lb·°F); steel: ≈0.108; air: ≈0.240. 1 BTU(IT)/(lb·°F) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K).
