Convert International table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius to Kilojoule per kilogram degree Celsius

Convert International table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius (kcal(IT)/(kg·°C)) to Kilojoule per kilogram degree Celsius (kJ/(kg·°C)) instantly and accurately.

International table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius (kcal(IT)/(kg·°C))
Kilojoule per kilogram degree Celsius (kJ/(kg·°C))

Conversion Formula

kJ/(kg·°C) = kcal(IT)/(kg·°C) × 4.1868

About International table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius

The international table kilocalorie per kilogram per degree Celsius (kcal(IT)/(kg·°C)) equals exactly 4186.8 J/(kg·K) (1 kcal(IT) = 4186.8 J). The common practical unit in European industry and the 'large calorie' of nutrition science. Water: 1 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C); ethanol: 0.588 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C). 1 kcal(IT)/(kg·°C) = 4186.8 J/(kg·K) = 1 cal(IT)/(g·°C).

About Kilojoule per kilogram degree Celsius

The kilojoule per kilogram degree Celsius (kJ/(kg·°C)) equals exactly 1 kJ/(kg·K) = 1000 J/(kg·K) since 1 °C = 1 K. The preferred notation in European process and chemical engineering where Celsius dominates. Often used interchangeably with kJ/(kg·K) in material datasheets. Water: 4.182 kJ/(kg·°C). 1 kJ/(kg·°C) = 1 kJ/(kg·K) = 1000 J/(kg·K).
