Convert BTU inch per hour per square foot per degree Fahrenheit to Kilocalorie per metre per hour per degree Celsius

Convert BTU inch per hour per square foot per degree Fahrenheit (BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F)) to Kilocalorie per metre per hour per degree Celsius (kcal/(m·h·°C)) instantly and accurately.

BTU inch per hour per square foot per degree Fahrenheit (BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F))
Kilocalorie per metre per hour per degree Celsius (kcal/(m·h·°C))

Conversion Formula

kcal/(m·h·°C) = BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F) × 0.124013662

About BTU inch per hour per square foot per degree Fahrenheit

The BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F) equals (1055.05585262 × 0.0254 × 9)/(3600 × 0.09290304 × 5) W/(m·K) ≈0.1442 W/(m·K). The standard US k-factor unit for insulation per ASTM C177, C335, and C518. Mineral wool ≈0.25; expanded polystyrene ≈0.24; polyurethane foam ≈0.14 BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F). 1 BTU·in/(h·ft²·°F) ≈0.1442 W/(m·K).

About Kilocalorie per metre per hour per degree Celsius

The kcal/(m·h·°C) equals exactly 4186.8/3600 W/(m·K) = 1.163 W/(m·K) (1 kcal(IT) = 4186.8 J). Used in pre-SI continental European engineering literature, older DIN/BS standards, and Soviet-era technical documentation. Brick: ≈0.6; dense concrete: ≈1.3; carbon steel: ≈38 kcal/(m·h·°C). 1 kcal/(m·h·°C) = 1.163 W/(m·K).
