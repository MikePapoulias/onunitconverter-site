About Millifarads per metre

The millifarad per metre (mF/m) equals 10⁻³ F/m. Composite dielectrics near percolation threshold (carbon nanotubes in epoxy) can exhibit effective permittivities in the mF/m range at low frequencies; electrochemical double-layer models treated as a distributed permittivity in thin-film supercapacitor electrodes reach mF/m. 1 mF/m = 10⁻³ F/m = 10³ µF/m.

About Kilofarads per metre

The kilofarad per metre (kF/m) equals 10³ F/m, appearing in theoretical models of heavily screened plasmas and ionic liquids at very low frequencies. The London penetration depth model for superconductors treated as a dielectric gives effective permittivities of 10³-10⁶ F/m in the microwave range; ionic double-layer capacitance per unit length of nanowire electrodes scales to kF/m. 1 kF/m = 10³ F/m.
