About Abfarads per centimetre

The abfarad per centimetre (abF/cm) is the permittivity unit of the CGS-EMU system, equal to 10⁹ F / 0.01 m = 10¹¹ F/m. The abfarad (= 10⁹ F exactly) was the CGS-EMU base capacitance unit; in EMU notation electromagnetic wave propagation uses abF/cm. 1 abF/cm = 10¹¹ F/m = 10⁹ GF/m.

About Megafarads per metre

The megafarad per metre (MF/m) equals 10⁶ F/m, a theoretical permittivity scale for extreme-screening media. Drude model of a free-electron metal below the plasma frequency gives effective permittivity in the MF/m range at audio frequencies; superconducting London kinetic inductance modelled as a dielectric permittivity yields MF/m values in the GHz band. 1 MF/m = 10⁶ F/m = 10³ kF/m.
