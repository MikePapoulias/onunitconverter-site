About Millifarads per metre

The millifarad per metre (mF/m) equals 10⁻³ F/m. Composite dielectrics near percolation threshold (carbon nanotubes in epoxy) can exhibit effective permittivities in the mF/m range at low frequencies; electrochemical double-layer models treated as a distributed permittivity in thin-film supercapacitor electrodes reach mF/m. 1 mF/m = 10⁻³ F/m = 10³ µF/m.

About Statfarads per centimetre

The statfarad per centimetre (statF/cm) is the permittivity unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, exactly equal to 4πε₀ = 10⁷/c² F/m ≈ 1.11265 × 10⁻¹⁰ F/m. In Gaussian units Gauss's law is ∇·E = 4πρ so the permittivity of free space is 1/(4π) statF/cm; the statF/cm is used in older plasma physics and quantum electrodynamics texts. 1 statF/cm ≈ 1.11265 × 10⁻¹⁰ F/m.
