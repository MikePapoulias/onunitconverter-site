About Microfarads per metre

The microfarad per metre (µF/m) equals 10⁻⁶ F/m, the scale for very high-permittivity ferroelectric ceramics and conducting electrodes. Relaxor ferroelectrics near their diffuse phase transition (e.g. PMN-PT) reach εᵣ ~ 10⁶ at low frequencies, giving ε ≈ 8.85 µF/m; effective medium permittivity of heavily loaded carbon-black polymer composites near percolation threshold approaches µF/m. 1 µF/m = 10⁻⁶ F/m.

About Statfarads per centimetre

The statfarad per centimetre (statF/cm) is the permittivity unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, exactly equal to 4πε₀ = 10⁷/c² F/m ≈ 1.11265 × 10⁻¹⁰ F/m. In Gaussian units Gauss's law is ∇·E = 4πρ so the permittivity of free space is 1/(4π) statF/cm; the statF/cm is used in older plasma physics and quantum electrodynamics texts. 1 statF/cm ≈ 1.11265 × 10⁻¹⁰ F/m.
