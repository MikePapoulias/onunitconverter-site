About Femtofarads per metre

The femtofarad per metre (fF/m) equals 10⁻¹⁵ F/m, the smallest practical permittivity scale. Permittivity of near-perfect vacuum regions at sub-pF/m levels, noise-floor permittivity in cryogenic microwave resonators, and modelled permittivity of hypothetical near-zero-index metamaterial layers all span the fF/m range. 1 fF/m = 10⁻¹⁵ F/m = 10⁻³ pF/m.

About Picofarads per centimetre

The picofarad per centimetre (pF/cm) equals 10⁻¹⁰ F/m = 100 pF/m, used for transmission-line capacitance per unit length in RF engineering. A standard 50 Ω air-dielectric coaxial line has capacitance per unit length ≈67 pF/m ≈0.67 pF/cm; PTFE coax gives ≈0.9 pF/cm. RF substrate characterisation often quotes pF/cm. 1 pF/cm = 10⁻¹⁰ F/m.
