About Farads per metre

The farad per metre (F/m) is the SI coherent unit of permittivity. It is an extremely large unit in practice - the vacuum permittivity ε₀ is only 8.854 × 10⁻¹² F/m. Permittivities in F/m arise in models of quasi-metallic electrolytes and ultralow-frequency behaviour of plasma where ionic screening raises the effective dielectric constant enormously. 1 F/m = 10³ mF/m.

About Millifarads per metre

The millifarad per metre (mF/m) equals 10⁻³ F/m. Composite dielectrics near percolation threshold (carbon nanotubes in epoxy) can exhibit effective permittivities in the mF/m range at low frequencies; electrochemical double-layer models treated as a distributed permittivity in thin-film supercapacitor electrodes reach mF/m. 1 mF/m = 10⁻³ F/m = 10³ µF/m.
