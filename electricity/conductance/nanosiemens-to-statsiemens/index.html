About Nanosiemens

The nanosiemens (nS) equals 10⁻⁹ S, the scale for JFET gate leakage, ion-channel patch-clamp measurements, and high-value resistor characterisation. A 1 GΩ resistor presents 1 nS; single open ion channels in biological membranes are quantified in nanosiemens by patch-clamp electrophysiology; environmental soil conductivity sensors use nanosiemens readouts at high resistivity. 1 nS = 10⁻³ µS = 10⁻⁹ S.

About Statsiemens

The statsiemens (statS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1/statΩ = 10⁵/c² S ≈ 1.113 × 10⁻¹² S (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). It is the reciprocal of the statohm and appears in electrostatics and plasma-physics texts where Coulomb's law is written without ε₀; a resistor of 1 statΩ has conductance exactly 1 statS. 1 statS ≈ 1.113 pS.
