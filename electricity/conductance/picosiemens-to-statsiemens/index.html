About Picosiemens

The picosiemens (pS) equals 10⁻¹² S, the practical scale for leakage conductance in MOSFET gate oxides, precision insulators, and high-impedance voltage probes. A 10 GΩ oscilloscope probe presents 100 pS input conductance; reverse-leakage in a silicon signal diode at room temperature is typically 1-10 pS; PTFE cable insulation is specified below 1 pS/m. 1 pS = 10⁻³ nS = 10⁻¹² S.

About Statsiemens

The statsiemens (statS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1/statΩ = 10⁵/c² S ≈ 1.113 × 10⁻¹² S (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). It is the reciprocal of the statohm and appears in electrostatics and plasma-physics texts where Coulomb's law is written without ε₀; a resistor of 1 statΩ has conductance exactly 1 statS. 1 statS ≈ 1.113 pS.
