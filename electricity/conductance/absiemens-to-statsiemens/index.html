About Absiemens

The absiemens (abS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-EMU system, equal to 1/abΩ = 1/(10⁻⁹ Ω) = 10⁹ S, exactly identical to 1 GS in SI. It appears in nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century electromagnetic engineering texts; 1 abS corresponds to the conductance of a 1 nΩ connection typical of large cryogenically cooled bus joints or welded thick-copper structures. 1 abS = 10⁹ S = 1 GS.

About Statsiemens

The statsiemens (statS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1/statΩ = 10⁵/c² S ≈ 1.113 × 10⁻¹² S (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). It is the reciprocal of the statohm and appears in electrostatics and plasma-physics texts where Coulomb's law is written without ε₀; a resistor of 1 statΩ has conductance exactly 1 statS. 1 statS ≈ 1.113 pS.
