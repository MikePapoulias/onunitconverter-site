About Kilosiemens

The kilosiemens (kS) equals 10³ S, encountered in high-conductance metallic connections and power-semiconductor on-state characterisation. A large copper busbar of 100 mm² cross-section and 1 m length has conductance of about 60 kS; high-power IGBT modules with Rₛₒₙ below 1 mΩ present conductances exceeding 1 kS; arc furnace electrodes exhibit kilosiemens-range contact conductance. 1 kS = 10³ S = 10⁻³ MS.

About Terasiemens

The terasiemens (TS) equals 10¹² S, the conductance equivalent of a 1 pΩ resistance. No bulk conductor approaches terasiemens conductance at room temperature - even a 1 m³ copper block has conductance of about 60 GS; terasiemens figures arise in theoretical treatments of macroscopic superconducting structures and in conductor-scaling extrapolations. 1 TS = 10¹² S = 10³ GS.
