About Terasiemens

The terasiemens (TS) equals 10¹² S, the conductance equivalent of a 1 pΩ resistance. No bulk conductor approaches terasiemens conductance at room temperature - even a 1 m³ copper block has conductance of about 60 GS; terasiemens figures arise in theoretical treatments of macroscopic superconducting structures and in conductor-scaling extrapolations. 1 TS = 10¹² S = 10³ GS.

About Absiemens

The absiemens (abS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-EMU system, equal to 1/abΩ = 1/(10⁻⁹ Ω) = 10⁹ S, exactly identical to 1 GS in SI. It appears in nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century electromagnetic engineering texts; 1 abS corresponds to the conductance of a 1 nΩ connection typical of large cryogenically cooled bus joints or welded thick-copper structures. 1 abS = 10⁹ S = 1 GS.
