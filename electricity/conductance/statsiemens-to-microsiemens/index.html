About Statsiemens

The statsiemens (statS) is the conductance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1/statΩ = 10⁵/c² S ≈ 1.113 × 10⁻¹² S (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). It is the reciprocal of the statohm and appears in electrostatics and plasma-physics texts where Coulomb's law is written without ε₀; a resistor of 1 statΩ has conductance exactly 1 statS. 1 statS ≈ 1.113 pS.

About Microsiemens

The microsiemens (µS) equals 10⁻⁶ S, the standard unit for water-quality conductivity measurement and analogue signal-path impedance. Drinking water has conductivity of 50-500 µS/cm; a 1 MΩ resistor presents 1 µS; conductivity meters for hydroponics and aquaculture read directly in microsiemens or millisiemens per centimetre. 1 µS = 10⁻³ mS = 10⁻⁶ S.
