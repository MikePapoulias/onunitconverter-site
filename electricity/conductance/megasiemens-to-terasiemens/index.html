About Megasiemens

The megasiemens (MS) equals 10⁶ S, the scale for very-low-resistance metallic structures such as thick busbars and short heavy-gauge connectors. A 10 cm long, 1 cm² copper bar has conductance of roughly 600 MS; aluminium overhead grid feeders have per-span conductances in the megasiemens range; conductance of grain boundaries in polycrystalline conductors at cryogenic temperatures reaches megasiemens. 1 MS = 10⁶ S = 10³ kS.

About Terasiemens

The terasiemens (TS) equals 10¹² S, the conductance equivalent of a 1 pΩ resistance. No bulk conductor approaches terasiemens conductance at room temperature - even a 1 m³ copper block has conductance of about 60 GS; terasiemens figures arise in theoretical treatments of macroscopic superconducting structures and in conductor-scaling extrapolations. 1 TS = 10¹² S = 10³ GS.
