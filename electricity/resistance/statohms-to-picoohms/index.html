About Statohms

The statohm (statΩ) is the resistance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1 statvolt per statampere = c² × 10⁻⁵ Ω ≈ 898.755 GΩ (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). In Gaussian CGS Ohm's law takes the form R [statΩ] = V [statV] / I [statA]; the statohm appears in classical electrodynamics and plasma physics texts alongside other Gaussian-CGS units. 1 statΩ ≈ 898.755 GΩ ≈ 0.899 TΩ.

About Picoohms

The picoohm (pΩ) equals 10⁻¹² Ω, used for ultra-low resistance measurements in superconductors and precision four-wire metrology. A superconducting wire transitioning through its critical temperature shows residual resistance at the picoohm level; high-precision Josephson-array resistance bridges operated at national metrology institutes resolve differences below 1 pΩ. 1 pΩ = 10⁻³ nΩ = 10⁻¹² Ω.
