About Statohms

The statohm (statΩ) is the resistance unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1 statvolt per statampere = c² × 10⁻⁵ Ω ≈ 898.755 GΩ (exact, where c = 299 792 458 m/s). In Gaussian CGS Ohm's law takes the form R [statΩ] = V [statV] / I [statA]; the statohm appears in classical electrodynamics and plasma physics texts alongside other Gaussian-CGS units. 1 statΩ ≈ 898.755 GΩ ≈ 0.899 TΩ.

About Megaohms

The megaohm (MΩ) equals 10⁶ Ω, the scale of insulation testing and high-impedance analogue circuit design. Insulation testers (meggers) measure cable and motor winding insulation from 0.1-1000 MΩ; pH electrode output impedance is 10-500 MΩ; precision op-amp feedback networks use 1-10 MΩ resistors to set very high signal-source impedance. 1 MΩ = 10⁶ Ω = 10³ kΩ.
