About Microohms

The microohm (µΩ) equals 10⁻⁶ Ω, the practical scale for contact resistance testing, bus-bar joints, and circuit-breaker contacts. A well-made bolted bus-bar connection has contact resistance below 50 µΩ; a 25 mm² copper conductor has resistance of about 0.7 µΩ/mm; IEC 60947 specifies acceptance limits for switchgear contacts in microohms. 1 µΩ = 10⁻³ mΩ = 10³ nΩ.

About Kilohms

The kilohm (kΩ) equals 10³ Ω, covering the most common range of discrete resistors in electronics. Pull-up and pull-down resistors are typically 1-10 kΩ; potentiometer tracks run 1-100 kΩ; input-bias resistors in transistor amplifiers and voltage-divider networks are almost always in the kilohm range. 1 kΩ = 10³ Ω = 10⁻³ MΩ.
