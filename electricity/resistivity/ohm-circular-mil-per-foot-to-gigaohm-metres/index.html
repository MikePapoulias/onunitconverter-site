Conversion Formula

GΩ·m = Ω·cmil/ft × 1.662426113e-18

About Ohm circular mil per foot

The ohm circular mil per foot (Ω·cmil/ft) is the US customary resistivity unit for wires and cables, equal to π×6.4516×10⁻¹⁰/(4×0.3048) Ω·m ≈ 1.66243×10⁻⁹ Ω·m. Copper has resistivity ≈1.012 Ω·cmil/ft; wire tables in NFPA 70 (NEC) and ASTM B3 list conductor resistivity in this unit. 1 Ω·cmil/ft ≈ 1.66243 nΩ·m.

About Gigaohm-metres

The gigaohm-metre (GΩ·m) equals 10⁹ Ω·m, the scale for excellent insulating materials and ultra-pure liquids. Fused silica (quartz glass) has resistivity ≈ 10⁴-10⁵ GΩ·m at room temperature; ultra-pure deionised water is ≈18 MΩ·cm = 180 kΩ·m; synthetic sapphire is ≈1-10⁴ GΩ·m depending on temperature. 1 GΩ·m = 10⁹ Ω·m = 10³ MΩ·m.
