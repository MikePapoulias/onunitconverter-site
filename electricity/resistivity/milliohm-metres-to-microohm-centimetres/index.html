About Milliohm-metres

The milliohm-metre (mΩ·m) equals 10⁻³ Ω·m, the scale for highly resistive alloys, graphite, and transition-metal oxides. Carbon-graphite brushes used in DC motors have resistivity of 20-100 mΩ·m; ferrites used in power magnetics are 0.1-10 mΩ·m; polyaniline conducting polymer films are in the mΩ·m range when fully protonated. 1 mΩ·m = 10⁻³ Ω·m = 10 Ω·cm.

About Microohm-centimetres

The microohm-centimetre (µΩ·cm) equals 10⁻⁸ Ω·m = 10 nΩ·m, the traditional unit for bulk metal resistivity in materials science. Copper is 1.68 µΩ·cm; tungsten 5.6 µΩ·cm; platinum 10.6 µΩ·cm; CRC Handbook tables still list metal resistivities in µΩ·cm. 1 µΩ·cm = 10⁻⁸ Ω·m = 10 nΩ·m.
