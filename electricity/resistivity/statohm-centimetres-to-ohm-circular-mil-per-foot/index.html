About Statohm-centimetres

The statohm-centimetre (statΩ·cm) is the resistivity unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to c²×10⁻⁷ Ω·m ≈ 898.755 Ω·m where c = 299 792 458 m/s. In Gaussian units Ohm’s law is J = σE (no 4π factors for conduction); the statΩ·cm is the natural resistivity unit when fields are in statV/cm and currents in statA/cm². 1 statΩ·cm ≈ 898.755 Ω·m.

About Ohm circular mil per foot

The ohm circular mil per foot (Ω·cmil/ft) is the US customary resistivity unit for wires and cables, equal to π×6.4516×10⁻¹⁰/(4×0.3048) Ω·m ≈ 1.66243×10⁻⁹ Ω·m. Copper has resistivity ≈1.012 Ω·cmil/ft; wire tables in NFPA 70 (NEC) and ASTM B3 list conductor resistivity in this unit. 1 Ω·cmil/ft ≈ 1.66243 nΩ·m.
