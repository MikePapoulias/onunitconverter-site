About Microhenries

The microhenry (µH) equals 10⁻⁶ H, the standard unit for power-electronics inductors, EMI filter chokes, and AM radio coils. DC-DC converter inductors in smartphones are 1-100 µH; ferrite-core EMI common-mode chokes are 10-10 000 µH; AM broadcast loop antennas are 200-500 µH; automotive ignition coil secondary inductances are a few µH. 1 µH = 10⁻³ mH = 10⁻⁶ H.

About Nanohenries

The nanohenry (nH) equals 10⁻⁹ H, the everyday unit for RF inductors, PCB trace inductance, and package parasitics. A 10 mm PCB microstrip trace is about 10 nH; surface-mount RF chokes range from 1 to 1 000 nH; bond-wire lead inductances are 1-20 nH; resonant LC tank circuits for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi use inductors of 1-100 nH. 1 nH = 10⁻³ µH = 10⁻⁹ H.
