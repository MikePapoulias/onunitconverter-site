About Terahenries

The terahenry (TH) equals 10¹² H. No physical inductor or coil approaches 1 TH; the value is used in dimensional scaling of cosmic electromagnetic phenomena (e.g., inductance of galactic current sheets) and in theoretical discussions of extreme-value electromagnetic parameters. The stathenry is ≈ 0.899 TH, placing stathenries and terahenries in the same order of magnitude. 1 TH = 10³ GH = 10¹² H.

About Picohenries

The picohenry (pH) equals 10⁻¹² H, the practical unit for short PCB traces, decoupling capacitor equivalent series inductance (ESL), and bond-wire inductance in RF packages. A 1 mm PCB trace (50 Ω microstrip) has about 1 nH/mm; SMD bypass capacitors have ESL of 0.1-2 nH; gold bondwires are 1-5 pH/mm. IC package lead inductances are typically 1-10 nH = 1 000-10 000 pH. 1 pH = 10⁻³ nH = 10⁻¹² H.
