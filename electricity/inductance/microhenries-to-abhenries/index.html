About Microhenries

The microhenry (µH) equals 10⁻⁶ H, the standard unit for power-electronics inductors, EMI filter chokes, and AM radio coils. DC-DC converter inductors in smartphones are 1-100 µH; ferrite-core EMI common-mode chokes are 10-10 000 µH; AM broadcast loop antennas are 200-500 µH; automotive ignition coil secondary inductances are a few µH. 1 µH = 10⁻³ mH = 10⁻⁶ H.

About Abhenries

The abhenry (abH) is the inductance unit of the CGS-EMU system, equal to 1 abΩ × 1 s = 10⁻⁹ Ω × 1 s = 10⁻⁹ H, exactly equal to 1 nH in SI. It appears in nineteenth- and early-twentieth-century electromagnetic engineering texts alongside abohms and abamperes; the abhenry was the standard inductance unit in EMU telegraphy and early radio literature. 1 abH = 10⁻⁹ H = 1 nH.
