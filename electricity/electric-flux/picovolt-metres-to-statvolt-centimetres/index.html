About Picovolt-metres

The picovolt-metre (pV·m) equals 10⁻¹² V·m. Electrostatic flux through the gate dielectric area of a MOSFET at sub-threshold bias is in the pV·m range; flux variations detected by precision Kelvin-probe force microscopy over nanoscale surface patches are in the pV·m to nV·m range. 1 pV·m = 10⁻¹² V·m = 10⁻³ nV·m.

About Statvolt-centimetres

The statvolt-centimetre (statV·cm) is the electric flux unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to c × 10⁻⁸ V·m ≈ 2.99792458 V·m where c = 299 792 458 m/s. In Gaussian units Gauss's law is ∇·E = 4πρ (no ε₀); the statV·cm is the natural flux unit when fields are in statV/cm and areas in cm². 1 statV·cm ≈ 2.998 V·m.
