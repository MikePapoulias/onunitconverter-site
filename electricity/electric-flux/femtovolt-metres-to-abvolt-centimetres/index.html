About Femtovolt-metres

The femtovolt-metre (fV·m) equals 10⁻¹⁵ V·m, the smallest practical scale for electric flux. Shot noise-limited flux measurements in single-electron transistors and nanoscale sensors operate in the fV·m range; flux through sub-micron apertures exposed to extremely weak quasi-static fields spans fV·m to pV·m. 1 fV·m = 10⁻¹⁵ V·m = 10⁻³ pV·m.

About Abvolt-centimetres

The abvolt-centimetre (abV·cm) is the electric flux unit of the CGS-EMU system, equal to 10⁻⁸ V × 0.01 m = 10⁻¹⁰ V·m. In the EMU system Gauss's law is applied with charge in abcoulombs and fields in abV/cm; the abV·cm numerically bridges the pV·m and nV·m scales. 1 abV·cm = 10⁻¹⁰ V·m = 0.1 nV·m.
