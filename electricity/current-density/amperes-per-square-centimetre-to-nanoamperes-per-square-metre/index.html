About Amperes per square centimetre

The ampere per square centimetre (A/cm²) equals 10⁴ A/m², the standard current density unit for power electronics, arc physics, and high-performance batteries. Bipolar junction transistor saturation current density is 0.5-5 A/cm²; vacuum arc contact spots on copper carry 100-1000 A/cm²; fast-charge lithium-ion cathodes support up to 0.05 A/cm². 1 A/cm² = 10⁴ A/m² = 10 kA/m².

About Nanoamperes per square metre

The nanoampere per square metre (nA/m²) equals 10⁻⁹ A/m². Corrosion current density of passive stainless steel in neutral chloride-free solutions is 0.1-10 nA/m²; exchange current density of the hydrogen evolution reaction on platinum in acid is around 1 nA/m²; photocurrent density in very weakly illuminated organic solar cells scales to nA/m². 1 nA/m² = 10⁻⁹ A/m² = 10⁻³ µA/m².
