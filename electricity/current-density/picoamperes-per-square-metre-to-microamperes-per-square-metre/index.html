About Picoamperes per square metre

The picoampere per square metre (pA/m²) equals 10⁻¹² A/m². Reverse-bias dark current density in silicon p-n junctions at room temperature is 1-100 pA/m²; gate leakage current density in sub-22 nm CMOS transistors spans pA/m² to nA/m²; dark current density of cooled InGaAs photodiodes is in the pA/m² range. 1 pA/m² = 10⁻¹² A/m² = 10⁻³ nA/m².

About Microamperes per square metre

The microampere per square metre (µA/m²) equals 10⁻⁶ A/m². Corrosion current density of carbon steel in neutral deaerated water is 0.1-100 µA/m²; exchange current density of the Zn/Zn2+ electrode is ≈20 µA/m²; cathodic protection criterion current density for buried steel pipelines in low-resistivity soil is 2-20 µA/m². 1 µA/m² = 10⁻⁶ A/m² = 10⁻³ mA/m².
