Conversion Formula

About Statamperes per square centimetre

The statampere per square centimetre (statA/cm²) is the current density unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 1000/c A/m² where c = 299 792 458 m/s, giving ≈3.336 × 10⁻⁶ A/m². In Gaussian electrodynamics the statA/cm² is the natural current density when fields are in statV/cm; it appears in plasma physics texts using Gaussian units. 1 statA/cm² ≈ 3.336 µA/m².

About Milliamperes per square metre

The milliampere per square metre (mA/m²) equals 10⁻³ A/m². Cathodic protection current density applied to reinforced concrete structures is 2-20 mA/m²; faradaic current density during slow anodic dissolution of iron in dilute H2SO4 is 1-100 mA/m²; exchange current density of the oxygen reduction reaction on platinum is ≈2 mA/m². 1 mA/m² = 10⁻³ A/m² = 0.1 µA/cm².
