About Megafarads

The megafarad (MF) equals 10⁶ F, a scale not reached by any discrete capacitor available today. Theoretical projections of utility-scale ultracapacitor arrays for grid energy storage discuss megafarad totals; the Earth-ionosphere spherical capacitor is estimated at about 0.9 F, so MF is well beyond any natural or engineered capacitor at present. 1 MF = 10³ kF = 10⁶ F.

About Kilofarads

The kilofarad (kF) equals 10³ F, reached only by large supercapacitor assemblies for energy buffering. Industrial supercapacitor modules for tram and metro regenerative braking are rated 500-10 000 F = 0.5-10 kF; hybrid-bus energy buffers stack 3 000 F cells to achieve several kilofarads; grid fast-response voltage-stabilisation banks are discussed in the kilofarad range. 1 kF = 10³ F = 10⁻³ MF.
