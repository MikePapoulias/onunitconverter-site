About Coulombs per cubic foot

The coulomb per cubic foot (C/ft³) equals exactly 1/(0.3048)³ C/m³ ≈35.3147 C/m³, used in North American materials testing and industrial electrostatics standards. Electrostatic charge density in polymer foam packaging is quoted in C/ft³ in ASTM standards; ESD-protective bag and tray charge retention is rated in C/ft³ in US industrial specifications. 1 C/ft³ ≈35.315 C/m³.

About Coulombs per cubic inch

The coulomb per cubic inch (C/in³) equals exactly 1/(0.0254)³ C/m³ ≈61 023.7 C/m³, used in US semiconductor and battery engineering. Volumetric charge capacity of lithium-ion battery electrodes is quoted in C/in³ in North American product datasheets; solid-state electrolyte charge density specifications in US engineering standards appear in C/in³. 1 C/in³ ≈61 024 C/m³ = 61.024 kC/m³.
