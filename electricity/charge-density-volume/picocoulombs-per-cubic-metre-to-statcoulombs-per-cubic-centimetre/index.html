About Picocoulombs per cubic metre

The picocoulomb per cubic metre (pC/m³) equals 10⁻¹² C/m³, the scale for solar wind charge density and fair-weather atmospheric electricity. The solar wind near Earth has electron density ~5×10⁶ /m³, giving charge density ≈0.8 pC/m³; fair-weather atmospheric space charge near sea level is 1-10 pC/m³. 1 pC/m³ = 10⁻¹² C/m³ = 10⁻³ nC/m³.

About Statcoulombs per cubic centimetre

The statcoulomb per cubic centimetre (statC/cm³) is the volume charge density unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 10⁵/c C/m³ where c = 299 792 458 m/s, giving ≈3.336 × 10⁻⁴ C/m³. Gaussian electrostatics expresses Gauss’s law as ∇·E = 4πρ (no ε₀ factor); the statC/cm³ is the natural ρ unit when fields are in statV/cm. 1 statC/cm³ ≈ 0.3336 mC/m³.
