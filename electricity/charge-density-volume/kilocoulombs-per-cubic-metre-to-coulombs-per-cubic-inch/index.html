Conversion Formula

C/in³ = kC/m³ × 0.016387064

About Kilocoulombs per cubic metre

The kilocoulomb per cubic metre (kC/m³) equals 10³ C/m³, the scale for heavily doped semiconductor depletion layers and electrochemical supercapacitor electrodes. Silicon doped at ~6×10²¹ /m³ has depletion charge density ≈1 kC/m³; porous carbon electrodes in electric double-layer capacitors store 1-10 kC/m³ of volumetric charge. 1 kC/m³ = 10³ C/m³ = 10⁻³ MC/m³.

About Coulombs per cubic inch

The coulomb per cubic inch (C/in³) equals exactly 1/(0.0254)³ C/m³ ≈61 023.7 C/m³, used in US semiconductor and battery engineering. Volumetric charge capacity of lithium-ion battery electrodes is quoted in C/in³ in North American product datasheets; solid-state electrolyte charge density specifications in US engineering standards appear in C/in³. 1 C/in³ ≈61 024 C/m³ = 61.024 kC/m³.
