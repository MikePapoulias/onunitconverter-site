About Nanocoulombs per cubic metre

The nanocoulomb per cubic metre (nC/m³) equals 10⁻⁹ C/m³, the scale for thundercloud volume charge and laboratory low-pressure plasmas. The main negative charge region of a mature thunderstorm has volume charge density of 1-5 nC/m³; glow discharge plasma at ~1 Pa has space charge density in the nC/m³ to µC/m³ range. 1 nC/m³ = 10⁻⁹ C/m³ = 10⁻³ µC/m³.

About Millicoulombs per cubic metre

The millicoulomb per cubic metre (mC/m³) equals 10⁻³ C/m³, the scale for space charge in streamer discharge channels and moderately doped semiconductor depletion regions. Silicon doped at ~6×10¹⁵ /m³ carries depletion charge density ≈1 mC/m³; streamer plasma cores during lightning leader propagation have net charge densities of 0.1-10 mC/m³. 1 mC/m³ = 10⁻³ C/m³ = 10³ µC/m³.
