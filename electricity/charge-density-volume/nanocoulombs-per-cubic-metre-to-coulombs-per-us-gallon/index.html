About Nanocoulombs per cubic metre

The nanocoulomb per cubic metre (nC/m³) equals 10⁻⁹ C/m³, the scale for thundercloud volume charge and laboratory low-pressure plasmas. The main negative charge region of a mature thunderstorm has volume charge density of 1-5 nC/m³; glow discharge plasma at ~1 Pa has space charge density in the nC/m³ to µC/m³ range. 1 nC/m³ = 10⁻⁹ C/m³ = 10⁻³ µC/m³.

About Coulombs per US gallon

The coulomb per US gallon (C/gal) equals exactly 1/(231×(0.0254)³) C/m³ ≈264.172 C/m³, used in electrochemical water-treatment and flow-battery engineering in North America. Electrocoagulation systems are specified by charge dosage in C/gal (US) of treated water; flow battery electrolyte charge state is monitored in C/gal in North American plant documentation. 1 C/gal (US) ≈264.172 C/m³.
