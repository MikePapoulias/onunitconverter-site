Conversion Formula

About Milliampere-hours

The milliampere-hour (mAh) equals exactly 3.6 C and is the universal capacity unit for portable-electronics batteries. Smartphone batteries range from 3 000-5 000 mAh; AA alkaline cells deliver ≈2 500 mAh; small lithium coin cells for RTC modules are rated 35-225 mAh. 1 mAh = 3.6 C = 10⁻³ Ah.

About Kilocoulombs

The kilocoulomb (kC) equals 10³ C ≈ 0.278 Ah, used in industrial electrochemistry, large capacitor bank energy calculations, and fault current analysis. Electroplating a 100 cm² nickel layer 25 µm thick requires roughly 2-4 kC; power-factor-correction capacitor banks in substations may store several kilocoulombs. 1 kC = 10⁻³ MC = 10³ C.
