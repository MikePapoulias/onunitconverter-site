About Nanocoulombs per square metre

The nanocoulomb per square metre (nC/m²) equals 10⁻⁹ C/m², the scale for charge density on moderately charged electret films and LCD alignment-layer surfaces. Commercial cellular polypropylene electret microphones carry surface charge densities of 0.1-10 nC/m²; charge on liquid-crystal alignment layers affects pixel response times. 1 nC/m² = 10⁻⁹ C/m² = 10⁻³ µC/m².

About Statcoulombs per square centimetre

The statcoulomb per square centimetre (statC/cm²) is the surface charge density unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 10³/c C/m² where c = 299 792 458 m/s, giving ≈ 3.336 × 10⁻⁶ C/m². In Gaussian units Gauss's law reads E = 4πσ (no ε₀ factor); 1 statC/cm² produces a surface field of 4π statV/cm. 1 statC/cm² ≈ 3.336 µC/m².
