About Gigacoulombs per square metre

The gigacoulomb per square metre (GC/m²) equals 10⁹ C/m², a value with no engineering realisation, used only in astrophysical and theoretical plasma models. Magnetar magnetosphere charge sheet densities, pulsar polar cap surface charge, and theoretical cosmic string surface charge are described at GC/m² scales in research literature. 1 GC/m² = 10⁹ C/m² = 10³ MC/m².

About Kilocoulombs per square metre

The kilocoulomb per square metre (kC/m²) equals 10³ C/m², the scale for electrochemical processes involving heavy plating, electrolytic metal dissolution, and pulsed-power surface ablation. Thick copper electroforming runs accumulate kC/m²-scale charge; electrochemical machining (ECM) removes material at multi-kC/m² charge densities; large electrolytic capacitor anode foils are etched at kC/m². 1 kC/m² = 10³ C/m².
