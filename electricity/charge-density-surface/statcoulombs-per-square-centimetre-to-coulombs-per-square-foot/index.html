Conversion Formula

C/ft² = statC/cm² × 3.098911848e-7

About Statcoulombs per square centimetre

The statcoulomb per square centimetre (statC/cm²) is the surface charge density unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 10³/c C/m² where c = 299 792 458 m/s, giving ≈ 3.336 × 10⁻⁶ C/m². In Gaussian units Gauss's law reads E = 4πσ (no ε₀ factor); 1 statC/cm² produces a surface field of 4π statV/cm. 1 statC/cm² ≈ 3.336 µC/m².

About Coulombs per square foot

The coulomb per square foot (C/ft²) equals exactly 1/(0.3048)² C/m² ≈ 10.7639 C/m², used in North American electrostatic specifications for insulating flooring, workbench surfaces, and static-dissipative materials. ANSI/ESD standards specify electrostatic charge decay and surface charge limits in C/ft²; antistatic mat and flooring performance is rated in C/ft². 1 C/ft² ≈ 10.764 C/m².
