About Microcoulombs per square centimetre

The microcoulomb per square centimetre (µC/cm²) equals exactly 10⁻² C/m² = 0.01 C/m², used in electrophotography and toner transfer engineering. Laser printer photoconductor drums are charged to 0.1-1 µC/cm² before exposure; toner particle charge-to-mass ratios are calibrated against µC/cm²-scale drum charge; ion implantation dose uniformity is verified at µC/cm² levels. 1 µC/cm² = 0.01 C/m².

About Gigacoulombs per square metre

The gigacoulomb per square metre (GC/m²) equals 10⁹ C/m², a value with no engineering realisation, used only in astrophysical and theoretical plasma models. Magnetar magnetosphere charge sheet densities, pulsar polar cap surface charge, and theoretical cosmic string surface charge are described at GC/m² scales in research literature. 1 GC/m² = 10⁹ C/m² = 10³ MC/m².
