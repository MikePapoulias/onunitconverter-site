About Statcoulombs per square centimetre

The statcoulomb per square centimetre (statC/cm²) is the surface charge density unit of the CGS-Gaussian system, equal to 10³/c C/m² where c = 299 792 458 m/s, giving ≈ 3.336 × 10⁻⁶ C/m². In Gaussian units Gauss's law reads E = 4πσ (no ε₀ factor); 1 statC/cm² produces a surface field of 4π statV/cm. 1 statC/cm² ≈ 3.336 µC/m².

About Attocoulombs per square metre

The attocoulomb per square metre (aC/m²) equals 10⁻¹⁸ C/m², the scale for areal charge density on individual molecular monolayers measured by Kelvin probe force microscopy. A single elementary charge on a 1 µm² area gives about 0.16 aC/m²; contact potential distributions in organic photovoltaic blends correspond to aC/m²-scale surface charge. 1 aC/m² = 10⁻¹⁸ C/m² = 10⁻³ fC/m².
