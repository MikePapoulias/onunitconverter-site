About Petabytes

A petabyte (PB) = 10¹⁵ bytes (SI). Major platforms operate at PB scale: Meta ingests > 4 PB/day; YouTube adds several PB of video content daily. CERN Run 3 produced ≈ 90 PB in 2022. 1,000-genome cohort (WGS at 30×) ≈ 0.4 PB. English Wikipedia with media ≈ 10 PB. A rack of Blu-ray cold storage (Facebook Project Amber) holds 1 PB. 1 PB = 10¹⁵ bytes = 1,000 TB.

About Tebibits

A tebibit (Tibit) = 2⁴⁰ = 1,099,511,627,776 bits (IEC 80000-13). JEDEC NAND roadmap targets: TLC at 300+ layers (2025-2026) aims for 1-2 Tibit per die. Samsung V-NAND 8th gen (2024) targets 1 Tibit/die; 2 TiB per stacked package. HBM3e stacked memory full roadmap projects 4 Tibit HBM stacks post-2030. 1 Tibit = 2⁴⁰ bits = 128 GiB.
