About Petabytes

A petabyte (PB) = 10¹⁵ bytes (SI). Major platforms operate at PB scale: Meta ingests > 4 PB/day; YouTube adds several PB of video content daily. CERN Run 3 produced ≈ 90 PB in 2022. 1,000-genome cohort (WGS at 30×) ≈ 0.4 PB. English Wikipedia with media ≈ 10 PB. A rack of Blu-ray cold storage (Facebook Project Amber) holds 1 PB. 1 PB = 10¹⁵ bytes = 1,000 TB.

About Yottabytes

A yottabyte (YB) = 10²⁴ bytes (SI) - among the largest original SI named powers. Global annual data generation forecast ≈ 175 ZB = 0.000175 YB by 2025 (IDC) - a yottabyte equals ≈ 5,700 years of data production at 2025 rates. Claims of multi-YB NSA data centres were debunked; credible estimates: 3-100 EB. Erasing 1 YB at the Landauer limit (300 K) would require ≈ 182 J - enough to heat 0.4 mL of water by 1°C. 1 YB = 10²⁴ bytes = 1,000 ZB.
