About Mebibits per second

Mebibit per second (Mibit/s) = 2²⁰ = 1,048,576 bit/s (IEC 80000-13). Used in FPGA-to-memory interface specs and JEDEC memory bus timing documents where bit rates are derived from 2ⁿ clock frequencies. A 100 MHz 8-bit parallel bus = 800 Mibit/s. Contrasted with Mbit/s (= 10⁶ bit/s). 1 Mibit/s = 1,048,576 bit/s ≈ 131 KB/s.

About Zebibytes per second

Zebibyte per second (ZiB/s) = 2⁷⁰ B/s ≈ 1.181 × 10²¹ B/s (IEC 80000-13). Global internet traffic in 2024 ≈ 5 ZB/year; at 1 ZiB/s the year's traffic transfers in ≈ 5 seconds. LHC ATLAS/CMS raw detector output ≈ 1 PB/s - six orders below 1 ZiB/s. Purely cosmological. 1 ZiB/s = 2⁷⁰ B/s ≈ 8 Zibit/s.
