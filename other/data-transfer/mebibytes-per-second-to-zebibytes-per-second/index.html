About Mebibytes per second

Mebibyte per second (MiB/s) = 2²⁰ = 1,048,576 B/s (IEC 80000-13). Used in Linux fio/dd benchmark output. Optical disc speeds: CD-ROM 1× = 150 KiB/s; 52× ≈ 7.62 MiB/s; Blu-ray 1× ≈ 4.39 MiB/s; 12× Blu-ray ≈ 52.73 MiB/s. SATA II effective throughput ≈ 286 MiB/s. microSD A1 spec = 10 MB/s ≈ 9.54 MiB/s. 1 MiB/s = 1,048,576 B/s = 8 Mibit/s.

About Zebibytes per second

Zebibyte per second (ZiB/s) = 2⁷⁰ B/s ≈ 1.181 × 10²¹ B/s (IEC 80000-13). Global internet traffic in 2024 ≈ 5 ZB/year; at 1 ZiB/s the year's traffic transfers in ≈ 5 seconds. LHC ATLAS/CMS raw detector output ≈ 1 PB/s - six orders below 1 ZiB/s. Purely cosmological. 1 ZiB/s = 2⁷⁰ B/s ≈ 8 Zibit/s.
