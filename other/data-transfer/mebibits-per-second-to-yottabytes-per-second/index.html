About Mebibits per second

Mebibit per second (Mibit/s) = 2²⁰ = 1,048,576 bit/s (IEC 80000-13). Used in FPGA-to-memory interface specs and JEDEC memory bus timing documents where bit rates are derived from 2ⁿ clock frequencies. A 100 MHz 8-bit parallel bus = 800 Mibit/s. Contrasted with Mbit/s (= 10⁶ bit/s). 1 Mibit/s = 1,048,576 bit/s ≈ 131 KB/s.

About Yottabytes per second

Yottabyte per second (YB/s) = 10²⁴ B/s (SI). The largest named SI byte-rate. The entire global datasphere created from the beginning of history through 2023 (≈ 120 ZB) could be transferred in ≈ 0.00012 seconds at 1 YB/s. Bremermann's limit ≈ 1.36 × 10⁵⁰ bit/s per kg - a microgram of matter at this limit processes ≈ 10²⁸ Ybit/s. 1 YB/s = 10²⁴ B/s = 8 Ybit/s.
