About Zebibits per second

Zebibit per second (Zibit/s) = 2⁷⁰ bit/s ≈ 1.181 × 10²¹ bit/s (IEC 80000-13). A 1 Zibit/s link would transmit the entire 2024 annual global internet traffic (≈ 40 Zbit) in ≈ 40 seconds. Single caesium atom oscillates at ≈ 9.2 GHz; reaching 1 Zibit/s by atomic oscillation would require ≈ 10¹¹ atomic channels. Theoretical unit only. 1 Zibit/s = 2⁷⁰ bit/s ≈ 128 EiB/s.

About Exabits per second

Exabit per second (Ebit/s) = 10¹⁸ bit/s (SI). Theoretical: Shannon limit for a single-mode fibre over 50 THz optical bandwidth ≈ 100 Pbit/s - still ~10,000× below 1 Ebit/s. Reaching 1 Ebit/s would require ≈1 million parallel fibres each at 1 Pbit/s. Global internet backbone installed capacity in 2024 ≈ 5-10 Pbit/s. 1 Ebit/s = 10¹⁸ bit/s = 125 PB/s.
