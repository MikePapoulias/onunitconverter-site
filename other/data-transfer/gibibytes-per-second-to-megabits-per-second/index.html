About Gibibytes per second

Gibibyte per second (GiB/s) = 2³⁰ B/s (IEC 80000-13). Precision unit for STREAM benchmark and LIKWID memory bandwidth measurements. Intel Xeon Platinum 8380 dual-socket STREAM ≈ 400 GiB/s. RTX 4090 GDDR6X = 1,008 GB/s ≈ 939 GiB/s. PCIe 5.0 ×16 = 64 GB/s ≈ 59.6 GiB/s. AMD EPYC Infinity Fabric (4-socket) ≈ 576 GiB/s cross-die. 1 GiB/s = 2³⁰ B/s = 8 Gibit/s.

About Megabits per second

Megabit per second (Mbit/s) = 10⁶ bit/s (SI). Standard consumer broadband unit: ADSL2+ up to 24 Mbit/s; VDSL2 up to 100 Mbit/s; Wi-Fi 6 up to 9,608 Mbit/s; Gigabit Ethernet = 1,000 Mbit/s. Streaming: Netflix 1080p = 5 Mbit/s; 4K HDR = 15-25 Mbit/s; 4K Blu-ray up to 128 Mbit/s. ISP 100 Mbit/s ≈ 12.5 MB/s download. 1 Mbit/s = 10⁶ bit/s = 125 KB/s.
