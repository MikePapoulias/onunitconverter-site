About Tebibits per second

Tebibit per second (Tibit/s) = 2⁴⁰ bit/s (IEC 80000-13). Used in exascale HPC academic papers for memory bandwidth: AMD MI300A APU = 5.3 TB/s ≈ 4.82 TiB/s = 38.6 Tibit/s. NVIDIA H100 HBM3 = 3.35 TB/s ≈ 24.5 Tibit/s. GPU-GPU NVLink 4.0 = 900 GB/s ≈ 6.55 Tibit/s. 1 Tibit/s = 2⁴⁰ bit/s = 128 GiB/s.

About Yobibits per second

Yobibit per second (Yibit/s) = 2⁸⁰ bit/s ≈ 1.209 × 10²⁴ bit/s (IEC 80000-13; 2022 revision adds ronbi Ri=2⁹⁰ and quetti Qi=2¹⁰⁰). Bremermann's limit: 1 kg of matter at maximum theoretical compute rate handles ≈ 1.36 × 10⁵⁰ bit/s - about 10²⁶ Yibit/s - showing 1 Yibit/s is not prohibited by physics but is meaningless for practical data transfer. 1 Yibit/s = 2⁸⁰ bit/s ≈ 128 ZiB/s.
