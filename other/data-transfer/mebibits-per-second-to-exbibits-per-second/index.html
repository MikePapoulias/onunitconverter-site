About Mebibits per second

Mebibit per second (Mibit/s) = 2²⁰ = 1,048,576 bit/s (IEC 80000-13). Used in FPGA-to-memory interface specs and JEDEC memory bus timing documents where bit rates are derived from 2ⁿ clock frequencies. A 100 MHz 8-bit parallel bus = 800 Mibit/s. Contrasted with Mbit/s (= 10⁶ bit/s). 1 Mibit/s = 1,048,576 bit/s ≈ 131 KB/s.

About Exbibits per second

Exbibit per second (Eibit/s) = 2⁶⁰ bit/s ≈ 1.153 × 10¹⁸ bit/s (IEC 80000-13) - approximately 750× the current single-fibre world record (1.53 Pbit/s, NTT 2024). Visible light at full Shannon capacity (300 THz bandwidth, 10 dB SNR) ≈ 10¹⁵ bit/s per beam - still three orders below 1 Eibit/s. Theoretical only. 1 Eibit/s = 2⁶⁰ bit/s ≈ 128 PiB/s.
